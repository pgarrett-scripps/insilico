DESK DECISION: proceed

This manuscript reports a mechanistic study of TDP-43 nuclear mislocalization driven by acetylation at lysine 82, with evidence spanning cultured human neurons, biochemical binding assays, mass spectrometry, and postmortem ALS tissue. The claims are checkable from the manuscript and its cited materials, the methods are described in sufficient detail to be repeated, and the work falls squarely within the scope of an open overlay journal reviewing original research. The study is not a clinical trial, does not provide diagnostic or treatment guidance, and its central evidence is presented in the figures and text.

The manuscript is complete, intelligible, and internally consistent. The conclusions are scaled to the data, and the authors acknowledge limitations (e.g., variability in K82 acetylation across sALS samples). Whether the evidence fully supports the causal model is a question for specialist reviewers, not a threshold problem visible on its face. The work is substantive and of clear interest to the neurodegeneration and RNA-binding protein communities.

Proceed to full review.
