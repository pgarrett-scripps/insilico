# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript (Zhang, Vazquez-Sanchez, Lu, et al., Cleveland lab) reports that acetylation of lysine 82 within the bipartite cNLS of TDP-43 disrupts its binding to importin-α1, blocking nuclear import and initiating TDP-43 cytoplasmic accumulation, and detects K82 acetylation in sporadic ALS motor cortex. The audit checked the 39 in-text citations against the reference list and, where possible, against external databases. The reference list contains 39 entries (the converter's numbering is garbled in places — e.g., "1. 2. 3." and "1. 26. 27." — but all entries are present and map to the in-text citations 1–39, including ref 39 = He 2015 for RawConverter). All entries are full citations (author, journal, volume, pages, year) from established venues; no DOIs/PMIDs are given, which is normal for a preprint and does not render them unresolvable. Two references were verified to exist and match their claims (Sreedharan 2008, PMID 18309045; Rutherford 2008, PMID 18802454). The research tools returned poor results for most other queries, so several load-bearing quantitative claims could not be independently confirmed; these are marked unverifiable and raised as questions rather than asserted to be wrong. No retracted or predatory sources were identified, and no conspicuous self-citation inflation was found. No HARD (non-repeatable) citation-integrity defects were identified; the open items are verification questions for the authors, most notably the load-bearing claims in Findings 7 and 8 (refs 30 and 34).

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 1 (Neumann 2006) for 'TDP-43 proteinopathy … found in 97% of ALS cases.'** — The 97% figure is attributed to ref 1. This is the canonical source for TDP-43 pathology in ALS and the figure is widely associated with it, but I could not confirm the exact percentage from the source via the tools. Plausible; not confirmed.
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 19 (Doll 2022) for 'bipartite cNLS … recognized by the importin-α/importin-β1 pathway.'** — Plausible and directly on-topic (title matches the claim), but I could not retrieve the paper contents to confirm.
- **[Claim–citation support] Refs 24, 25 (Melamed 2019; Klim 2019) for stathmin-2 loss with reduced TDP-43 function** — Both are the established primary sources for STMN2/TDP-43 loss-of-function; the claim is plausible but not confirmed from the tools.
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 30 (Ko 2024) for 'consistent with a recent report' that K82Q disrupts importin-α1/β signaling** — This is a 2024 J Mol Biol paper cited to corroborate the central K82Q finding. I could not retrieve it to confirm it reports the same result. Because it is used to corroborate a central claim, the authors should confirm the citation is accurate.
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 34 (Kametani 2016) for 'found K82 acetylation in one out of two ALS postmortem samples.'** — This specific claim is used in the Discussion to support the consistency of the central K82-acetylation finding. I could not confirm from the source that Kametani 2016 reports K82 acetylation in one of two ALS samples. This should be verified by the authors.
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 9 (Cohen 2015) for 'antibodies recognizing acetylated lysine 145 … stained neuropathological inclusions.'** — Plausible (the paper is on an acetylation switch in TDP-43), but not confirmed from the tools.
- **[Claim–citation support] Ref 7 (Nelson 2019) for LATE prevalence and FTD comparison** — The LATE consensus report is the correct venue for these claims, but the specific figures could not be confirmed.
- **[Quotation/number fidelity] '97% of ALS cases' (ref 1)** — Exact figure not confirmed against the source.
- **[Quotation/number fidelity] '20–50% of people older than 80 years' and 'estimated to be 100-fold more frequent than FTD' (ref 7)** — Both specific figures attributed to Nelson 2019 could not be confirmed from the tools.
- **[Quotation/number fidelity] 'one out of two ALS postmortem samples' (ref 34)** — Specific count attributed to Kametani 2016 could not be confirmed.
