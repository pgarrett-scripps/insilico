# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript presents findings that acetylation of lysine 82 (K82) within the bipartite classical nuclear localization sequence (cNLS) of TDP-43 disrupts its binding to importin-α1, causing nuclear loss of function and cytoplasmic accumulation. The study reports that partial proteasome inhibition (mimicking age-related decline) induces acetylation/ubiquitination at K79, K82, K84 within the cNLS, and that acetylation-mimicking mutation K82Q abolishes nuclear import. Custom antibodies detect K82 acetylation in motor cortex lysates from 6/6 sporadic ALS patients but not 4 controls. The overall picture is of resolvable references with several specific attribution claims that require author confirmation.
