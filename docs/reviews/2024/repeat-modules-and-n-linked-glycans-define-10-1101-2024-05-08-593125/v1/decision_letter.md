# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
Eight specialist reports converge on a positive assessment of the manuscript's core empirical contribution: cryo-EM structures of EtpA in complex with mAbs 1C08 (3.34 A) and 1G05 (3.97 A) with reported FSC/MolProbity/EMRinger validation, a site-specific glycosylation map covering 133/166 Asn sites cross-validated against native H10407 protein, and a domain-resolved functional pattern in which CTR-binding mAbs (mouse 1G05, human 1F09) inhibit blood-group-A binding and adhesion while NTS-binding mAbs do not. Seven reviewers recommended minor revision at high confidence (weighted 3.73/5); one ethics reviewer dissented at 2/5, confidence 5.

I weighed the rebuttal carefully. Three of the authors' disagreements are credible and I do not require action on them beyond what the authors have already offered. (i) On the methodology reviewer's concern that the CTR/NTS functional split rests on a single mAb pair, the authors quote Results text showing the pattern replicates across two species and two immunization routes (three human NTS mAbs non-inhibitory, human CTR mAb 1F09 inhibitory); this is genuine independent convergence and the reviewer appears to have under-weighted the human panel. (ii) On the title's use of 'define,' the authors' reading is defensible; this is a stylistic preference, not an error. (iii) On the EMPEM 'neutralizing vs. decoy' framing, the authors correctly point out that the manuscript already hedges with 'suggesting... likely'; I ask only that the hedge be carried consistently into the Abstract, not that the framing be removed.

The authors conceded, fully and specifically, every load-bearing critique: the missing in-text IRB/consent disclosure for the primary ETEC H10407 challenge study that supplied the human plasmablasts, the absent EMDB/PDB accessions, the internally contradictory competing-interest statement (a blanket 'no financial competing interests' alongside disclosure of US 8323668 covering EtpA vaccine antigens), the unverified fold status of rEtpA1-1086, the unhedged immune-evasion narrative, the unquantified EMPEM epitope-calling criteria, and the possible pseudo-replication in Figure 1B. These concessions are constructive and the proposed remedies are the right ones.

I depart from a nominal 'minor' outcome, which the weighted score alone would support, for two reasons that the concessions themselves establish. First, the cryo-EM maps and models are the central evidence for essentially every structural claim in the paper, and at present they are undeposited placeholders ('EMDB-xxxx', 'PDB xxxx'); under this journal's standard, evidence a reader cannot reach is not yet evidence. Second, the human-subjects provenance for the protective 1F09 antibody currently requires the reader to chase an external reference (Guerrez et al. 2024) to establish that the primary challenge study had ethical oversight at all. Neither gap indicates misconduct or a scientific defect — the authors clearly have the protocol number and intend to deposit — but both require verification in the revised manuscript rather than assurance. The Methods audit additionally flags twelve reproduction-blocking gaps (flow gating hierarchy, MS repository accession, software versions, human-subject demographics, per-experiment n and error-bar definitions) that are mechanical to close. Reject was never warranted: no reviewer questioned the validity of the structural or glycosylation work, and the citation audit found zero hard gaps, no retractions, and appropriate self-citation. I expect this to become a solid contribution to ETEC adhesin biology once the disclosure, deposition and hedging items are closed.

## Required Revisions
1. Deposit the cryo-EM maps and refined atomic models for both the 1C08- and 1G05-bound complexes in EMDB and PDB, and replace the 'EMDB-xxxx'/'PDB xxxx' placeholders in Supplemental Table 3 and the Data Availability statement with actual accession numbers. If accessions are on hold, state this explicitly and supply reviewer-access codes so the maps can be inspected during re-review; a forward-looking promise ('will be submitted prior to publication') is not sufficient for a paper whose central evidence is the maps themselves.
2. Add to Methods (Plasmablast isolation section) an explicit, self-contained human-subjects statement for the primary ETEC H10407 challenge study: the Naval Medical Research Command IRB protocol number, the approval statement, and confirmation that written informed consent was obtained from all volunteers. Currently only the secondary-use WUSTL protocol (201110126) for archived de-identified specimens is given, so a reader cannot confirm oversight of the study that generated 1F09 without retrieving reference 88.
3. Revise the Conflicts of Interest section as conceded: remove the blanket 'no financial competing interests related to the work' and state plainly that US patent 8,323,668, held by J.M. Fleckenstein, covers use of EtpA-related antigens in vaccine development and is therefore directly related to the findings reported here.
4. Add biophysical fold verification for rEtpA1-1086 (SEC elution profile and/or DSF thermal-shift compared side-by-side with full-length rEtpA) as a new supplemental figure, and soften the Results text to state that efficient TPS-pathway secretion is consistent with, but not proof of, native folding. Absent this, loss of bgA binding cannot be attributed to the repeat modules rather than to global misfolding.
5. Revise the Discussion paragraph (~lines 390-401) so that immune evasion via glycan heterogeneity and affinity maturation toward stable, high-occupancy glycosylated epitopes are presented as two co-equal, currently untested hypotheses, matching the hedged language already used in Results. Remove assertions that evasion is 'exploited as a mechanism.'
6. Add a Methods paragraph stating the quantitative criteria used to call EMPEM epitopes distinct (minimum angular/spatial separation of Fab density relative to cryo-EM-defined domain boundaries; minimum particle count per 2D class), and change 'confidently identify' to 'estimate' for the 5-6 NTS / 3-4 CTR counts throughout Results and the Figure 5 legend.
7. For all functional-assay figures, state biological replicates (independent experiments) and technical replicates (fields/wells) separately in each legend, define error bars explicitly (SD, SEM or CI) for every panel including Figures 1 and 3 and the supplemental figures, and re-run statistics treating biological replicate as the unit of analysis where fields were previously pooled (Fig. 1B, 'n=20 replicate fields from two independent experiments'). Report both the original and re-analysed results if conclusions change.
8. Report the measured KD values for 1C08 and 1G05 in the main Results text and Figure 1 legend, state n and replication for the biolayer interferometry runs, and state how antibody concentrations in the inhibition assays were chosen relative to those affinities.
9. Deposit the mass-spectrometry raw files and search results in PRIDE or MassIVE and cite the accession; state the number of MS replicates summed by GlycoMSQuant; and give versions for ProLuCID, Census2, ColabFold/AlphaFold2, ROSETTA, Relion and the MS acquisition software (CryoSPARC v2.95, Chimera/ChimeraX versions are already given).
10. Provide the full flow-cytometry gating hierarchy for both the mouse plasmablast sort and the human rEtpA-binding plasmablast sort, as a supplemental figure with representative plots, rather than only the terminal marker phenotype.
11. Add the human-subject cohort description currently absent: number of volunteers from whom PBMCs were obtained, and available demographics (age range, sex, health status, blood group where known), plus inclusion/exclusion criteria or a pointer to where these are specified in the challenge-study protocol.
12. Soften the Abstract and Results claim that mAbs 'directly engage' glycan-containing epitopes to the wording the authors propose: epitopes encompass N-linked glycosylation sites, with monohexose density directly visualised and glycan contribution to affinity established by N-to-A mutagenesis. Dihexose density is not resolved and should not be implied.
13. Replace 'low-fidelity' with 'promiscuous' or 'relaxed sequence specificity' when describing EtpC, and add the clarifying sentence in Results acknowledging the canonical N-X-S/T occupancy bias shown in Supplemental Figure 5, so the terminology no longer contradicts the manuscript's own occupancy data.
14. Carry the existing Results hedge on EMPEM epitopes into the Abstract: the Abstract currently states that vaccination 'generates responses against neutralizing and decoy regions,' which should be marked as an inference by analogy to the monoclonal data, since the polyclonal sera were not functionally assayed.

## Minor Suggestions
- State HT-29 provenance more fully: add RRID:CVCL_0320, and note whether the line was authenticated (STR) and mycoplasma-tested, or state plainly that it was not.
- Add confocal acquisition parameters (objective, NA, detector, laser power, pinhole, gain) and the image-analysis software used for the adhesion imaging in Figure 1.
- Give mouse age at immunization and the number of CD-1 mice contributing to the pooled polyclonal sera used for EMPEM.
- Note whether AlphaFold2/ColabFold and ROSETTA runs used fixed seeds or seed averaging, and record the hardware; this is not load-bearing but helps anyone attempting to regenerate the starting models.
- The citation audit flagged ten references it could not resolve by automated search (refs 3, 9, 17, 19, 23, 24, 41, 46, 65, 95). None appeared misattributed and several are well-known (Kotloff GEMS, Punjani cryoSPARC), but a pass to confirm DOIs/PMIDs for these entries would remove any doubt.
- Consider stating the IgG subclass of the mouse mAbs 1G05 and 1C08 alongside the human IgG1 expression construct already described.
- The CaBLAM outlier rate of 15.05% for the 1G05 model is high relative to the 1C08 model (2.8%); a sentence acknowledging this and the lower 3.97 A resolution would preempt reader concern about backbone geometry in that map.
