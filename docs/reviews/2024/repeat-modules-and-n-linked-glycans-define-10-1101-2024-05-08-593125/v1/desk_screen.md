# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This is a substantial structural and immunological study of EtpA, an enterotoxigenic E. coli adhesin, combining cryo-EM, mass spectrometry, and immunological approaches. The manuscript presents original research with clear experimental design, comprehensive methods, and detailed results. While the work is primarily empirical and structural rather than computational, it falls within In Silico's scope as original research whose claims can be evaluated from the presented evidence and deposited materials (cryo-EM maps and models to be submitted to EMDB/PDB). The work is complete, clearly reported with appropriate controls, and addresses a significant public health problem. No threshold problems are evident that would warrant desk rejection. The manuscript should proceed to full review.
