# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript contains 124 numbered references spanning structural biology, microbiology, immunology, and epidemiology. The audit examined resolvability and claim–citation support across representative load-bearing citations. Overall finding: HIGH INTEGRITY with minor unverifiable elements. All sampled primary citations are resolvable via PubMed/DOI or internally supported by the paper's own experimental data. No retracted sources or predatory venues detected. Six citations could not be verified via PubMed search (Refs 9, 19, 24, 41, 46, 95), but authors do not claim these as unpublished and they are primarily supporting rather than load-bearing to the paper's central structural and immunological findings. Central mechanistic claims are supported by the paper's own cryo-EM, mass spectrometry, and ELISA data. Self-citation is substantial but appropriate and not inflated.
