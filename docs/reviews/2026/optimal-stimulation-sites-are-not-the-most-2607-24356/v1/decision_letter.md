# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel's confidence-weighted score (3.91/5) and verdict distribution (1 accept, 5 minor, 2 major) sit close to the minor/major boundary. I am upholding the meta-reviewer's draft recommendation of major revision, but for a narrower and more specific reason than the raw distribution might suggest, and I want to be clear that this is a positive assessment of the underlying work.

The majority 'minor' votes are concentrated in dimensions where the required fixes are additive and do not touch the conclusions: missing CIs, uncited concurrent work, software versions, definitional ambiguities. The two 'major' votes (methodology and rigor, both at confidence 4) converge independently on the same load-bearing sentence — the abstract's claim of complete, individualised reclassification from a single site — from two angles (intervention-selection leakage; causal/translational overreach), with novelty's circularity point corroborating from a third. When mid-confidence dissent converges on the headline claim rather than on peripheral matters, that outweighs a broad but diffuse majority.

The rebuttal is unusually strong and materially reduced the scope of what remains. Critically, the authors concede the intervention-selection leakage point outright and propose the correct remedy: promote the population-average LDA-resonant site result (~78% net cure, chosen entirely from training patients) to the primary leakage-free headline, and relabel the per-patient argmax figure as an explicit upper bound everywhere including the abstract. That is exactly the fix the panel asked for, and it does not require new modelling. They likewise concede the causal-language rescoping, the Fig. 6 chance-overlap permutation test, and (going beyond what was strictly required) a reservoir-hyperparameter sensitivity analysis.

I weighed the three disagreements and find two of them credible. On transductive embedding, the manuscript quote ('The SVD embeddings were built transductively (across all patients) while the classifiers were trained and tested on disjoint folds') supports the authors' position that basis construction is distinct from, and less severe than, the conceded site-search leakage; this is a defensible and common practice provided it is foregrounded alongside the LOPO confirmation, and I do not require a change beyond clearer exposition. On novelty, the authors correctly identify their claimed contribution as the identifiable per-subject read-out used as a perturbable counterfactual testbed, not the reservoir formalism; Fig. 2F–H supports the identifiability premise. On rigor's model-geometry-vs-disease point, the authors' argument that \u0394W is a data-driven quantity has force, but the dissociation between focal and distributed correction is still mediated by a fixed random J, so the promised hyperparameter sensitivity check moves from 'strongly desirable' to required for me — it is the one place where the authors' own defence of the central finding depends on evidence not yet in the paper.

The reason for major rather than minor is therefore not that the evidence base is weak — it is sound, the negative result is robust and honestly reported, and the closed-loop controller is a genuine methodological contribution — but that the flagship number in the abstract must be replaced with a different, leakage-free number, and one new analysis (hyperparameter sensitivity) is needed to support a claim the authors defend in rebuttal. Those are substantive changes to the headline result, and they warrant a second look rather than an editorial check.

The compliance audits found no blocking citation problems but three HARD methods gaps (random seed, library versions, participant demographics), which I fold in below. Two citations ([6] Fox 2014, [30] Vogel 2020) and a possible year error in [22] Koch require author confirmation.

## Required Revisions
1. Replace the abstract's flagship personalisation claim with the leakage-free number. Promote the population-average LDA-resonant site result (~78% net cure, site chosen entirely from training patients with no per-test-patient search) to the primary headline in title framing, abstract and Results. Report the per-patient 121-way argmax result alongside it and label it explicitly as an upper bound on achievable personalisation at every occurrence, including the abstract. This is the change the panel's two dissenting reviewers converged on and it is the gating item for this decision.
2. Add the chance-overlap permutation test for Fig. 6 (10,000 draws of random subsets of sizes 35 and 33 from the 121 parcels), report the empirical p-value in the Methods and figure caption, and revise the Fig. 6 title and the corresponding Discussion sentence to match the test outcome. If the observed overlap is not significantly below chance, the disjointness claim must be softened to descriptive language.
3. Add the reservoir-hyperparameter sensitivity analysis promised in the rebuttal (N \u2208 {1000, 2000, 4000}; spectral radius \u2208 {0.85, 0.95, 0.99}), re-running the \u0394W-based pathology-site ranking and LDA-resonant site selection, and report whether (a) the cortical/subcortical anatomical dissociation and (b) the focal-fails / distributed-succeeds asymmetry are preserved. State plainly in the Results or Discussion whether the dissociation survives; if it does not survive across configurations, scope the claim accordingly. This item is required because the authors' rebuttal to the 'model geometry vs. disease property' critique rests on it.
4. Rescope causal and translational language throughout title, abstract and the opening of Results, replacing unqualified 'stimulation', 'cure' and 'prescribes how to stimulate' with 'in-silico perturbation' / 'model-prescribed correction' as proposed, and move the existing Discussion caveat (that the reservoir is fit to infraslow BOLD and an intervention here is an abstract input to regional FC dynamics) forward to immediately precede Fig. 4. Also state explicitly that the resonant frequency f_eig derives from the spectrum of J rather than from patient data.
5. Close the three HARD methods gaps: (a) state the random seed(s) or seed-averaging procedure used to generate J and J_in, and confirm reproducibility of the fixed reservoir; (b) give exact versions for Python, NumPy, scikit-learn, nilearn and Matplotlib, and deposit an environment file or requirements.txt in the GitHub repository; (c) add a participant demographics table (age, sex, education, MMSE or equivalent, and disease-stage information) for both the 40/40 analysis cohorts and the 145/41 full pool, together with the diagnostic and exclusion criteria used to define CC and AD groups (citing or summarising the ADNI protocol).
6. Add bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (patient-level resampling, 2000 iterations) for AUROC and balanced accuracy in Fig. 3A\u2013D and for the tangent-space SVM benchmark, reported in figure legends and a supplementary table. Also report fit quality (residuals or CIs) for the learning-curve extrapolation in Fig. S1, or explicitly label the projected N=100\u2013200 accuracies as an unvalidated extrapolation.
7. Define 'physiological amplitude' numerically (the proposed RMS_pert/RMS_base \u2264 2 threshold matching Fig. S10A) in Methods, and state the threshold explicitly at each point in Results where 'physiological' or 'supra-physiological' amplitude is invoked, so that the claim that focal targeting fails 'at any amplitude' is checkable.
8. Add the missing citations: Luppi et al. (2026, eNeuro) and Bonn\u00ec et al. (2026, Alzheimer's Research & Therapy) in the Discussion, with an explicit contrast of scope and method against the present work; and a standard Riemannian/tangent-space FC classification reference (e.g. Barachant et al.) in the Methods description of the tangent-space SVM benchmark.
9. Resolve the three citation queries raised by the audit: confirm or correct Ref [6] (Fox et al. 2014, PNAS 111:E4367\u2013E4375, which could not be located and is load-bearing for the focal-target premise); confirm the year and venue of Ref [22] (Koch et al., precuneus rTMS \u2014 the audit located a 2025 Alzheimer's Research & Therapy paper rather than a 2022 Brain paper); and confirm Ref [30] (Vogel et al. 2020, Nat Commun 11:2612). Provide DOIs for all three.
10. Add the clarity fixes conceded in the rebuttal: (a) a paragraph at the start of the 'In-silico stimulation' Results subsection contrasting read-out interpolation (\u03b1) with input oscillation amplitude (A) before Fig. 4; (b) 2\u20133 sentences in Results defining transductive embedding versus leave-one-patient-out reconstruction in plain language, stating the 17.5%\u219242.5% baseline misclassification shift and its cause at first mention. On the transductive-embedding question specifically, I accept the authors' rebuttal that basis construction across all patients with fold-disjoint classifier training is defensible; no methodological change is required, but the exposition must make the distinction from site-search leakage explicit.

## Minor Suggestions
- Specify a numerical value for the membrane time constant \u03c4 rather than describing it qualitatively as 'effectively instantaneous', and briefly justify the feedback gain f=0.1.
- Detail the exact construction of the train-only tangent reference mean in the nested CV (inner vs. outer loop), so the SVM benchmark can be reproduced precisely.
- State the ADNI data freeze / release version and download date so readers can assemble the identical cohort.
- Add a specific commit hash or release tag to the code-availability statement, and confirm in the repository README that the leave-one-AD-patient-out leakage-free analysis is included.
- Specify the reproducibility details conceded in the rebuttal: that the closed-loop reservoir state is inherited from the teacher-forced warmup rather than reset to zero, and that the sinusoid is added to rather than substituted for u(t)=f\u00b7\u015d(t).
- Briefly justify N_PC=50 specifically (Fig. S2 shows a plateau at K_PC\u2265100), or note that results are insensitive across that range.
- Report hardware and approximate compute time for the main analyses.
- Consider a brief note on why 40 CC / 40 AD were chosen for the main analyses given the larger available pool (145/41); a sentence of rationale would pre-empt the reviewers' sample-size question even absent a formal power calculation.
- Consider adding a conflict-of-interest statement (even a nil declaration) alongside the existing funding disclosure.
