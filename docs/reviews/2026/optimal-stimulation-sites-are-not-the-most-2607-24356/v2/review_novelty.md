## Questions
- Why were main classification results (Figs. 3A–D) not recomputed under LOPO to match the therapy-target analysis? The transductive embedding in diagnostic accuracy claims creates a methodological asymmetry.
- Is per-patient site selection (33 distinct sites across 40 patients) stable under perturbations (resampling training patients, noise injection, regularization variation)? Does heterogeneity reflect biological variability or model noise?
- Can you formalize the mechanistic account by computing network centrality (betweenness, closeness, eigenvector) or coupling measures for LDA-resonant sites and comparing to ΔW pathology sites?
- The claim that 'whether its FC signature reduces to a few focal sites has not been tested causally' is strong. Have you systematically reviewed prior causal/interventional work on distributed vs. focal AD signatures (e.g., network diffusion models of tau spread)?
- Is the resonant frequency f_eig ≈ 0.077 cycles/step (0.026 Hz) specific to AD patients or identical in controls? If identical, does this suggest frequency is a property of the fixed reservoir J rather than disease-specific?
