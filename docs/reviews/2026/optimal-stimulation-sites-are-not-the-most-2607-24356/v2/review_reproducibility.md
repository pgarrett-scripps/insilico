# Reproducibility Reviewer — Revision Review

**Score:** 4/5 → 4/5 (confidence 4/5)

## Summary
The revision has made minor improvements to clarity in the LOPO procedure (reproducibility-5 now partially addresses the ranking criterion) and added references to support the network-level intervention framework. However, the core reproducibility issues remain unresolved. The controlled-access nature of ADNI data (reproducibility-1) is unchanged and remains a hard barrier. The transductive embedding procedure (reproducibility-2), reservoir state initialization (reproducibility-3), G-space SVD computation (reproducibility-4), oscillatory stimulation injection method (reproducibility-7), feedback gain justification (reproducibility-8), and test–retest ceiling definition (reproducibility-9) are all still ambiguous or unspecified. Software versions (reproducibility-6) are absent. These gaps prevent independent replication of the classifiers and stimulation analyses without author contact. The code repository is stated to be available but lacks versioning (commit/tag/DOI), and no mapping of scripts to figures is provided. The manuscript's scientific claims are sound and the methods are well-motivated, but the procedural specification falls short of what is needed for reproducibility in a computational neuroscience context.

## Score rationale
The score remains 4 (minor revision) because the core scientific contribution—the dissociation between pathology sites and therapeutic targets—is well-supported by the in-silico analysis, and the main methodological choices (reservoir computing, LDA-resonant site selection, closed-loop control) are justified. However, the reproducibility issues are not minor: they are load-bearing for the classification and stimulation results. The authors have not addressed any of the nine reproducibility points substantively. A replicator cannot obtain the raw ADNI data without institutional approval, cannot determine the exact fold-wise operation of the transductive embedding, cannot initialize the closed-loop reconstruction, and cannot specify the oscillatory stimulation injection method. These are not formatting issues; they are procedural gaps that block independent verification. The revision should have included: (1) a clarification of the transductive embedding (per-fold or once-only), (2) explicit initialization of x(t) in closed-loop mode, (3) specification of whether X is per-session or pooled for G-space, (4) the exact ranking criterion for the transductive population-average site, (5) software versions and hyperparameters, (6) the oscillation injection method, (7) justification or sensitivity analysis for f=0.1, (8) definition of the test–retest ceiling, and (9) a versioned code snapshot (commit/tag/DOI). None of these have been addressed. The score would rise to 5 if these were resolved, because the science is sound; it would drop to 3 if the authors had introduced new ambiguities or if the data/code were locked behind 'upon publication' promises. As it stands, the manuscript is publishable as a preprint with a clear reproducibility caveat, but the reproducibility gaps prevent a higher score.

## Points from the previous round
- **[reproducibility-1] outstanding** — The manuscript still states 'ADNI data are available to qualified researchers upon registration and acceptance of the ADNI Data Use Agreement' (Methods, Data and code availability). This is a controlled-access gate that has not changed. The authors have not deposited the processed data or provided an alternative path to access. This remains a hard barrier for independent replication without ADNI institutional approval.
- **[reproducibility-2] outstanding** — The Methods section (Classification) still reads: 'The SVD embeddings were built transductively (across all patients) while the classifiers were trained and tested on disjoint folds.' The manuscript does not clarify whether the Gram-matrix SVD for FC-lag features is computed once on all patients before any fold split, or recomputed within each training fold. The LOPO procedure (Methods, Leakage-free target selection) is described separately but the default transductive procedure's exact fold-wise operation remains ambiguous. This is critical for assessing leakage.
- **[reproducibility-3] outstanding** — Methods, Closed-loop reconstruction states: 'the reservoir was reset and run closed-loop: the real target drove the network for the first 5 steps, after which it free-ran for the remainder of the session.' The initialization of the reservoir state x(t) is not specified. It is unclear whether x(0) is zero, random, or inherited from a teacher-forced warmup. This affects reproducibility of Fig. 2I (lagged-FC validation).
- **[reproducibility-4] outstanding** — Methods, Read-out G-space states: 'with V_k the top K_PC=200 right singular vectors of X, the projected read-out is fW=W^T V_k V_k^T'. The manuscript does not state whether X is computed per-session or pooled across all sessions, and whether the SVD is recomputed per fold or shared across folds. Fig. S2 shows training/test error but does not validate this choice against the classification results in Fig. 3.
- **[reproducibility-5] partial** — Methods, Leakage-free target selection now states: 'the personalised site chosen as the argmax of the train-only score reduction over the 121 sites, and the population-average site as the argmax of the mean reduction across the training AD patients'. This clarifies the LOPO case. However, the transductive case (Fig. 5A, Fig. S3) is still not explicitly stated. The manuscript does not say whether the transductive population-average site is computed as argmax of mean score reduction across all AD patients, or by some other criterion. This is a partial clarification.
- **[reproducibility-6] outstanding** — Methods, Statistics and software still lists 'Python (NumPy, scikit-learn, nilearn, Matplotlib)' without versions. No Python version, scikit-learn version, or hyperparameter defaults (e.g., LDA solver, RF tree depth) are provided. This is a SOFT issue but limits exact reproducibility of the classifiers.
- **[reproducibility-7] outstanding** — Methods, Oscillatory stimulation states: 'a sinusoid A sin(2πft) is injected as additional input at the top-k sites during the teacher-forced pass, with W unchanged'. It is still unclear whether the oscillation is added to the teacher-forced input u(t)=f·ŝ(t) or injected as a separate input channel. It is also not stated whether the reservoir state is reset between frequency/amplitude sweeps. This affects reproducibility of Figs. S7–S8.
- **[reproducibility-8] outstanding** — Methods, Reservoir model and Read-out fitting state that the feedback gain is f=0.1, but this choice is not justified or swept. It is unclear whether this parameter affects the relative amplitude comparisons (RMS_pert/RMS_base in Fig. S10) or the resonance frequency. No sensitivity analysis is provided.
- **[reproducibility-9] outstanding** — Methods, Closed-loop reconstruction and Fig. 2I caption do not define the even/odd test–retest ceiling (grey line in Fig. 2I). It is unclear whether this is computed from the empirical data (splitting sessions into even/odd volumes) or from the model, and over which sessions/patients the median is taken.

## Strengths
- The core finding—that optimal stimulation sites are not the most pathologically altered sites—is novel and well-supported by the in-silico analysis.
- The use of subject-specific, identifiable reservoir models to generate counterfactual predictions is methodologically sound and goes beyond descriptive group comparisons.
- The dissociation between ΔW (pathology) and LDA-resonant (therapy) sites is large and consistent across classifiers and protocols, suggesting a robust finding.
- The closed-loop control analysis (Fig. 5D–F) demonstrates a practical path toward adaptive neuromodulation with lower dose, adding translational value.
- The leave-one-patient-out cross-validation (Fig. S9) appropriately addresses classifier leakage concerns and shows the therapeutic effect survives honest CV.
- The manuscript is well-written, figures are clear, and the progression from reading out disease to intervening on it is logical and compelling.

## Questions
-  Can you provide the exact procedure for the transductive SVD embedding in the classification pipeline? Specifically, is the Gram-matrix SVD computed once on all patients before any fold split, and then the resulting coordinates projected per fold, or is it recomputed within each training fold? This is critical for assessing whether test-set information leaks into the embedding.
- How is the reservoir state x(t) initialized at the start of the closed-loop reconstruction? Is it zero, or does it inherit state from a teacher-forced warmup? This affects reproducibility of Fig. 2I.
- In the read-out G-space projection, is the SVD of X computed per-session or pooled across all sessions? Is it recomputed per cross-validation fold, or computed once and shared?
- For the transductive (non-LOPO) case, what is the exact ranking criterion for the population-average LDA-resonant site (Fig. 5A, Fig. S3A)? Is it argmax of mean FC-lag score reduction across all AD patients?
- Can you provide the versions of Python, NumPy, scikit-learn, and other key libraries used, along with scikit-learn hyperparameter defaults (LDA solver, RF tree depth, etc.)?
- In the oscillatory stimulation, is the sinusoid A sin(2πft) added to the teacher-forced input u(t)=f·ŝ(t), or injected as a separate input channel? Is the reservoir state reset between frequency/amplitude sweeps?
- The feedback gain f=0.1 is used for teacher-forcing but not justified. Was this parameter swept, and does it affect the relative amplitude comparisons (RMS_pert/RMS_base) or the resonance frequency?
- How is the even/odd test–retest ceiling (Fig. 2I, grey line) computed? Is it from the empirical data (splitting into even/odd volumes) or from the model, and over which sessions/patients?
- Can you provide a versioned snapshot of the code (commit hash, tag, or DOI) rather than a mutable repository link? Can you also provide a mapping of scripts to figures/tables for clarity?
