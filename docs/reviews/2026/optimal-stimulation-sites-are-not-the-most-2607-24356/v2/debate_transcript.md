## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate treats the remaining objections as "statistical polish," but the rigor reviewer's sharpest point is not polish: Fig. 6's headline claim that pathology and therapy sites are 'largely disjoint' (11/35 vs 11/33 shared) is statistically indistinguishable from chance. With 121 sites, a random partition into sets of 35 and 33 would overlap by ~9.5 sites (35×33/121); the observed 11 is barely above that null, yet no permutation test is offered (rigor-8) and the figure caption states the dissociation as fact. This is a central figure, not a footnote — it is what motivates the entire 'pathology ≠ therapy target' narrative in the abstract and Discussion.

Second, and compounding this, the primary reclassification numbers touted in the abstract ('complete, individualised reclassification from one site') come from the transductive, supra-physiological open-loop analysis (Fig. 5A), while the only leakage-free estimate — Fig. S9's population-average LOPO target — reaches just ~78% net cure, a number that never appears in the abstract or Results narrative and is only reachable by digging into Methods/Supplement (methodology-1, rigor-11).

I concede LOPO genuinely resolves circularity for the personalized-site *existence* claim (novelty-1, resolved) — that is real progress, not a fatal flaw. But the paper still headlines an inflated, leakage-exposed number as its primary result while the honest, generalizable estimate is materially weaker and the flagship anatomical dissociation lacks any significance test. That is a rigor failure in what the paper claims to have shown, not merely how it's phrased — FIXABLE, but requiring the authors to re-anchor Results/Abstract to the honest numbers and add the missing permutation test, not just reword captions.

**Key points:**
- Fig. 6's 'largely disjoint' pathology/therapy site claim (11/35 vs 11/33 overlap) is statistically close to the chance expectation (~9.5) and untested — a headline claim without a supporting statistical test
- Abstract's 'complete reclassification' figure derives from the leaky transductive analysis; the only leakage-free estimate (Fig. S9, ~78% net cure for population-average target) is buried in Methods/Supplement, not integrated into the main narrative
- LOPO cross-validation (Fig. S9) is a genuine, conceded improvement for circularity but does not extend to validating the anatomical dissociation claim in Fig. 6
- This is FIXABLE via re-anchoring Results/Abstract to honest numbers and adding a permutation/hypergeometric test, but as submitted the paper's two flagship claims outrun what its own honest validation supports
