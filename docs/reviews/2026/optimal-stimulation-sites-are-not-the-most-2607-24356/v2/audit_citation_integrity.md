# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This audit examined 15 key citations across foundational claims, methodological references, and disease-specific assertions in the manuscript on personalized resting-state fMRI models in Alzheimer's disease. Categories detected and checked include reference resolvability, claim–citation support, quotation/number fidelity, and retracted/predatory sources. All checked references are resolvable and plausibly support their attributed claims. No retracted sources, predatory venues, or unresolvable dead links were identified in load-bearing positions. Two soft items (Martorell et al. 2019 Cell and Jaeger 2001 GMD Report) are unverifiable but not load-bearing for central claims.
