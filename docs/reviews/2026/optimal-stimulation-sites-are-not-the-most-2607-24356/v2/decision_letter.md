# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
This decision rests on what the compliance audit could locate in the revised manuscript, not on the response document. The audit checked all ten of round 1's required revisions against the new draft and found zero fully addressed, with five of the five blocking items still open. The panel score is unchanged (3.91 -> 3.91) and no individual reviewer moved. The author submission is a list of proposed changes — 'Add a hypergeometric test', 'Rewrite the abstract to lead with LOPO', 'Deposit a frozen snapshot', 'Add a numerical threshold' — which read as an accurate and largely well-judged plan, but a plan is not a revision. On the two items that separate 'major' from 'minor' here, the manuscript has moved in the wrong direction: the abstract was substantially rewritten (an undisclosed change) so that the leakage-exposed per-patient argmax result 'complete, individualised reclassification' is now the headline, while the only leakage-free estimate (~78% net cure, population-average LOPO, Fig. S9B) still appears nowhere in the abstract or Results narrative. R1-01 asked for the opposite. Fig. 6C's 'largely disjoint' claim — the title's premise — still carries no test against the ~9.5-site chance expectation.

I want to be explicit about what is *not* driving this verdict. The authors' three disagreements are substantially reasonable and I accept two of them outright: the ADNI controlled-access pathway is lawful data governance and should not be scored as a reporting defect, and the justification for a generative model on perturbability rather than diagnostic gain is sound and already in the text. On the two unverifiable 2026 citations, the authors are right that they cannot be asked to cite works whose existence a confidence-3 reviewer could not confirm; R1-08 is narrowed accordingly to the tangent-space/Riemannian methods reference only. Their argument that a shared fixed reservoir cannot by itself explain a divergence between two site-selection criteria is also a fair partial rebuttal to R1-03, and a plainly stated limitation would close that item without new experiments. Reviewer drift was flagged (8 issues equally visible last round); those have not been added as new asks. I am adding no new requirements of my own beyond the random-seed gap, which is a HARD reproduction blocker identified by the methods audit and falls inside the existing R1-05.

The substance of the paper is not in question — the LOPO validation, S10 diagnostics, candid reporting of 0.66–0.70 AUROC against a 0.84 structural benchmark, and the closed-loop dose-reduction result all stand, and ethics is clean at 5/5. Every remaining ask is bounded, requires no new experiments, and is one the authors have already agreed to and specified in detail. That is why this is 'major' rather than 'reject': the route to acceptance is short and the authors have described it themselves. It is not 'minor' because a title-level dissociation with no significance test and an abstract anchored to the leaky arm are precisely the failure mode this venue's first criterion exists to catch.

One procedural point, stated plainly because this record is public: this is round 2 of at most 3. One revision round remains. If items R1-01, R1-02 and R1-04 are not verifiably present in the next draft's abstract, Fig. 6 caption and Results text, the manuscript will be decided on what is in front of me at that point, and the flagship claims as currently written will not survive that decision.

## Required Revisions
1. [R1-02] Still open and blocking. Run a hypergeometric or permutation test on the Fig. 6C overlap (121 sites; sets of 35 and 33; observed 11 shared against ~9.5 expected), report the exact p-value in the figure caption, the Results text and Methods, and revise the wording to whatever the test supports. If the overlap is not significantly below chance, 'largely disjoint' must be removed from the caption, the Results and the title framing. This is the single most important outstanding item because it is the premise of the title.
2. [R1-01] Still open and blocking, and the revision moved against it. Re-anchor the abstract and the corresponding Results paragraph to the leakage-free estimate: report the ~78% population-average LOPO net cure as the primary generalizable result, and label the ~100% per-patient 121-way argmax figure explicitly as an upper bound obtained under per-patient site selection on the fitted cohort. Move Fig. S9 into the main Results flow. Also report the 17.5%->42.5% baseline misclassification shift in Results, not only in Methods and the Supplement. The current abstract rewrite does the reverse of this and must be revised again.
3. [R1-04] Still open and blocking. Qualify the causal/biological language at the point of first claim, not only in the Discussion on p.18. Specifically: add an explicit 'within the fitted model' clause to the abstract sentences asserting that the required correction is 'intrinsically distributed' and that optimal targets are 'cortical and heterogeneous'; state immediately before Fig. 4 that the reservoir is fit to infraslow BOLD connectivity rather than electrophysiology, so an 'intervention' here is an abstract input to regional FC dynamics; and state that f_eig derives from the spectrum of J, not from patient data.
4. [R1-05] Still open and blocking. Provide (a) the random seed(s) used to generate J and J_in, or a statement that results are averaged over seeds — the methods audit records this as a hard reproduction blocker, since the paper specifies one fixed reservoir but not how to regenerate it; (b) pinned versions for Python, NumPy, scikit-learn, nilearn and Matplotlib, ideally as a deposited environment file; and (c) a participant demographics table (age, sex, MMSE or equivalent, disease stage) for the 40/40 and 145/41 cohorts, plus the QC/exclusion criteria actually applied. Table S1 currently gives session and subject counts only.
5. [R1-03] Still open and blocking, but narrowed. Either add the hyperparameter sensitivity analysis (re-running the DeltaW pathology ranking and LDA-resonant site selection across a small grid of N and spectral radius, reporting whether the cortical/subcortical dissociation survives), OR state plainly in the Limitations that robustness to reservoir architecture is untested and include the authors' own argument — that a single shared reservoir is used across subjects and both selection criteria, so architecture dependence cannot by itself produce the divergence — as an explicitly labelled argument rather than a demonstration. Either route closes this item; the current draft does neither.
6. [R1-10] Still open. In the 'In-silico stimulation' Results subsection, add the short paragraph distinguishing read-out interpolation (alpha) from input oscillation amplitude (A), and 2–3 plain-language sentences at first mention distinguishing the transductive SVD embedding from leave-one-patient-out reconstruction, making explicit which forms of leakage each analysis is and is not exposed to.
7. [R1-06] Still open. Report bootstrapped 95% CIs alongside mean+/-SD for the AUROC and balanced-accuracy metrics in Fig. 3, state in Methods that the +/-0.12 on the tangent-space SVM is the SD of the 25 fold-level AUROCs from the nested 5x5 CV, and add the supplementary table of fold values. For Fig. S1, state explicitly that the N=100–200 projections are unvalidated extrapolations.
8. [R1-07] Still open. Add the numerical definition of 'physiological amplitude' to Methods (the proposed RMS_pert/RMS_base <= 2 is adequate) and reference that threshold at each use of 'physiological' or 'supra-physiological' in Results, so that the claim that focal targeting fails 'at any amplitude' becomes checkable.
9. [R1-09 / methodology-5] Still open. Either re-run the tangent-space SVM benchmark on the matched 40 CC + 40 AD cohort with the same fold structure as the main classifiers and report that value alongside the larger-cohort number, or withdraw the 'lower end of the benchmark' comparison. As written, the preprocessing (GSR, 557+145 sessions) and CV differ from the main analysis and the comparison is not interpretable as stated.
10. [R1-08] Narrowed and still open. Cite a standard Riemannian/tangent-space FC methods reference (e.g. Barachant et al.) in the Methods description of the tangent-space SVM benchmark. The request to cite Luppi et al. (2026) and Bonni et al. (2026) is withdrawn: the reviewer could not verify those works exist, and the authors are right to decline to cite unverified references.

## Minor Suggestions
- Deposit a frozen, versioned snapshot of the analysis repository (Zenodo/OSF DOI) and add a README table mapping each script to the figures and tables it produces. The authors have agreed to this; doing it in the next round rather than 'at time of publication' would let a reviewer verify it.
- Fix the abstract's characterisation of 97.5% vs 100% as 'comparable' — the authors' own proposed phrasing ('reaches 97.5% reclassification at lower dose, nearly matching the 100% open-loop rate') is fine and matches the main text on p.12.
- Add the AI-use disclosure statement the authors offered, to Acknowledgments or Methods.
- Provide DOIs for Fox et al. (2014), Koch et al. (precuneus rTMS) and Vogel et al. (2020), and confirm the Koch venue/year — the citation audit found a 2025 Alzheimer's Research & Therapy paper that may be the intended reference. Non-blocking, but cheap to resolve.
- The citation audit flagged Jaeger (2001, GMD Report 148) and Martorell et al. (2019, Cell) as unverifiable through its tools. Neither is load-bearing; a DOI or handle for each would close the record.
- Consider stating explicitly, where the AUROC comparison is made, that the model's justification is perturbability rather than diagnostic gain. The Discussion already makes this argument well and it answers a reviewer objection directly; surfacing it earlier would prevent the same reading recurring.
- Note in Methods whether any multiple-comparison correction was applied across the Mann–Whitney comparisons reported in Figs. 1C and 4A, or state that none was and that the p-values are descriptive.
