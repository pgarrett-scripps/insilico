## Rationale
The manuscript presents a computational modeling study using reservoir computing to analyze resting-state fMRI in Alzheimer's disease and explore in-silico stimulation strategies. It makes original, testable claims about the dissociation between pathological sites and therapeutic targets in network-level interventions. The work appears to be within scope for In Silico: it is a research manuscript with checkable claims, includes methodological details, and presents computational results that can be evaluated from the manuscript and supplementary materials. While there are limitations acknowledged by the authors, these do not constitute fundamental flaws that would warrant desk rejection. The manuscript should proceed to full review.
