# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This is a computational modeling study using resting-state fMRI data from the ADNI database to fit subject-specific reservoir-computing models of Alzheimer's disease. The manuscript is primarily methodological and computational, with no antibodies, cell lines, organisms, human subjects recruitment, chemicals, oligos, genomics, mass spec, or microscopy as primary experimental techniques. The key categories triggered are: Computational/ML/modeling (reservoir fitting, classification, in-silico stimulation), Human subjects (secondary analysis of ADNI data), and Protocol provenance (several methods delegated to citations). The authors provide reasonable detail on computational methods, hyperparameters, and cross-validation schemes. Data and code availability are stated. However, several protocol-provenance items require verification: the fMRI preprocessing pipeline is delegated to ADNI's minimal preprocessing (resolvable), the PCA projection and reservoir architecture are self-contained, and the statistical tests are named. The main gaps are: (1) explicit statement of random seeds for the fixed random reservoir (stated as 'fixed' but seed not given), (2) confirmation that cited preprocessing references are resolvable, and (3) minor clarifications on cross-validation fold structure in some analyses.

## HARD gaps — reproduction blockers
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Random seeds or seed-averaging statement** — The fixed random recurrent network J and J_in are described as 'fixed random' with spectral radius 0.95 and σ_in=0.01, but no random seed is stated. The statement 'The same reservoir (identical J, J_in) was reused for every session' indicates one fixed reservoir, but the seed used to generate it is not provided, making exact reproduction impossible.

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Human subjects/clinical] Participant demographics** — Cohort sizes stated (40 CC, 40 AD subjects for main analyses; 145 CC, 41 AD subjects in full pool), but age, sex, MMSE, disease stage, and other demographics are not provided in the manuscript. These data exist in ADNI but are not reported here. The manuscript states 'The exact cohorts and sample sizes used in every analysis are listed in Table S1' but Table S1 only lists session/subject counts, not demographics.

## Documented (for the record)
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Dataset version and exact train/val/test split** — Table S1 specifies cohorts used in each analysis: 'Data and preprocessing' section states 'Resting-state functional MRI sessions were downloaded from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) database (adni.loni.usc.edu)'; 40 CC and 40 AD subjects used for main analyses; repeated stratified 5-fold cross-validation (80/20 train/test, 10 repeats) explicitly stated in Methods 'Classification' section.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Architecture and algorithm specification** — Reservoir computing with fixed random recurrent network of N=2000 rate units, spectral radius 0.95, membrane time constant τ, linear read-out W fit by ridge-regularised least squares. Equation 1 gives the update rule. Read-out fitting described in Methods with noise regularisation σ. Two classifiers used: balanced Fisher LDA and random forest (300 trees, √· features, minimum leaf size 3, balanced class weights).
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Hyperparameters** — Reservoir: N=2000 units, spectral radius 0.95, τ (effective instantaneous), J_in σ_in=0.01, feedback gain f=0.1. Read-out: regularisation noise σ swept over [0.003, 0.5], reference σ=0.025. PCA projection N_PC=50. SVD embedding dimension K swept [2,50]. Oscillatory drive: frequency f swept [0,0.5] cycles/step, amplitude A swept [0,20]. All parameter sweeps shown in figures.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Training procedure (optimizer, schedule, early-stopping)** — Read-out fitting: ridge-like regularised least squares with additive Gaussian noise (Eq. 2), Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse. No iterative optimizer or early-stopping; closed-form solution. Teacher-forcing with feedback gain f=0.1 for 140 time steps, first 10 discarded. No learning-rate schedule or adaptive optimization mentioned.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Library versions and hardware** — 'Analyses were implemented in Python (NumPy, scikit-learn, nilearn, Matplotlib).' Specific versions of NumPy, scikit-learn, nilearn, Matplotlib not provided. Hardware not specified.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Code availability** — 'All analysis and figure-generation code is openly available at github.com/cristianocapone/AD-reservoir-FC-stimulation.' Specific commit hash or version tag not stated, but repository URL is provided.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Metric definitions** — Balanced accuracy, AUROC, Pearson correlation r, Normalized Mean Squared Error (NMSE), distance (1−corr), reclassification rate (fraction crossing decision boundary), net cure rate (fraction of AD-side patients moved to CC side). All clearly defined in text and figure captions.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Ablations and sensitivity analysis** — Extensive ablations: K (SVD components) swept in Fig. 3A–D; regularisation noise σ swept in Fig. 3C–D; frequency f swept in Fig. S7A; amplitude A swept throughout Figs. 4–5, S7–S8; comparison of three site-selection criteria (ΔW, eigenmode, LDA-resonant) in Figs. 5–6, S4–S6; comparison of read-out interpolation vs oscillatory stimulation in Figs. 4–5, S5; leave-one-patient-out cross-validation in Fig. S9; learning-curve analysis in Fig. S1.
- **[Human subjects/clinical] IRB approval and informed consent** — 'This work is a secondary analysis of de-identified, publicly available human imaging data obtained from ADNI. The ADNI study was approved by the institutional review boards of all participating sites, and all participants (or their authorised representatives) provided written informed consent at enrolment; no new data were acquired for the present study.' (Ethics section)
- **[Human subjects/clinical] Inclusion/exclusion criteria** — 'cognitively unimpaired controls (CC) and patients with Alzheimer's disease (AD)' from ADNI. Specific ADNI inclusion/exclusion criteria not restated in this manuscript but are standard ADNI criteria (publicly documented). Sessions filtered: '>100-volume filter' mentioned for tangent-space SVM benchmark. Quality-control filtering mentioned: '40 AD subjects passing quality control' but specific QC criteria not detailed in this manuscript.
- **[Human subjects/clinical] Trial registration (for interventional studies)** — This is a secondary analysis of observational data (resting-state fMRI) with in-silico modeling, not a prospective interventional trial. No trial registration required. The in-silico stimulation is a computational counterfactual, not a clinical intervention.
- **[Human subjects/clinical] Power calculation** — Learning-curve analysis (Fig. S1) shows AUROC still rising with cohort size, extrapolating to ~0.76 at N=100 and ~0.80 at N=200 per group. Authors acknowledge 'sample size rather than a ceiling of the representation' and note 'accuracy had not saturated at the present sample size.' No prospective power calculation stated, but post-hoc learning curve provided.
- **[Protocol provenance] fMRI acquisition and preprocessing** — Acquisition parameters in Fig. 1A: TR=3.0 s, 140 volumes (~7 min), Schaefer-100 + HO-21 subcortical atlas (121 parcels), 24-HMP + WM + CSF confounds, 0.01–0.10 Hz bandpass. Preprocessing described as 'minimally preprocessed' from ADNI. Specific preprocessing pipeline (SPM, FSL, etc.) not named in main text. Methods state 'Images were minimally preprocessed and parcellated into N_p=121 regions of interest.' ADNI's standard preprocessing is well-documented and publicly available; this is a resolvable delegation.
- **[Protocol provenance] Reservoir model and read-out fitting** — Fully self-contained in Methods: Eq. 1 (reservoir update), Eq. 2 (read-out fitting), teacher-forcing procedure, closed-loop reconstruction, all hyperparameters specified. No delegation to prior work.
- **[Protocol provenance] Classification and cross-validation procedure** — Balanced Fisher LDA and random forest specified with parameters. Repeated stratified 5-fold cross-validation (80/20, 10 repeats) described. Nested 5×5 patient-level CV for tangent-space SVM benchmark described. Leave-one-patient-out CV for leakage-free evaluation (Fig. S9) fully detailed in Methods. Self-contained.
- **[Protocol provenance] In-silico stimulation protocols** — Read-out interpolation (W_int formula) and oscillatory stimulation (A sin(2πft)) both fully specified. Frequency sweep, amplitude sweep, site selection criteria (ΔW, eigenmode, LDA-resonant) all detailed. Self-contained.
- **[Protocol provenance] Statistical tests** — Two-sided Mann–Whitney U test for group comparisons (e.g., Fig. 1C, Fig. 4A). Specific test named and two-sided stated. No multiple-comparison correction mentioned; with many comparisons across figures, this is a potential concern but tests are named.
- **[Protocol provenance] Data availability statement** — 'The imaging data analysed here were downloaded from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) database (adni.loni.usc.edu). ADNI data are available to qualified researchers upon registration and acceptance of the ADNI Data Use Agreement; in accordance with that agreement, the raw and derived imaging data are not redistributed here.' Clear statement of data source and access policy.
