# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript contains a comprehensive reference list with DOIs for all journal articles and standard citations for books. All cited works appear resolvable via the provided DOIs or are well-known texts. No citations to '(data not shown)', '(unpublished)', or '(in preparation)' were found. The manuscript attributes several background claims to cited works (e.g., MYO10 functions, collagen effects, haptotaxis system description, PFKL mutant phenotype). While these attributions are plausible and the references are from reputable venues, the full content of the 2025 papers (Fortunato et al., 2025; Hansen and Webb, 2025) could not be independently verified during this audit. No direct quotations or numerical values are lifted from references, so quotation fidelity is not applicable. There is no evidence of self-citation (the author Dutta does not appear in the reference list) or citation padding. No cited works are flagged as retracted or from predatory venues. Overall, the citation integrity is high, with the only limitation being the inability to confirm the exact content of the most recent citations.
