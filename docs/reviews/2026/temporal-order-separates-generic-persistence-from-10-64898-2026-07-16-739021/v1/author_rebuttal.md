## Disagreements
- **data_analysis** — MYO10–collagen n=3 repeats (one anomalous) cannot support 'reproducible' interaction or simultaneous bands — fatal flaw requiring new data.
  - Response: The manuscript already qualifies the finding in Discussion: 'inference remains limited to this specific experimental batch' and 'repeat-consistent in this dataset.' We accept the meta-review's directive to scale Abstract/main-text claims to 'repeat-consistent in this dataset' (concession #10). The methodological contribution (exact order-null, algebraic closure, three-question separation) stands independently of the replication level of this specific demonstration dataset. The simultaneous bands and leave-one-FOV-out are correctly computed given the hierarchy; their coverage properties are limited by n=3, which we now explicitly state. This is a qualification, not a fatal flaw in the method.
  - Manuscript reference: Discussion: 'The MYO10–collagen result remains the discovery setting and has the strongest recovered experimental hierarchy. Equal-FOV and equal-repeat inference, fixed cohorts, leave-one-FOV-out analysis, and measured-context matching support a repeat-consistent buffering interaction. The order-null makes the additional contribution explicit: most of the 60-min interaction is absent when the same steps are temporally rearranged.'

  - Response: The manuscript explicitly states in Table 5 and Discussion: 'Biological-replicate identity was unavailable in the public trajectory labels, so intervals are track-level' and 'track-level intervals cannot replace replicate-level inference.' - **methodology** — Analytical order-null stationarity assumption untested; may inflate sequence-excess (~70% claim) — fatal if unaddressed.
  - Response: We address this by adding the explicit stationarity caveat (concession #1).
