# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** minor

## Synthesis
The panel is unusually consistent: seven of eight reviewers score 4-5, with reproducibility the sole dissenter at 3/5 (conf 4). All reviewers agree the theoretical core is sound — Theorem 2's level-wise recursion exploiting monotonicity of S(t), with proofs in Appendix A, reduces the global O(|S_T|^3) solve to O(P^3N^2 + P^2N^3) phase-level solves (methodology, rigor, novelty all endorse this). The state-dependent switching intensities \u03bb_{p,p'}(s,i) are agreed to be a meaningful, if incremental, extension of Almaraz & G\u00f3mez-Corral (2018) and Artalejo & G\u00f3mez-Corral (2010). Ethics is clean (5/5): public aggregate data, disclosed funding and conflicts, and candid statements of limitation.

Two genuine tensions emerged and were sharpened by the debate.

(1) Overclaiming. The rigor reviewer flags as HARD that the abstract's 'results show how switching mechanisms affect...' invites a causal reading that the very next sentence disowns. The advocate calls this an emphasis problem; the skeptic countered that the title ('Evaluating the Impact of Epidemic Control') carries the same promise before any disclaimer is reached. The skeptic wins the narrow point that this is more than a single sentence, but the advocate wins the substantive one: the disclaimer is present in the abstract, Section 3 and Section 4, and multiple reviewers (ethics, novelty, data_analysis, methodology) independently credit the authors for candour. This is a framing fix, not a claim that outruns evidence in the way that would warrant major revision.

(2) Verification of the numerics. This critique survived the debate essentially intact. The methodology reviewer's HARD flag \u2014 no positive control (Monte Carlo, matrix exponentiation, or published benchmark) validating the recursive implementation \u2014 combines with the reproducibility reviewer's HARD flag that code and processed data sit behind 'available upon reasonable request' with no repository, DOI, seed, or software versions, and with data_analysis's and rigor's shared concern that the Abate\u2013Whitt inversion is unvalidated and that negative/non-finite values were silently zeroed before plotting. The advocate did not rebut this; he reclassified it as revision-fixable, which the skeptic conceded. The panel therefore agrees on the substance and differs only on whether it blocks acceptance.

Secondary, converging weaknesses: no sensitivity analysis on the fixed \u03b3 = 1/3, on the switching rates q and \u03c8, or on the ad hoc coefficients in Eq. 19 (raised independently by methodology, rigor and data_analysis); no head-to-head demonstration of what the regime-switching structure adds over a one-phase stochastic SIR (novelty, methodology); unverified future-dated self-citations and the uncited Feng & Wang (2023) concurrent work (literature, novelty); partial weeks and integer rounding of MLEs unexamined (data_analysis, rigor).

## Decisive Factors
The confidence-weighted average (4.03, modal verdict 'minor') is the right landing place, and I adopt it rather than escalating to major. The reason is a distinction the guidelines make explicitly: the paper's central claims are theorems with proofs supplied, and its empirical claims are already scaled down to 'conditional model-based comparisons' by the authors themselves. Nothing in the panel argues that a stated conclusion is contradicted by the evidence; the criticisms are about verification and framing, both of which are addressable without new science.

That said, two items must be treated as conditions of acceptance rather than optional polish, because they are the only HARD flags that survived the advocate's rebuttal: (a) deposit code and processed data in a versioned public repository with a DOI, including software versions and the optimization random seed \u2014 the reproducibility reviewer's 3/5 rests almost entirely on this, and it is cheap to fix; (b) supply at least one independent validation of the recursive implementation (Monte Carlo simulation or matrix exponentiation for N=63, P=3) plus a check on the Abate\u2013Whitt inversion against a known density, with disclosure of how many values were zeroed. Without (b), the paper's distinguishing selling point \u2014 exact distributional computation \u2014 remains proven but not demonstrated.

I also ask for the title and abstract's opening sentence to be reframed so the conditional scope is stated before the effect language (the skeptic's title point is well taken), for at least a one-way sensitivity analysis on \u03b3 and on the Eq. 19 coefficients, and for confirmation of the publication status of the 2025/2026 self-citations plus a brief comparison to Feng & Wang (2023). These are substantial but bounded requests, consistent with 'minor' at a venue whose reviews are published alongside the preprint and where the reader's main need is to know exactly what has and has not been checked.
