# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript describes a shotgun single-cell top-down proteomics (SC-TDP) method applied to 13 individual mouse cardiomyocytes. Triggered categories include: Model organisms (mouse cardiomyocytes), Mass spectrometry (proteomics), and Computational/ML (data analysis software). The work is largely well-documented in methods, with clear instrument specifications, search parameters, and software versions. However, several HARD gaps exist: the IACUC protocol number is missing, the exact ProSight PD version behavior for the three-node search strategy is unverifiable from the manuscript alone, and the cardiomyocyte isolation protocol is delegated to a single reference whose full contents cannot be confirmed. Additionally, the data-availability statement is incomplete (figshare link promised but not provided in the text), and random seeds for any stochastic analysis steps are not stated. SOFT gaps include lack of power justification for the n=13 cell choice and absence of explicit randomization/blinding statements.

## HARD gaps — reproduction blockers
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] IACUC protocol number** — Methods state 'All animal studies were approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center and The Scripps Research Institute, and carried out in accordance with the National Institute of Health guidelines.' No specific IACUC protocol number is provided.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Repository accession (PRIDE/MassIVE)** — Data availability statement: 'Raw spectrum data are available via figshare.' No PRIDE or MassIVE accession number provided; figshare link is not included in the text.
- **[Computational/ML (data analysis)] Random seeds or seed-averaging statement** — No mention of random seeds for any stochastic steps in data analysis (e.g., if ProSight PD or UniDec employ randomization). No statement that results are seed-averaged or that a single seed was used.
- **[Computational/ML (data analysis)] Code availability** — No statement regarding availability of custom analysis code, scripts, or workflows. Data availability mentions figshare for raw spectra but not code.
- **[Cross-cutting] Statistical test and error-bar definition** — The manuscript reports identification counts and overlap heatmaps (Figures 2A–B) but does not employ inferential statistics or error bars. No statistical tests are named. Reproducibility is assessed qualitatively (e.g., 'retention times were highly reproducible') without confidence intervals or statistical measures.
- **[Cross-cutting] Data-availability statement completeness** — Data availability section states 'Raw spectrum data are available via figshare.' but does not provide the actual figshare URL or accession identifier in the manuscript text. Supporting tables (S1, S2) are mentioned as available but not linked.
- **[Cross-cutting] Protease and phosphatase inhibitor cocktail catalog number** — Methods mention 'protease and phosphatase inhibitor cocktails (Thermo Fisher Scientific)' multiple times but do not provide the specific product catalog number (e.g., Halt Protease and Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail #78440 or similar).
- **[Cross-cutting] BCA assay kit catalog number** — Methods: 'quantified using a BCA assay kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific)' without a specific catalog number.

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Protocol provenance] Cardiomyocyte isolation protocol (delegated to reference 46)** — Methods: 'Adult mouse ventricular cardiomyocytes were isolated as described previously.' with citation to reference 46 (Karlstaedt et al., Circ Res 126, 60–74, 2020). The manuscript does not provide the full isolation protocol in-house. While the reference is resolvable (DOI provided), the manuscript does not confirm that reference 46 contains sufficient detail for independent reproduction, and any deviations from that protocol are not explicitly stated.
- **[Protocol provenance] EThcD fragmentation strategy (delegated to references 25–27)** — Methods: 'Our EThcD strategy evolved from our previous publications (Gomes et al.)' citing references 25–27. The manuscript states the strategy was used but does not fully describe the optimization or validation steps in-house. References are resolvable (DOIs provided), but the manuscript does not confirm they contain the complete protocol or state explicitly how the current work deviates from prior methods.

## SOFT gaps — recommended
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Power justification for sample size choice** — No justification provided for why n=13 cardiomyocytes was chosen or whether this represents a powered study design.
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Randomization and blinding statement** — No explicit statement regarding randomization of cell selection or blinding during analysis. Methods describe cell size selection ('we limited our cells to be approximately the same size') but not random assignment or analyst blinding.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Quantification method** — The manuscript reports identification of proteoforms but does not describe a quantification method (e.g., label-free, spectral counting, intensity-based). Analysis is qualitative/discovery-focused.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Technical replicates for single-cell samples** — Bulk cardiomyocyte sample analyzed in triplicate (stated: 'analyzed in triplicate (10 ng total protein)'). Individual cardiomyocytes: 'Analysis of 13 individual cardiomyocytes' with no mention of replicate injections or technical replicates per cell.

## Documented (for the record)
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Species, strain, and source** — Methods: 'Wild-type male and female mice (strain C57BL/6J) were purchased from Jackson Laboratories.'
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Sex and age of animals** — Methods: 'Male and female mice (8-12-week-old)' for cardiomyocyte isolation; bulk sample used 'adult male C57BL/6J wild-type mouse.'
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Sample size (n) per group and what n represents** — Main text: 'Across 13 individual cardiomyocytes isolated from mouse heart' and 'Analysis of 13 individual cells yielded 57 proteins and 165 proteoforms.' n=13 cells analyzed; n=1 bulk sample in triplicate (technical replicates). Unclear how many animals these cells/samples came from.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Instrument model and acquisition mode** — Methods: 'Orbitrap Fusion Lumos mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher)' operated 'in intact protein mode' with specified resolving power (15,000–60,000), AGC targets, and µscans.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Sample preparation and lysis protocol** — Methods detail lysis buffer composition: '30% TFE and 65% DMSO in 5% formic acid, supplemented with protease and phosphatase inhibitor cocktails.' For bulk sample: 'mechanical disruption using a Dounce homogenizer.' For single cells: 'sonicated in ice for ~1 min.' Protease/phosphatase inhibitor cocktails identified by vendor (Thermo Fisher Scientific) but not specific product catalog number.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Chromatography and column specifications** — Methods: 'fused silica capillary (~20-30 cm × 75 µm i.d., with a 5 µm pulled tip) packed in-house with C4 reversed-phase resin (2.7 µm, 1000 Å, Halo).' Mobile phases and gradient conditions specified.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Fragmentation method and parameters** — Methods: 'Two fragmentation modes were used in consecutive scans: 1) electron-transfer/higher-energy collision dissociation (EThcD) with calibrated ETD charge-dependent parameters and supplemental activation (HCD collision energy of 23-25%).' In-source fragmentation voltage set to 15V. RF lens set to 75%.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Search engine and version** — Methods: 'Raw files (bulk sample and individual cardiomyocytes) were processed using ProSight PD 4.5 (Thermo Fisher Scientific).'
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Database and version** — Methods: 'searched against a Mus Musculus database (Uniprot) containing annotated modifications. The database was subsequently converted into a ProSight PD-compatible (.psdb) database.' Uniprot version/date not specified.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] FDR threshold and modifications searched** — Methods: 'Results were filtered using a false discovery rate (FDR) threshold of 1% at both the protein and proteoform levels.' Modifications: 'wide absolute precursor mass tolerance (200 Da)' to capture 'unexpected modifications' and 'up to two phosphorylation events.' Specific PTMs searched (acetylation, phosphorylation, trimethylation, succinylation) are mentioned in results but not exhaustively listed in methods.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Mass tolerances (precursor and fragment)** — Methods: 'three-node strategy' with 'wide absolute precursor mass tolerance (200 Da)' for unexpected modifications, 'narrow absolute precursor mass tolerance (2.2 Da)' for known proteoforms, and 'narrow precursor biomarker mass tolerance (10 ppm)' for truncations. 'Fragment mass tolerance was set to 10 ppm' for all nodes.
- **[Computational/ML (data analysis)] Software and tool versions for data processing** — Methods: 'Freestyle (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and UniDec' for standard protein mixture; 'ProSight PD 4.5 (Thermo Fisher Scientific)' for bulk and single-cell samples. 'ProSight Lite and TDValidator in ProSight PD 4.5' for spectral interpretation. UniDec version not specified.
- **[Computational/ML (data analysis)] Key analysis parameters (deconvolution, search strategy)** — Methods: 'Precursor and fragment ions were deconvoluted using the Xtract algorithm.' Three-node search strategy described with specific mass tolerances and FDR filtering. However, Xtract algorithm parameters (e.g., charge-state range, intensity thresholds) are not detailed.
