# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel is unanimous that the technical core of this work is sound: the workflow validation (protein standards, triplicate bulk runs, a consistent core proteoform set across 13 cells) and, especially, the fragmentation-based PTM site localization (MLC-2 A1 trimethylation with diagnostic losses, T51 phosphorylation supported by b53/c59 ions and TDValidator, Qcr7 K11 succinylation) were credited by methodology, rigor, and novelty reviewers, and the skeptic conceded the point in debate. The specific proteoform identifications are therefore treated as settled and are not at issue in this decision.

The split (4 minor / 4 major, weighted 3.52) concerns the headline claim rather than the data. Three reviewers independently converge on one load-bearing problem: all 13 cells derive from a single animal, there is no technical-noise control (repeat analysis of one lysate) and no null model, and the manuscript itself invokes stochastic MS/MS undersampling to explain replicate discrepancies (Fig. S4). On the present design, the 18-70 proteoform range per cell cannot be attributed to biology. Because that claim sits in the title and abstract, and because the figure that carries it visually (Fig. 2) currently has no defined metric or scale, the required fix is a substantive reframing plus new quantification, which exceeds 'minor.' I depart from neither the draft recommendation nor the average here; the objections are not independent nuisances but all point at the same claim.

I weighed the rebuttal carefully and it improves the outlook materially. The authors concede nearly every actionable item (proteoform definition, Fig. 2 metric and color scale, singleton/shared breakdown, size-vs-count regression, DOI deposition, UniProt/software/.psdb provenance, delta-search node assignment and FDR aggregation, IACUC number and COI scope, softening of 'unbiased'/'functional heterogeneity'/'previously unrecognized', and citation of the recent cardiac single-cell literature). Their disagreement with the novelty reviewer is partly credible: Melby et al. (2023) is already cited and differentiated in the manuscript, the platform and cell type differ, and the proposed reframing to 'extends proteoform-level heterogeneity analysis to cardiomyocytes via a shotgun LC-MS platform' is the right resolution. Their protein-vs-proteoform differential-overlap argument is a reasonable interpretive point and may be presented as such, but the authors themselves acknowledge it is not a formal statistical test, so it cannot license the biological-heterogeneity claim in the title or abstract.

On the two critiques the authors flag as fatal: I am NOT requiring new multi-animal cohorts, a dedicated technical-noise control, or orthogonal antibody validation of the novel PTMs as conditions of acceptance. In lieu of those, the manuscript must explicitly and prominently state that biological and technical variance cannot be partitioned in this design, and the headline claims must be scoped accordingly. That option is available to the authors and is the path I expect them to take.

The methods audit returned eight HARD gaps, all of which are addable in revision (protocol number, resolvable deposit, code/parameter availability, reagent catalog numbers, statistical/error-bar reporting, and clarification of the single-animal provenance of the 13 cells). The citation audit found zero HARD gaps; the one load-bearing item, the 35-147 um cardiomyocyte size range attributed to Ref 20, must be re-sourced or corrected because it underpins the cell-selection rationale. None of these is grounds for rejection.

This is a real contribution to single-cell top-down proteomics once the claims are matched to the evidence. I invite a major revision.

## Required Revisions
1. Reframe the paper as a validated SC-TDP feasibility and proteoform-cataloguing study. Revise the title, abstract, and conclusions to remove or scope 'unbiased' (use 'untargeted'), 'functional heterogeneity' (use 'molecular/compositional heterogeneity'), 'previously unrecognized', unsupported 'first' claims, and biomarker speculation, per the authors' own proposed wording. Where a 'first' claim is retained, document the literature/database search scope that supports it or rephrase to 'we report'.
2. Add an explicit, prominent statement (in the Results or Discussion AND in the abstract where the heterogeneity result is reported) that the single-animal design without a technical-noise control cannot partition biological from stochastic MS/MS sampling variance, and that the 18-70 proteoform range per cell is therefore not attributable to biology on the present evidence. New animals or a repeat-lysate control are NOT required; the differential protein-vs-proteoform overlap argument from the rebuttal may be presented in the Discussion, but explicitly labelled as a qualitative observation rather than a statistical test.
3. Define the Figure 2 overlap metric numerically in the legend (shared identifications divided by the pairwise union, as proposed) and rescale the color bar with numbered ticks (0-100%). Add the promised supplementary table reporting: proteins with 1 vs >1 proteoform; and the number and percentage of the 165 proteoforms that are singletons (1 cell), shared by 2-5 cells, and shared by all 13 cells.
4. Add the scatterplot/regression of protein and proteoform counts against cell length (and total protein estimate where available) for the 13 cells, with the correlation coefficient reported in the Results.
5. Deposit the raw spectra with a resolvable public DOI (figshare acceptable; a PRIDE or MassIVE accession is preferred for MS data) and cite the identifier explicitly in the Data Availability statement. Add a code/script availability statement covering the .psdb conversion procedure and any custom analysis scripts.
6. Complete the computational and MS provenance in Methods: UniProt release date/version; software versions for Xtract, UniDec, ProSight PD, ProSight Lite, and TDValidator; key Xtract parameters (charge-state range, intensity thresholds); the .psdb conversion procedure; explicit statement that the 200 Da delta tolerance applied only to node 1; and whether the 1% FDR was computed per node or across the combined result set.
7. Re-source or correct the 35-147 um mouse cardiomyocyte size range currently attributed to Ref 20 (Chazarin et al. 2025, Circ Res 137, 1377-1379). This value is load-bearing for the cell-selection rationale and could not be verified from the cited brief report; provide a citation to the actual source of the range or report your own measurement basis.
8. State explicitly in Methods how many animals contributed the 13 single cells and the bulk sample (the audit could not determine this from the text), and add the IACUC protocol number(s) for both institutions.
9. Expand the Notes/COI statement to specify the nature of K.R.D.'s interest in ProSight PD (consulting, equity, royalty, or other).
10. Add the Discussion paragraph citing and contrasting the recent single-cell cardiac proteomics/proteoform literature (e.g., Marin-Vicente 2026, Hu 2026, Gao 2025), and reframe the novelty statement relative to Melby et al. (2023) along the lines proposed in the rebuttal ('extends proteoform-level single-cell heterogeneity analysis to cardiomyocytes via a distinct shotgun LC-MS platform', with the combinatorial PTM co-occurrence on a single MLC-2 backbone as the specific advance).
11. Add the operational definition of 'proteoform' in the Introduction (per Consortium for Top-Down Proteomics/HUPO usage) and state which categories (PTM, truncation, sequence variant) are included in the reported proteoform counts.
12. Add catalog numbers for the protease/phosphatase inhibitor cocktail and the BCA assay kit, and state explicitly whether the cardiomyocyte isolation followed Ref 46 without deviation or, if modified, describe the deviations.

## Minor Suggestions
- Where identification counts or overlap values are reported, state explicitly that the analysis is qualitative/discovery-oriented and that no inferential statistics or error bars are applicable, rather than leaving this implicit.
- Briefly justify the choice of n=13 cells (practical/instrument-time constraints are an acceptable justification) and note whether cell selection or analysis involved any randomization or blinding.
- State whether any technical replicate injections were performed on single-cell samples; if none, say so explicitly, since this bears directly on the sampling-noise discussion.
- Consider noting explicitly that no quantification method (label-free, intensity-based) was applied, so all reported results are identification-based.
- Several claims sourced to Refs 14, 10, 32, 36-40, 42-45 could not be verified from abstracts alone. Please re-check that the specific numbers and mechanistic statements attributed to these sources (notably '~50 proteoforms' from Johnson et al. 2022 and '~100 ng total protein, ~1500-2500 um' from Melby et al. 2023) match the cited papers, and adjust or add page-level specificity where needed.
- If a seed-dependent step exists anywhere in the analysis pipeline, state the seed or note that no stochastic steps were used; a single sentence suffices.
