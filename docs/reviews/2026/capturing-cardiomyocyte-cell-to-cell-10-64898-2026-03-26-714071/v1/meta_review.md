# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel is unanimous that the technical core is sound and worth publishing in some form. Methodology, rigor, and novelty reviewers independently credit the workflow validation (protein standards, triplicate bulk runs, consistent core proteoform detection across 13 cells) and, most importantly, the fragmentation-based PTM site localization (MLC-2 A1 trimethylation with 42 Da shift and diagnostic losses, T51 phosphorylation via b53/c59 with TDValidator, Qcr7 K11 succinylation). The skeptic explicitly conceded this point in debate, so it is settled: the specific proteoform identifications are credible.

The split is over the paper's headline claim rather than its data. Four reviewers (clarity, data_analysis, ethics, methodology) landed at 'minor'; four (literature, novelty, reproducibility, rigor) at 'major'. The 'major' cluster converges on three mutually reinforcing problems that the advocate's 'just add numbers' framing did not survive. First, the inferential gap: methodology, data_analysis, and rigor independently note that all 13 cells come from one animal, no null model or negative control (repeat analysis of one lysate) exists, and the authors themselves invoke stochastic MS/MS undersampling to explain replicate discrepancies (Fig. S4). The 18-70 proteoform range per cell is therefore not attributable to biology on the present evidence. Second, framing: rigor flags 'unbiased,' 'functional heterogeneity,' 'previously unrecognized,' and biomarker language as unlicensed by the data; novelty notes Melby et al. (2023) already demonstrated single-muscle-cell proteoform heterogeneity, so the 'first insights' framing must become 'extends to cardiomyocytes.' Third, verifiability: reproducibility flags a figshare deposit with no DOI, an unspecified UniProt release, an unspecified .psdb conversion, and ambiguous application of the 200 Da delta search across nodes. Clarity independently flags that the Fig. 2 heatmap has no defined metric or scale, so the central heterogeneity figure is currently uninterpretable. Literature adds missing recent cardiac single-cell/proteoform work and unsupported 'first' claims.

The advocate is right that a large share of these are text-and-deposition fixes, and right that the feasibility demonstration stands independently. The skeptic is right that no amount of text revision converts the current design into evidence for biological heterogeneity. Both can be satisfied: reframe the paper as a validated SC-TDP feasibility and proteoform-cataloguing study, quantify overlap properly, and either add a technical-noise control/multi-animal data or explicitly retire the biological-heterogeneity claim from the title and abstract. Ethics is essentially clean (IACUC stated, funding and COI disclosed) with minor gaps: protocol number, COI scope for the ProSight PD commercial interest, and code/parameter availability.

## Decisive Factors
I land at 'major' rather than 'minor' despite the 3.52 weighted average and an even 4-4 verdict split, because the objections are not independent nuisances but all point at the same load-bearing claim in the title and abstract. The heterogeneity claim is what the paper is sold on, and three separate reviewers plus the skeptic establish it is confounded with stochastic MS sampling in a single-animal design. This survived the advocate's rebuttal intact: variance decomposition cannot be recovered from Table S2. Adjacent to that, the Fig. 2 heatmap that carries the claim visually has no defined metric (clarity, highest-confidence reviewer at 5), and the Melby et al. (2023) precedent (novelty) undercuts the 'previously unrecognized' framing on priority grounds too. Because the fix is either new control data or a substantive retitling/reframing plus quantification, this exceeds 'minor.' It is not 'reject': the chemistry and workflow validation are sound and conceded by the skeptic, and the contribution is real once the claims are matched to the evidence. Conditions for acceptance: (1) reframe title/abstract/conclusions to a feasibility and proteoform-catalogue contribution, removing 'unbiased,' 'functional heterogeneity,' 'previously unrecognized,' unsupported 'first' claims (or scope them explicitly against a documented database/literature search), and biomarker speculation; (2) either provide a technical-noise control and/or multi-animal replicates, or explicitly state that biological versus technical heterogeneity cannot be resolved here; (3) define the Fig. 2 overlap metric numerically and report the singleton/shared proteoform breakdown and the protein-to-proteoform mapping; (4) deposit raw data with a resolvable DOI and specify UniProt release, software versions, .psdb conversion, delta-search node assignment, and FDR aggregation; (5) cite and situate the recent single-cell cardiac proteomics literature; (6) add IACUC protocol number and expand the ProSight PD COI scope.
