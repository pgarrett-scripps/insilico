# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** reject

## Synthesis
The panel converges strongly: five of eight reviewers recommend rejection, with the two highest-confidence reviewers (clarity 2/5@5, rigor 2/5@5) among them. Only the ethics reviewer scores above threshold, and does so on the narrow grounds that a purely computational secondary-use study triggers no HARD ethics requirements (though funding/COI/data-availability statements are missing). Literature and novelty reviewers land at 3/5 with major-revision framing.

All reviewers agree on what is real: the curation effort (821 non-synonymous mutations, 31,073 isolates, 149 studies, 30 years) and the multi-tool triangulation are substantive, and the raw frequency atlas has standalone reference value. The skeptic conceded this three times.

The split is not about whether the atlas is useful but about whether the paper's central deliverable is salvageable by revision. Three independent hard critiques cluster:

(1) The lethal/moderate/mild/neutral ranking (Table 2: 340/284/185/12) that anchors every headline claim is never operationally defined. Methodology, data_analysis, reproducibility, and rigor independently flag this. The debate sharpened it usefully: the advocate showed the manuscript does state an informal graded rule over five already-tabulated dimensions, so formalization is achievable without new data. The skeptic's arithmetic point survives, however \u2014 507 concordant deleterious + 79 concordant neutral leaves 235 of 821 mutations with undisclosed classification logic, so Table 2 cannot currently be audited.

(2) Ascertainment/survivorship bias. This critique survived contact with the advocate entirely unrebutted across two rounds. By design (Fig. 4), the corpus contains only phenotypically resistant, viable isolates; mutations with severe fitness cost cannot appear. The finding that prevalent mutations are 'mild' is therefore close to tautological within the sampling frame, and the design cannot separate 'Mtb favours mild mutations' from 'only mild mutations survive to be sampled.' The rigor and methodology reviewers both flag this as HARD, and no formalization of the ranking addresses it because it is upstream of labeling.

(3) No statistical testing of the central hypothesis. The data_analysis reviewer notes no chi-square/Fisher test comparing category prevalence, no CIs or heterogeneity tests on the WHO-region comparisons, no inter-tool agreement metric, no multiple-comparison control, and no validation of docking energies whose reported differences (~0.1\u20130.5 kcal/mol) sit within typical docking noise.

Secondary but real: no data or code deposition with a DOI; incomplete structural models for ~226 emb mutations that are nonetheless ranked uniformly; contested novelty against Portelli et al. (2018) and absent 2020\u20132025 WGS/ML resistance-prediction literature; pervasive clarity defects including core category definitions appearing ~15 pages after first use.

I side with the skeptic. The advocate's framing \u2014 that overclaiming is a hedging fix \u2014 is correct as far as it goes, and the ranking-disclosure gap is indeed closable. But the paper as constituted is organized as mechanistic discovery of a 'universal strategy,' and the two load-bearing supports for that framing (a validated ranking, and a design capable of distinguishing selection from survivorship) are both absent. What remains after honest hedging is a descriptive atlas plus untested in silico predictions \u2014 a different, much narrower paper.

## Decisive Factors
The recommendation matches the confidence-weighted signal (2.47/5, five reject votes, both maximum-confidence reviewers rejecting), so no divergence needs justifying. The decisive factor is the ascertainment-bias critique, which the advocate declined to engage in either round: the study samples only viable, phenotypically resistant isolates, so 'prevalent mutations are mild' is largely entailed by the sampling frame rather than evidenced against it. This is a design-level flaw, not a presentation or disclosure one, and it invalidates the 'universal strategy'/'guided evolutionary pathway' framing that the title, abstract, and discussion are built around. Compounding it, the central quantitative deliverable (Table 2's four-tier ranking) is unauditable \u2014 235 of 821 mutations have no disclosed classification logic \u2014 and the hypothesis is never formally tested with any statistic. Reject rather than major revision because the fixes required are not additive: they entail reframing the paper from mechanistic discovery to descriptive resource, re-specifying and validating the ranking, adding formal statistical testing, and either acquiring external validation (published fitness assays, clinical outcomes, phylogenetic selection coefficients) or abandoning the selection claim. Authors are encouraged to resubmit the atlas plus hedged in silico characterization as a clearly hypothesis-generating resource paper, with data and code deposited under a DOI, the concordance algorithm published as an explicit decision tree, emb mutations flagged as structurally uncertain, and an explicit differentiation from Portelli et al. (2018) and recent WGS/ML prediction work.

The ethics reviewer's 4/5 is correctly reasoned within its scope and does not offset the methodological consensus; its own findings (missing funding, COI, and data-availability statements) are conditions on any eventual publication rather than evidence of soundness.
