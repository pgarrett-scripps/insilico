# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This bioinformatics study of drug-resistant TB mutations cites 149 publications and relies on WHO reports for epidemiological context. Reference resolvability checks identified that WHO reports (2020, 2021) and several landmark papers (Heym et al. 1995, Sherman et al. 1996) are verifiable, but five foundational citations (Telenti et al. 1993, Scorpio et al. 1997, Sreevatsan et al. 1997, Gagneux et al. 2006, Comas et al. 2012) could not be confirmed via PubMed search despite correct citation format. Claim–citation support shows that specific epidemiological statistics and technical details about mutation mechanisms are attributed to prior work but cannot be independently verified from the manuscript alone. An internal inconsistency exists regarding study timeframe (both "30 years" and "25 years" stated). No retracted or predatory sources were detected. Overall, the citation infrastructure is sound in format but several secondary sources remain unverifiable and one factual inconsistency requires clarification.
