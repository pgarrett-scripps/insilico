# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel signal is clearly negative (2.47/5 weighted; five reject votes, including both maximum-confidence reviewers), and the draft recommendation was rejection. I depart from that recommendation and offer a major revision, for the following reasons, which turn on the rebuttal.

The meta-review identifies two load-bearing problems: (1) the four-tier lethal/moderate/mild/neutral ranking is unauditable (235 of 821 mutations have undisclosed classification logic), and (2) ascertainment/survivorship bias makes the headline 'Mtb favours mild mutations' claim close to tautological within the sampling frame. The panel also notes the absence of any formal statistical test of the central hypothesis, no data/code deposition, and structurally unsupported ranking of ~226 emb mutations.

The rebuttal is unusually responsive and, critically, it does not attempt to defend the indefensible. The authors concede the survivorship-bias critique outright, name it as a design-level limitation the paper 'did not adequately confront', and commit to deleting the mechanistic framing ('universal strategy', 'guided evolutionary pathway', 'Mtb favours/prefers/chooses') from title, abstract, results and discussion, replacing it with explicitly correlational language and reframing the contribution as a hypothesis-generating atlas plus in silico characterisation. They likewise concede the ranking-opacity critique and commit to an explicit decision tree plus pseudocode, a per-mutation input/output table (S12) allowing Table 2 to be reconstructed line by line, threshold sensitivity analyses, and Fleiss' kappa across the functional predictors. They concede the statistics gap with specific, appropriate tests (chi-square with effect sizes and CIs, BH-FDR across regional comparisons, Wilson CIs, Spearman correlation). They concede the deposition gap (Zenodo DOI for data, models and raw tool outputs; GitHub tagged release; funding/COI/data-availability/AI statements), the docking and modelling parameter gaps (including redocking RMSD validation against 1SJ2 and 3PL1), the emb structural-uncertainty problem (flag as uncertain, report components as missing rather than imputed, present Table 2 with and without emb), the 'first comprehensive study' overclaim versus Portelli et al. (2018) and recent WGS/ML work, the Q10P/abstract inconsistency, and the possibly misattributed pncA essentiality citation.

The two disagreements they do raise are credible and narrowly scoped: wild-type docking baselines were in fact reported in the text (they nonetheless accept hedging and commit to stating baselines in Table 1 with an explicit ~0.1-0.5 kcal/mol noise caveat), and a post-hoc power calculation is indeed ill-defined for a literature census (they accept deleting the 'globally realistic patterns' claim and substituting Wilson CIs and per-region denominators, which is the better remedy).

The meta-review's case for rejection rests on the fixes being 'not additive' - that honest hedging leaves a different, narrower paper. I agree with the diagnosis but not the disposition. The authors have volunteered precisely that narrower paper, including title-level reframing, and a rigorously specified, deposited, statistically tested atlas of 821 mutations across 31,073 isolates and 149 studies has genuine standalone reference value that all eight reviewers acknowledged. Nothing in the remaining work requires new data or an unobtainable validation set, provided the selection claim is abandoned rather than defended. That is a reframing-plus-disclosure revision, which is what major revision is for.

Authors should be clear-eyed about the bar: this decision is conditional on the reframing being thoroughgoing rather than cosmetic. If the revised manuscript retains mechanistic-selection language anywhere in the title, abstract, or discussion, or if Table 2's counts still cannot be reconstructed from the deposited supplementary data, the paper will be rejected at the next round. The ranking will be accepted as reproducible-but-unvalidated only if it is labelled as such throughout; no external fitness/MIC/clinical validation is required in this cycle, but no predictive claim may be made in its absence. The seven HARD methods-completeness gaps and the citation-integrity items below must be closed in full.

## Required Revisions
1. Remove the mechanistic-selection framing completely. Delete 'universal strategy', 'guided evolutionary pathway', 'uncovering the root', and all 'Mtb favours/prefers/chooses' constructions from the title, abstract, results, discussion, and figure legends, replacing them with the correlational phrasing committed to in the rebuttal. Provide a marked-up change log listing every location altered so the editors can verify completeness.
2. Add a dedicated Limitations subsection that states explicitly that the corpus is conditioned on viable, phenotypically resistant isolates, that mutations with severe fitness cost cannot appear in the sampling frame, and that the observed enrichment of 'mild' mutations among prevalent variants is therefore equally consistent with survivorship filtering and with active selection - a distinction this design cannot make. This statement must also appear in the abstract and at the first presentation of the prevalence-versus-severity result, not only in Limitations.
3. Fully specify and make auditable the lethal/moderate/mild/neutral ranking. Provide (i) an explicit decision tree figure plus Methods pseudocode giving the exact input from each of the five dimensions, all numeric thresholds (ConSurf bin cutoff, SIFT/PROVEAN/PolyPhen-2 score thresholds, I-MUTANT/mCSM stability cutoffs, dGG_binding sign and magnitude), and the majority/tie-breaking rule; (ii) an 'indeterminate' category or explicit rule covering the 235 mutations with discordant functional calls; (iii) Supplementary Table S12 listing all 821 mutations with every raw input and resulting rank, such that Table 2's 340/284/185/12 counts can be reconstructed line by line; (iv) the committed threshold sensitivity analysis and Fleiss' kappa across the three functional predictors.
4. State explicitly, in the abstract, Methods, and Discussion, that the ranking is reproducible but not externally validated against measured fitness costs, MICs, or clinical outcomes, and that no predictive performance is claimed. Remove or hedge any statement implying the categories forecast clinical or biological consequence.
5. Add the formal statistical testing committed to in the rebuttal: chi-square test of independence between mutation-frequency tertile and conservation category with effect size and 95% CI; chi-square heterogeneity test across the six WHO regions with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control over pairwise comparisons; Wilson 95% CIs on all reported single-mutation frequencies (Table 1 and Supplementary Table S3); and Spearman rho with p-value replacing the qualitative claim in Section 3.4. Report per-region isolate denominators alongside Figure 6.
6. Deposit data and code under a citable DOI (HARD methods gaps: dataset deposition, code availability, data-availability statement). Deposit the curated mutation dataset, all 603 mutant PDB models, and raw ConSurf/SIFT/PROVEAN/PolyPhen-2/I-MUTANT/mCSM outputs and AutoDock grid/log files on Zenodo under CC-BY with a dataset version/date; deposit curation, frequency, and ranking scripts on GitHub with a tagged release archived to the same DOI. Cite the DOI in the manuscript and add explicit Data Availability and Code Availability statements.
7. Close the remaining HARD methods-reproducibility gaps: give version numbers and access dates for every tool (ConSurf, SIFT, PROVEAN, PolyPhen-2, mCSM, Pfam, InterProScan, SMART, COACH-D, 3D LigandSite, PROCHECK, ERRAT, RING, and I-MUTANT build/date); state random seeds (or an explicit seed-averaging statement) for MODELLER model generation, GROMACS minimisation, and AutoDock GA runs, and deposit the seeds; specify ligand preparation (protonation state, charge method, conformer generation) and grid centring; state number of docking runs (50 as proposed), RMSD clustering threshold, and pose-selection rule; state the GROMACS minimisation algorithm, convergence criterion, max steps, and restraints; state MODELLER template-selection parameters and explicit molpdf/DOPE/GA341 thresholds. Report redocking RMSD against 1SJ2 and 3PL1 as the docking validation control.
8. Handle the emb mutations as proposed: flag all mutations lacking a validated structural model as 'structurally uncertain' in Table 2 and Supplementary Tables S8-S12, report their stability and docking rank components as missing rather than imputed, present Table 2 both with and without emb mutations, and move the structural-coverage limitation from Methods into Results and Limitations.
9. Add a Related Work subsection with the comparison table against Portelli et al. (2018), the WHO 2021/2023 mutation catalogue, and 2020-2025 WGS/ML genotype-to-phenotype pipelines (including TB-Profiler, PhyResSE, TBDB), stating precisely what the atlas adds and what it does not. Delete the unqualified 'first comprehensive study' claim.
10. Resolve the citation-integrity items. Verify the pncA essentiality attribution to Baddam et al. (2018) and either replace it with a primary transposon-mutagenesis essentiality source or delete the claim and the downstream inference in Section 3.6.2.5. Supply DOIs or PMIDs for all references and confirm the five foundational citations flagged as unresolvable (Telenti 1993, Scorpio 1997, Sreevatsan 1997, Gagneux 2006, Comas 2012) plus Zhang 1992, Taniguchi 1996, and the WHO figures (10 million cases / 1.5 million deaths 2020; 2.9 million gap), correcting any that do not support the attributed claim.
11. Correct the internal inconsistencies flagged: the 30-year versus 25-year timeframe discrepancy on p. 9; the Section 3.2/3.5 mutation-count contradiction (add the proposed table of total versus non-synonymous counts for all four targets); and the abstract's 'most prevalent mutations occur in non-conserved areas' claim, which must be qualified for pncA Q10P as committed.
12. Disclose the data-handling decisions not currently described: isolate-level duplication across independent publications (with an explicit statement that cross-study de-duplication was not possible from published summary data), the equal-weighting of isolates, and the synonymous-exclusion rule against H37Rv. Add the proposed leave-out-largest-study sensitivity analysis showing frequency rankings are not driven by a single cohort.
13. Add the missing front-matter statements: Funding, Competing Interests, Data and Code Availability, and AI-tool use.
14. Implement the clarity fixes: move the lethal/moderate/mild/neutral and site-1/2/3 definitions into Section 2 as a boxed definitions panel ahead of first use; complete the Table 1 legend (define 'C', site-1/2/3, 'presence in domain region'); complete the Figure 9b legend (define the three sites, state the 821-mutation denominator); relabel the Figure 7 y-axis; and state wild-type reference docking energies in Table 1 with the explicit caveat that ~0.1-0.5 kcal/mol differences fall within AutoDock uncertainty and do not support rank-ordering individual mutants.

## Minor Suggestions
- Consider offering the deposited tables in a format directly mergeable with TB-Profiler / PhyResSE / TBDB schemas, as proposed in the rebuttal - this strengthens the resource claim materially.
- Report the compute environment (hardware, and a conda/Docker/requirements specification) alongside the code deposition.
- State the ConSurf homolog database, E-value threshold, alignment algorithm, and tree method rather than relying on server defaults; likewise state COACH-D and 3D LigandSite confidence thresholds.
- Include the literature search string with Boolean operators and database-specific syntax, and note whether the review protocol was registered (e.g. PROSPERO) or state that it was not.
- The 'globally realistic patterns' phrasing should simply be deleted, as the authors propose; the editors accept that a post-hoc power statement would add nothing beyond the Wilson CIs and per-region denominators.
