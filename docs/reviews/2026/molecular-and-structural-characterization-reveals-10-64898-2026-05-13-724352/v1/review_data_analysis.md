# Statistics & Data-Analysis Reviewer

## Summary
The authors deploy a genuinely wide, and mostly appropriate, arsenal of EV-characterization methods, and they are honest about the low tetraspanin levels and the qualitative nature of the cryo-TEM findings. The paper's statistical infrastructure does not support its central claims as stated: the quantitative comparisons that carry the narrative (EV number/size by vFC, the proteomic "significant" protein lists, and the co-culture Aβ-accumulation contrast) are each built on a replication design from which valid inference cannot be drawn. The experiments appear sound, but are presented as *n* = 1 biological sample per genotype, with technical replication treated as biological replication throughout. This is fixable, but requires more biological replicates and a reporting of the actual independent-unit sample sizes.

## Weaknesses
- Pseudo-replication in the co-culture experiment. The claim that WT organoids co-cultured with AD organoids accumulate more intratissue Aβ (Figure 7C–D) rests on a Student's t-test over "n = 27 images for WT+AD and n = 49 images for WT+WT". Images are not the independent unit of analysis. If these images are drawn from one (or even a few) organoids per condition, the effective sample size is the number of organoids, not the number of images, and the p-value — whatever it is — is meaningless: standard errors are minimized by the number of tiles 27/49, not 2–4. The text never states how many independent organoids were co-cultured per condition, nor whether images were sampled randomly, staged, or selected for quality. A ROSOM with organoid as random effect, or an analysis aggregated to the organoid level, is what the comparison actually requires.
- vFC vesicle-count and size claims have no stated test and no independent replication. The report "The AD organoid EV isolate contained more EVs compared to the WT organoid (~3.1 e9/mL vs 1.6 e9/mL)" and the "wider size distribution" advance in the Abstract are not supported by any statistic in the text. Each value appears to come from a single EV isolate per genotype — one pooled 120 mL harvest per apS. That yields * n = 1 per condition, for which no inferential statement is possible. The size/count comparisons are thus descriptive figures, not findings, and should be either accompanied by at least 3–6 biologically independent EV preparations (collections separated in time, and compiled), or explicitly demoted from claims you made to observations. "Very low levels of tetraspanin expression (~20 ABC per EV)" is likewise a single estimate not subject to actual variant measurement.
- The "significant" proteomics list has *n* = 1 biological sample per genotype. Deep profiling from a single AST/AD pooled conditioned-media harvest, run in "technical triplicate", produces 3848 proteins with 170 (WT)-upregulated and 184 (AD-upregulated) at FC ≥ 2 / adj p ≤ 0.05 — and a series of strongly errant candidate numbers e.g. RAB8A, FC 2.2, adj p = 0.030. The reported p-values and adj.p-value sequences measure protocol reproducibility between three injections of one biological mixture, not biological signal between genotypes. The multiple-comparison correction (MSstats dataProcess) is performed correctly, but correct science gating cannot rescue biological replication that is not recorded. Every differential-importclaim resting on these lists is over-stated to the precision the design supports; the experience is reframable: these are candidate distinguishing proteins — validate on three or more biological replicates are needed.
- ThT binding — "25-fold higher than synthetic liposome control" has no stated n, no error bars, and no test; numerically the signal is integer-level; flat line versus a fiber calibration is missing.
- He IP-ELISA "Aβ42 — present at the highest level in the TS+ eluate" has no stated n, no test, no input-size normalization — one trifecta of undefined statistics.
- We lower variance in the FT0 bar plot was not defined as "SEM" or "SD"; figure legends don't provide exact p-values, no CIs, at "value > threshold (yet 4.3.2 'n = technical replicates' pre-defines the whole paper's level of replication as technical-ended).
- L scalar "elementary instrument-free" image-based quantitative measure (copy the ROUT-detect outputs) — the size of the FDR on repeated-measures that would have been obtained by 4-difference shrinkage — neither the Αβ-threshold or the normalization coefficient (per cell) — for "volume per cell" as reported now established.
- The abstract's "differential expression of tetraspanins CD63, CD9, CD81" — is not supported even at the descriptive level of §3.2, which says the populations were "phenotypically similar" and showed "very low TE". This is not a question; it is a direct contradiction.
