# Journal Recommendations

## As-is (current quality)
- **bioRxiv**
  - Fit: This is an empirical, multi-method characterization of extracellular vesicles from Alzheimer's disease cerebrocortical organoids — exactly the kind of preprint that bioRxiv hosts in its neuroscience and cell biology sections, and the manuscript already has the structure of a preprint with deposited proteomics data.
  - Realism: High — bioRxiv is a preprint server with no peer review, so posting is essentially certain regardless of the major-revision verdict.

## After required revisions
- **Journal of Extracellular Vesicles**
  - Fit: The paper is a comprehensive EV characterization (isolation, vFC, cryo-EM, proteomics, amyloid-dye binding, transfer experiments) with an isogenic AD model — squarely within JEV's scope as the flagship ISEV journal for EV biology and methodology.
  - Realism: Moderate — JEV is selective and would require the independent-batch replication, transfer controls, and cryo-EM quantification the editor demands, but the novelty (isogenic PSEN1-M146V + multilamellar/fibrillar cryo-EM) is a genuine fit.
- **Journal of Extracellular Biology**
  - Fit: A sister ISEV journal focused on EV biology and function, well suited to the mechanistic EV-transfer and cargo-characterization content once the required controls are added.
  - Realism: Moderate-to-good — less selective than JEV, so realistic after the major revisions are addressed.
- **Stem Cell Reports**
  - Fit: The manuscript centers on a human iPSC-derived cerebrocortical organoid model of familial AD (PSEN1-M146V) and its disease-relevant phenotypes, which is a core Stem Cell Reports topic.
  - Realism: Moderate — the organoid model and isogenic design are a strong fit, but the journal would likely want the EV findings framed around the organoid disease model and the replication issues resolved.

## Alternative outlets
- **Alzheimer's & Dementia**
  - Fit: The AD-relevant content (Aβ, tau, amyloid spread, PSEN1 mutation) fits this translational AD journal, and it publishes organoid-model and biomarker-oriented work.
  - Realism: Low-to-moderate — a high-profile clinical/translational journal that would require the full replication and controls; more likely a stretch than a realistic target at current quality.
- **Journal of Alzheimer's Disease**
  - Fit: A specialty AD journal that regularly publishes organoid-model and EV-based studies of amyloid pathology, offering a narrower but well-matched home for the disease-specific claims.
  - Realism: Moderate — realistic after revision; the journal is receptive to mechanistic organoid work even with modest sample sizes, though the inferential issues would still need addressing.
- **bioRxiv (updated preprint)**
  - Fit: If the headline journals are out of reach, the authors can post a revised preprint incorporating the required controls and replication, making the work citable and reviewable while it seeks a venue.
  - Realism: Certain — posting is not gated on peer review, and the deposited proteomics/code already support the reproducibility the panel valued.

## Notes
The intended target (In Silico) is an overlay review venue, not a hosting journal, so it is not listed as a submission target; the manuscript is squarely in scope for it as a preprint under review. Fit for the specialty journals is strong on topic but the editor's required revisions (independent batches, transfer controls, cryo-EM quantification, ethics statement) are prerequisites before any of the after_revision venues become realistic. Flag: the ethics/consent gap must be resolved before submission to any journal that requires IRB documentation.
