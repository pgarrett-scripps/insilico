## Notes
In Silico's overlay model — reviewing existing preprints rather than hosting papers — is a structural fit for this manuscript, which already exists as a preprint (proteomics deposited to PRIDE). However, the decision letter's required revisions are extensive and the panel verdict is mixed (2.88/5, 7 major/1 reject). The descriptive core (isogenic organoid EV characterization) is publishable after revision; the mechanistic spread claim requires substantial new data. The as_is bucket is therefore realistic only for arXiv/fallback; after_revision venues include the target and comparable cell-biology/EV journals; alternative covers preprint and narrower specialty outlets. I've excluded Journal of Extracellular Vesicles (ISEV's flagship) because the TS-negativity result and single-replicate proteomics would face very stiff review; JEV would be an aspirational stretch even after revision.
