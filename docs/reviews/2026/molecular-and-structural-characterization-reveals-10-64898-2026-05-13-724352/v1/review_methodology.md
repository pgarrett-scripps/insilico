# Methodology Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript has real strengths — the isogenic WT/PSEN M146V design, the inclusion of "permeate" and high-molecular-weight SEC fractions as analytical comparators, and a WT+WT co-culture baseline. Based on the paper as I read it (text only; I cannot inspect the figures), the two headline claims outrun their evidence. The co-culture experiment does not exclude free-dye release or soluble-factor transfer as the route between the two organoids, and the EV proteomic comparison is built on a single differentiation batch per genotype, with the technical triplicates being the same sample injected three times. The central "non-cell-autonomous" claim therefore is not established as stated; the design can support it if the missing controls and biological replication are added.

## Strengths
- The isogenic WT/PSEN1M146V comparison controls for genetic background across all measurements.
- EV characterizations consistently carry the EV-depleted "Permeate" and SEC "High MW protein" fractions as comparators for the EV fraction itself.
- The co-culture study includes a WT+WT arm rather than judging only the WT+AD condition against pre-co-culture tissue.

## Weaknesses
- Load-bearing — HARD: the co-culture/"non-cell-autonomous" claim. The abstract concludes "and there is evidence of endosomal membrane transfer between isogenic cultures with an increase of Aβ peptides in the WT organoids." The design supports the conclusion only if the fluorescent signal that appears in the WT is actually transferred intracon membra — but the Aco-650 stained AD organoid was merely "rinsed several times" and then co-cultured for 7 days. With no evidence that free dye is fully removed from the well, the fluorescent signal observed in the WT could be dye diffused out (from dead/leaky cells), not vesicle-bound, and taken up directly by WT cells. The claim about the Aβ increase in WT is similarly under-separated: soluble factors, inflammatory mediators, and/or cell stress in the shared well are all plausible causes; the design that would separate EV-mediated transfer from release — passaged AD conditioned medium with EVs vs. EV-depleted AD medium vs. a transwell insert — is not present. What would settle it: (1) a condition in which attempt-confirmed 'no-organoid' or filtered stained-AD medium is added to a naive WT organoid; (2) a transwell that allows soluble factors but the vesicles; /3) an added dye-only control to show the washout kinetics. The second issue is sampling: the quantification reports 27 vs 49 images (z-stacks in the tile), but the number of independent co-culture w characters and the treatment of images as the experimental unit are not disclosed. If 27/49 images came, for instance, from one co-culture, the test is pseudo-replication.
- Load-bearing — H2: the EV proteomic differences. The comparison WT vs AD EVs is derived from one prep (pooled above one differentiation batch), analyzed in technical triplicate. Technical NMR of the same peptide digest cannot address the covariate of biological variability — the batch effects of the differentiation procedure and genotype are now intertwined. The stated claims (Chapter 5's "significant differences", "secretory autophagy" protein set) need a design where two to three fully independent, interleaved batches per genotype are processed blind, and the "n" of statistical analysis is the number of batches, not the number of injections. Without this, the 170/184 significant proteins cannot be attributed to M146V.
- Load-bearing — H3: "fibrillar material" in EVs. The abstract records "AD-deficient EVs display luminal fibrillar material," but the cryo-EM evidence as reported is: fibrillar in vesicle lumen sections, "often nearby vesicles or in dense clumps," and partly co-purified. If it's adjacent/freely-floating tissue, it is not shown to be luminal; and in the absence of immunolabeling (or a cryo-ET tomography plane through the vesicle), fibrillar morphology alone doesn't establish one protein composition. The fact that WT and AD differences are illustrated with 65 AUC-positive micrographs vs 17 WT—without a per-genotype count of total EVs, the proportion with loose fibrils — only one-quarter. Provide a per-vesicle quantitation and a labeled FIBRIL protein element; otherwise, the distinct claim is not evidenced.
- The abstract's "differential expression of tetraspanins" is at odds with the body text stating that WT and AD are "phenotypically similar" — an internal inconsistency; one of the two statements should be changed or shown with precise fluorescence quantified.
- The "flat / similar" conclusion from the vesicle flow cytometer relies on `TS` events at or below the antibody binding capacity (ABC) LOD — measuring at the instrument's detection floor can only show "≥", not equivalence, so "phenotypically similar" is unjustified.
- In the tetraspin IP experiment, there is a negative bead arm (TS−) but no positive single concentration (i.e., an EV preparation known to express CD63/CD9/CD81 processed through the same workflow); without it, a plausible null ("the capture didn't work") is not excluded — particularly for the tau-negative result (tau was in the flow-through).
- The ThT "EVNAMS" assay compares only to liposomes rather than to a biochemically matched EVs able whose protein/RNA are digested (RNase A, proteinase K): since ThT binds RNA and other non‑amyloid hydrophobic aggregates, the signal cannot be called "amyloid in EV" without these digest controls per vesicle.
- The tissue/confocal "amyloid in WT" quant is described only as "segmented nuclei + Aβ signal" — no descriptions of how WT vs AD cells were distinguished in the same well, or from when cell lines were preshopped, and no blinding of region selection; which matters in the multiple-extracondition.

## Questions
- What is the level of Aco-650 (or free dye) in the culture medium immediately post-rinse and at day 1/4/7 — and how would you distinguish that pooled "dye in solution" from vesicle-bound dye being taken up by the WT?
- How many independent co-cultures were performed per condition, and how were cell traces for analysis assigned to WT versus AD organoid identity after clearing/immunostaining (which organoids in the analysis, if the cell Tracker signal was used)?
- Of triple MS injections — are these (y) the three same sample, and how many separate differentiations, were evaluated (by genotype) to independently replicate the protein fold changes in Figure 5A?
- In the cryo-EM micrographs counted, how many total micrographs (rather than "positive" ones) are present per genotype — and how many micrographs with fibrillar material adjacent to/hiding in vesicles, i.e., inside the vesicle lumen?
