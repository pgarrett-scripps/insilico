## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate's core move is to call the two main objections 'fixable in revision' and therefore not fatal. But that framing mislabels the problem. The load-bearing claims are not merely overstated in the abstract — they are unsupported at the level of the data, and the fixes are not cosmetic edits but experiments that have not been done.

First, the co-culture claim. The advocate says the honest fix is to 'scale the claim.' But the rigor, methodology, and literature reviewers all independently identify the same gap: the Aco-650 signal in WT organoids is not established as transferred endosomes at all. The dye is a small fluorophore that can diffuse through shared media after release from dead/leaky cells; the design (shared well, full media changes, only 'rinsed several times') cannot exclude free-dye carryover. No EV-depleted-media control, no transwell, no dye-only washout control exists. This is not an interpretation problem — it is a missing control that determines whether the central phenomenon occurred. Scaling the claim to 'transfer of endosomal material' still asserts the transfer, which the design does not demonstrate.

Second, pseudo-replication is not a 'design addition' — it is the difference between a finding and an anecdote. The proteomics 'significant' list (170/184 proteins), the vFC 2x EV count, and the co-culture p-value all rest on n=1 biological sample per genotype, with technical triplicates of one injection. The data-analysis reviewer is explicit: 'the p-value — whatever it is — is meaningless.' The abstract's 'wider size distribution' is contradicted by the authors' own text ('not significantly different') and by the vFC data showing identical medians. The abstract's 'differential expression of tetraspanins' directly contradicts the body's 'phenotypically similar.' These are internal contradictions, not overclaiming that a hedge fixes.

FATAL: the central claims — EV-mediated spread, differential proteomics, wider size distribution — are not supported by the evidence as designed. FIXABLE only if the missing biological replicates and the dye-transfer controls are actually run.

**Key points:**
- The co-culture claim lacks the decisive control (EV-depleted media, transwell, dye-only washout); scaling the wording does not establish that transfer occurred at all
- Pseudo-replication is not a design addition — n=1 biological sample per genotype makes the proteomics 'significant' list, the 2x EV count, and the co-culture p-value inferentially void
- Abstract contradicts body: 'wider size distribution' vs 'not significantly different'; 'differential tetraspanins' vs 'phenotypically similar' — internal contradictions, not hedges
- The fixes are experiments not yet run (independent differentiations, transfer controls), so the claims are unsupported as designed — FATAL for the central claims, not cosmetic revision
