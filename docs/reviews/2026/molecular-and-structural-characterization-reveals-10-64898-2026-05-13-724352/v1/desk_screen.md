## Rationale
This is an original basic-science research manuscript characterizing extracellular vesicles from Alzheimer's disease and isogenic wild-type cerebrocortical organoids. It falls squarely within In Silico's scope: the claims are checkable from the manuscript itself and its deposited materials (proteomics data deposited to ProteomeXchange, analysis code on GitHub, detailed methods for all assays). It is not a clinical trial, diagnostic/treatment guidance, or a non-research submission, and no fundamental, unfixable flaw is evident on its face. Any concerns about the strength of specific conclusions (e.g., the interpretation of the co-culture transfer and amyloid spread data, or the statistical treatment of the proteomics) are matters for the full review panel rather than grounds for desk rejection.
