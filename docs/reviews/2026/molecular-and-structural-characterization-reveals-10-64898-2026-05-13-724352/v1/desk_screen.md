# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This manuscript is an original research preprint describing the characterization of extracellular vesicles from Alzheimer's disease and wild-type cerebral organoids. It falls squarely within In Silico's scope: the claims are empirical and checkable from the manuscript and its deposited materials (proteomics data deposited to ProteomeXchange, analysis code on GitHub). The work is not a clinical trial or treatment guidance, and the central evidence (EV purification, flow cytometry, cryo-EM, proteomics, co-culture experiments) is presented in detail and is inspectable. The claims are scaled to the evidence and the authors flag limitations (e.g., tau not co-purifying with tetraspanin EVs, inability to observe tau spread). The manuscript is complete and intelligible. It should proceed to full review.
