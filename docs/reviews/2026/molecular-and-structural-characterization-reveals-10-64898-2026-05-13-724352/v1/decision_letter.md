# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
All eight reviewers identified the same core tension: the descriptive EV characterization (purification, vFC, cryo-TEM morphology, proteomics, dye binding) is methodologically sound and genuinely novel in an isogenic-PSEN1 organoid system, but the headline claims — the abstract's size and TS statements, the mechanistic 'EV-mediated spread' narrative, and the 'failed AEV biogenesis' biomarker — all outrun the evidence as presented. The advocate's case (first isogenic AD-vs-WT organoid EV study; cryo-TEM fibrils extending Fowler et al. into live organoids; deposited proteomics) is persuasive for the descriptive core, and the skeptic's objections (single biological replicate; co-culture does not isolate EVs; overclaiming in abstract) are empirically correct and must be addressed. The numerical signal (2.88/5, 7 major/1 reject) supports major revision rather than rejection, because no reviewer argued the descriptive data are unsalvageable — every weakness is fixable with additional controls, replication, requalification or corrections. The compliance audits found one HARD gap (IHC antibody identifiers missing) and no retracted/predatory sources. I depart from the raw average only in rejecting the '1 reject' signal, which was based on correct-but-fixable objections. The required revisions above are ordered by importance: biological replication and co-culture vector control are essential before any mechanistic claim can stand; the citation and figure-panel corrections are straightforward; the ethics statement is mandatory. A revised manuscript that satisfies these requirements would be appropriate for In Silico's scope.

## Required Revisions
1. 1. Provide biological replication. The vFC count comparison, the proteomics and the co-culture Aβ quantification each need at least three independent organoid differentiations per genotype before any claim of a genotype-driven difference can stand. Until then, state that all findings are from a single paired differentiation and frame them as preliminary or hypothesis-generating.
2. 2. Isolate the vector in the co-culture experiment. The claim of EV-mediated transfer is not supported by the current design. Add at least one control that distinguishes EV-mediated transfer from free-dye/diffusible-factor transfer — for example a transwell co-culture that shares media but blocks vesicle contact, or a conditioned-medium-alone control. Until added, requalify all claims in the abstract and section heading from 'EV-mediated spread' to 'membrane and Aβ transfer between co-cultured organoids'.
3. 3. Correct all overclaiming in the abstract to match the body. The abstract must be rewritten so that: (a) the size-distribution claim states the actual result (non-significant trend, not 'wider'), (b) the tetraspanin claim states that expression was at or below the detection limit in both genotypes, and (c) the Aβ measurement is described as immunoreactive puncta, not 'amyloid-β peptides'.
4. 4. Provide ethics approval and donor-consent statement for the hiPSC lines. Cite the protocol identifier or approval number.
5. 5. Correct the figure-panel citations. The fibril and MLV claims cite Figure 4G (WT panels) — correct to Figure 4I. Cite the correct panels throughout (Section 3.6's Figure 2C reference should be Figure 3; Section 3.2's 'Figure 3D-E' should be 'Figure 3D-F').
6. 6. Reconcile or correct the TS ABC numbers and inequalities. The text gives '>20 ABC' and '~38 ABC' — provide the correct single measured value with a clear statement of what is above vs below the detection limit.
7. 7. Reconcile the statistical test for the co-culture experiment. The Methods say two-way ANOVA; the Figure 7 legend says Student's t-test. State the single correct test and report all assumptions checked.
8. 8. Correct the two SNARE citations. Kumar et al. 2018 and Matsui et al. 2018 are both STX17 papers; they do not support the STX3/STX7 claims. Replace with sources that actually establish STX3 as a secretory SNARE and STX7 as a degradative-autophagy SNARE, or reattribute.
9. 9. Fix the citation of Mirdha 2024 and Budvytyte & Valincius 2023. Neither is about EVs; both are about lipid-membrane interactions. Cite EV-specific aggregation studies instead, or requalify the framing to 'lipid membranes influence Aβ aggregation' (distinct from EVs). Acknowledge that Halipi et al. 2024 reports the opposite direction (EVs slow aggregation).
10. 10. Add MLV/fibril frequency counts to the cryo-TEM comparison. Report the number of vesicles with MLVs or fibrils per total vesicles examined for each genotype, with confidence intervals.
11. 11. Provide vendor/catalog/clone/RRID for the three IHC primary antibodies (Aβ XP, AT8, MAP2) in Methods 2.16.
12. 12. Pin the analysis code to a versioned commit or archived DOI, and supply the exact Uniprot FASTA accession/version and DIA-NN library-generation parameters so the search is reproducible.
13. 13. Report the number of independent organoids (not images) that contributed to each condition in the co-culture quantification, and re-analyse with organoid as the unit.
14. 14. State an explicit competing-interest declaration covering all authors, not only J.W.K. and S.A.L.
