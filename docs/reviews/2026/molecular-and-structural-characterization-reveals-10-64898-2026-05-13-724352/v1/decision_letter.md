# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel's numerical signal (confidence-weighted 3.00/5; 6 major, 1 minor, 1 reject) and the specialist reports converge on a clear picture: the manuscript's core contribution is genuinely novel and the underlying experiments are largely sound, but the central claims are presented at a strength the current evidence does not license. The novelty reviewer found no preempting preprint and rated novelty 4.0 — the isogenic PSEN1-M146V design and the cryo-EM multilamellar/fibrillar observations are a real delta over Fowler 2025. The proteomics pipeline is exemplary for reproducibility (PRIDE PXD076102, GitHub code, full DIA-NN/MSstats settings). However, three load-bearing claims outrun their evidence: (1) the co-culture 'non-cell-autonomous spread' claim lacks the decisive controls (EV-depleted media, transwell, dye-only washout) needed to distinguish vesicle-mediated transfer from free-dye diffusion or soluble-factor effects; (2) the proteomic 'significant' lists, the vFC 2x EV-count difference, and the co-culture p-value rest on n=1 biological sample per genotype, with technical triplicates of a single injection treated as biological replication — the data-analysis reviewer is explicit that the co-culture p-value is inferentially void under this design; (3) the abstract contains internal contradictions with the body text ('wider size distribution' vs 'not significantly different'; 'differential expression of tetraspanins' vs 'phenotypically similar'). The rigor reviewer credits the body text's candour but notes the overclaiming concentrates in the abstract and title. The ethics gap (no consent/IRB statement for the donor-derived hiPSC lines) is a required one-paragraph amendment. The fibrillar-material claim rests on micrographs not deposited anywhere, and the EV concentration factor is unspecified. These are fixable with new experiments (independent differentiations, transfer controls) and reporting additions — hence major rather than reject — but they are not cosmetic edits: the fixes require new data or reanalysis whose outcome could change a conclusion.

## Required Revisions
1. Add 2–3 fully independent, interleaved differentiation batches per genotype for the EV proteomics comparison, process them blind, and report the number of biological replicates; aggregate statistical analysis to the organoid/batch level (the number of batches, not the number of injections), and re-report the differential protein lists (170/184) and all fold-changes/p-values on this basis. If this is not feasible, explicitly demote the differential-proteomics and EV-count claims from findings to candidate observations.
2. Add the decisive controls to the co-culture experiment to establish that the Aco-650 signal in WT organoids is vesicle-mediated transfer rather than free-dye diffusion or soluble-factor effects: (a) an EV-depleted AD conditioned-medium control added to a naive WT organoid, (b) a transwell insert that allows soluble factors but blocks vesicles, and (c) a dye-only (no-organoid) washout control showing the dye is fully removed post-rinse. Report the Aco-650 concentration in the medium at day 0/1/4/7.
3. Reconcile the abstract with the body text: remove or qualify 'wider size distribution' (contradicted by the authors' own 'not significantly different' and by vFC showing identical medians) and 'differential expression of tetraspanins CD63, CD9, CD81' (contradicted by 'phenotypically similar' and TS at/below the LOD). Scale the 'non-cell-autonomous spread of amyloid-containing EVs' claim to 'transfer of endosomal material and amyloid-associated signal,' explicitly flagging the templating interpretation as unproven.
4. For the co-culture Aβ quantification, state the number of independent organoids per condition, report the Arivis 'Simple Threshold' value used, and re-analyze with organoid as the independent unit (e.g., mixed-effects model or aggregation to organoid level) rather than treating images as the experimental unit.
5. Quantify the frequency of multilamellar vesicles and fibril-containing vesicles per genotype from the cryo-TEM data (report total micrographs per genotype, not just 'positive' ones), and either molecularly identify the fibrillar material (immunogold or targeted proteomics) or explicitly state that its identity is unconfirmed.
6. Deposit the cryo-EM movies/micrographs in an accessible repository (EMPIAR accession) and state the classification criteria for 'fibril' vs artifact; state the final volume of the concentrated EV sample after the 10K MWCO spin column and the overall concentration factor from the 120 mL conditioned media so the reported EV concentrations can be reproduced.
7. Add a statement in Methods or Acknowledgements covering (a) informed consent/IRB approval for the original donor-derived hiPSC derivation and (b) that the present reuse/EV study falls within the scope of that consent or an IRB 'not human subjects research' determination by the receiving institution, citing the relevant protocol/MTA identifier.
8. Add biological negative controls for the ThT/AmyTracker signal (e.g., EVs from a non-AD or non-neuronal source, and RNase A/proteinase K digest controls to exclude RNA or non-amyloid hydrophobic aggregates), and report the n and error bars for the ThT assay.

## Minor Suggestions
- Add a positive control (an EV preparation known to express CD63/CD9/CD81) to the tetraspanin immunoprecipitation workflow to exclude the null that the capture did not work, particularly for the tau-negative result.
- Normalize EV counts and concentrations to organoid number, mass, or cell count so the 2x EV difference can be attributed to the mutation rather than culture size.
- Pin the GitHub analysis code with a commit hash or archived DOI, and deposit the custom Python scripts used for the cryo-TEM violin plots.
- Complete the reference list entries that lack volume/page identifiers (e.g., Fowler 2025, Mao 2025, Forero 2024) and confirm the Jakhria 2014 attribution for the membrane-rupture claim.
- Acknowledge the contradicting Halipi 2024 finding (EVs slow Aβ aggregation) in the sentence claiming EVs enhance aggregation propensity.
- Define the arrow colors in the Figure 4 legend and state what the Aco-650 signal represents in the Figure 7 WT-organoid image.
