# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel agrees the manuscript makes at least one solid, well-supported contribution: that ATP hydrolysis is mechanistically dispensable for MreB polymerization, demonstrated by two independent ATPase-dead mutants (E136A, D158A) and the ADP-AlF4- result, converging across TIRF, HS-AFM, anisotropy, QCM-D and in vivo assays (novelty, methodology, rigor, data_analysis all note this as reviewer-uncontested). Ethics (5/5) and clarity (4/5) are strong, and literature flags only fixable citation gaps (most notably the omitted Pande et al. 2022 paper, directly relevant to the novelty claim). The point of genuine, sustained disagreement is the paper's headline claim — that continuous *intrafilament* nucleotide exchange 'tunes' filament stability and constitutes 'a new class of biological polymer behavior.' Five reviewers (rigor, novelty, methodology, data_analysis, reproducibility) independently converge on the same gap: the FRAP evidence (Fig. 3F ATP* recovery vs Fig. 3E TFLime non-recovery) cannot distinguish true intrafilament nucleotide exchange from lateral subunit diffusion, solution rebinding, or exchange confined to filament ends, and the capping experiment (Fig. 4G-H), while ruling out a simple end-cap-only model, does not establish that interior exchange rather than an ADP-bound-end/nucleotide-free-interior scenario drives destabilization. The Monte Carlo model is repeatedly noted as circular support (it encodes exchange as a parameter and reproduces data consistent with, not diagnostic of, the mechanism), and reproducibility further identifies a hard failure: the model code underlying this central claim is undeposited, promised only 'after publication,' making the load-bearing computational argument currently unverifiable. In debate, the advocate argued these are revision-tractable wording/deposition fixes; the skeptic countered — with direct citations to the rigor and methodology reports — that disentangling the mechanism requires new data (single-molecule nucleotide tracking, stopped-flow off-rate measurement, or a mutant blocking interior exchange), not merely softened language. This rebuttal was not answered on its substance and is the more persuasive reading of the record.

## Decisive Factors
The confidence-weighted average (3.79/5) and the verdict count (4 minor, 3 major, 1 accept) sit close to the minor/major boundary, but the exact three reviewers recommending major (methodology, reproducibility, rigor) are also the three most directly interrogating the paper's title-bearing, most novel claim (intrafilament exchange as a causal driver of stability) — this is the paper's central claim under the journal's own priority ranking (evidence-claim fit outweighs everything else), not a peripheral issue. The debate sharpened rather than resolved this: the advocate's framing of the gap as revision-by-wording did not survive contact with the skeptic's specific citation of the rigor reviewer's point that the model reproduces the data 'without requiring any intrafilament exchange at all,' and the methodology reviewer's point that the capping experiment is equally consistent with an ADP-end/nucleotide-free-interior alternative. Combined with reproducibility's explicit 'HARD reproducibility failure' on the undeposited, load-bearing Monte Carlo code — a criterion (checkability) the journal weights above novelty — this tips the recommendation to major revision rather than the softer minor-revision lean implied by a simple confidence-weighted average. This is not a rejection: no reviewer disputes the hydrolysis-independent-polymerization finding, which is itself a defensible, citable result, and the fixes requested (deposit the model code and parameters now, quantify FRAP recovery kinetics against hydrolysis/exchange timescales, add a genuinely dissociative control such as single-molecule tracking or stopped-flow off-rate measurement, cite Pande et al. 2022 and address its bearing on novelty, and rescale the abstract's causal/'new class' language to what the data presented actually establish) are concrete and addressable without abandoning the paper's core findings.
