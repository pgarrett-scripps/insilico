# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript describes biophysical and cell biological studies of MreB polymerization dynamics using multiple imaging modalities (TIRF, HS-AFM), biochemical assays (fluorescence anisotropy, ATPase activity, liposome binding), and computational modeling. Detected categories include Chemicals/Drugs/Dosing, Oligos/Plasmids/Constructs, Microscopy/Imaging, Computational/ML/Modeling, and Protocol Provenance. Most core reagents and methods are traceable; however, several protocol delegations to prior work are unverifiable from the manuscript alone, and some computational modeling details lack sufficient specification. Sample sizes are generally stated, statistical tests are named, and software versions are provided for most tools. A data-availability statement is present but defers to post-publication deposition.
