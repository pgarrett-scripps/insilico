# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript presents a clear, well-structured discovery of intrafilament nucleotide exchange in MreB filaments — a novel biopolymer behavior distinct from eukaryotic actin. The central claims are supported by multiple complementary approaches (TIRF, HS-AFM, biochemistry, modeling, in vivo). Presentation is generally excellent, but a few specific ambiguities in figure labeling, terminology consistency, and method details create friction for readers trying to verify the evidence.

## Strengths
- Multiple orthogonal methods (TIRF, HS-AFM, biochemistry, modeling, in vivo) converge on the same mechanistic conclusions, making the central claim robust.
- The narrative flow from in vitro reconstitution to mechanistic dissection to in vivo validation is logical and well-signposted.
- Quantitative parameters are reported with units and compared directly to actin in Table 1, enabling immediate contextualization.

## Weaknesses
- Load-bearing claim 1 (intrafilament nucleotide exchange): The FRAP experiment in Figure 3F uses ATP* incorporated during polymerization (17:1 ATP:ATP*), then photobleaches and monitors recovery. The text states this shows 'nucleotide exchange between the filaments and the solution' (line 232), but recovery could also reflect subunit exchange at filament ends or annealing of new ATP*-labeled subunits. The critical control — preformed filaments exposed to pure ATP* (Figure 3H) — is presented later and does show homogeneous incorporation along the length, but the logical order in the text (Fig 3F before 3H) leaves a temporary ambiguity about what Fig 3F alone proves. A reader cannot determine from Fig 3F alone whether recovery is intrafilament exchange or end-wise addition/annealing.
- Load-bearing claim 3 (ADP-induced depolymerization mechanism): The capping experiment (Fig 4G-H) uses E136A labeled with both ATP* and TFCoral added to WT filaments labeled with TFCoral. The text says 'filaments no longer underwent rapid (< 1 min) end-wise depolymerization. Instead, after less than 1 min, most filaments started breaking in the WT section or near one cap and depolymerized, leaving only the E136A caps' (lines 303-305). However, Figure 4H shows only two example kymographs; the quantification in Figure 4F (referenced in line 302) is not clearly described — it says 'Quantification of the effect of the ADP flush on preformed filaments' but the panel shows three bars (WT, E136A, D158A) without explaining what the bars represent (disassembly rate? fraction remaining? breakage frequency?). A reader cannot determine what metric Figure 4F quantifies or how it supports the claim that capping prevents end-wise depolymerization.
- Terminology inconsistency: The manuscript uses 'filament' to mean a doublet (pair of protofilaments) (line 52: 'Throughout this work, a 'filament' refers to one doublet'), but Figure 1 legend says 'pairs of protofilaments' and Figure 2A legend says 'MreB doublets'. In Figure 3E-F, 'single filaments' are analyzed by FRAP, but it's unclear whether these are single doublets or bundles. The intensity histogram in Figure S3E shows 'a dominant population of uniform intensity, and additional minor populations of two- and three-fold higher intensities, consistent with bundles of double and triple filaments' — but the main text never explicitly states that the 'single filaments' in FRAP are confirmed doublets, creating ambiguity about the structural unit undergoing nucleotide exchange.
- Methods readability gap: The Monte Carlo model parameters in Figure 4D legend include rates like k_hyd = k_Pir = 1.54e-3 s^-1 and k_+n = 0.28 µM^-1 s^-1, but the text does not explain how these were derived from experiments. The Supplementary Information 'Modeling MreB dynamics' is referenced but not provided in the manuscript; a reader cannot evaluate whether the model is constrained by data or fits it by construction. The main text should at minimum state which parameters were measured directly vs. fitted.
- Figure 2E: The elongation rate vs. concentration plot shows a plateau above 0.06-0.09 µM. The text attributes this to 2D membrane diffusion limitation (lines 165-170) but does not report the actual plateau value or compare it numerically to the HS-AFM rate (14.4 nm/s). The HS-AFM rate is from 1.2 µM MreB (line 142), while TIRF plateau starts at 0.06 µM — the text says they are 'consistent' but gives no numbers for the TIRF plateau. A reader cannot verify this consistency claim.
- Abstract line 12: 'continuous nucleotide exchange within filaments tunes their stability on the membrane' — the term 'tunes' is vague; the manuscript shows ATP stabilizes and ADP destabilizes, which is more specific than 'tunes'. The abstract should use the directional language the data support.

## Questions
- Figure 3F FRAP: What was the mobile fraction and half-time of recovery for ATP*? The legend says 'Standard deviation bars on top only' but no quantitative FRAP parameters are reported in the text or figure. This is needed to compare exchange kinetics to hydrolysis rates.
- Figure 4F: What do the three bars represent? The legend says 'Quantification of the effect of the ADP flush on preformed filaments' but does not define the y-axis metric. Is it disassembly rate, time to complete disassembly, or fraction of filaments breaking vs. depolymerizing?
- Monte Carlo model: Which parameters in Figure 4D legend were directly measured (e.g., from TIRF elongation rates, ATPase assays, anisotropy) and which were fitted to match the disassembly curves? The manuscript should state this explicitly in the main text.
- Line 173: 'One end occasionally elongated slightly faster than the other. This weak apparent polarity likely results from crowding...' — was this quantified? What fraction of filaments showed asymmetry, and what was the magnitude? The phrase 'occasionally' is unquantified.
- Line 228: 'D158A filaments were not visible, likely because of the reduced nucleotide binding affinity... together with the lower efficiency of ATP* over ATP for polymerization' — if D158A filaments are not visible under standard ATP* labeling, how were the D158A data in Figure 3H-I obtained? The text says 'When preformed pFAST-MreB filaments labelled with TFCoral were exposed to pure ATP* instead, homogeneous binding of ATP* was observed along the length of both WT and D158A filaments' — but how were D158A filaments formed and visualized initially if they're not visible with ATP*? The method for forming D158A filaments for the pure ATP* experiment is not described.
