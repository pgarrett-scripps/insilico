# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript presents a comprehensive experimental and computational study of MreB polymerization dynamics on lipid membranes, with clear ethical compliance. No human subjects, animal research, clinical trials, dual-use risks, or restricted-consent data are involved. Funding sources are disclosed and competing interests are explicitly stated as none. All required compliance statements are present and properly documented.

## Strengths
- Funding sources are clearly listed and competing interests are explicitly declared as none in the manuscript.
- All experimental work involves recombinant bacterial proteins and lipid bilayers with no human subjects, animal models, or restricted biological materials requiring additional ethical oversight.
- Materials and methods are sufficiently detailed to enable verification and reproduction, with plasmids, strains, and constructs documented in supplementary tables and availability stated through corresponding authors.
