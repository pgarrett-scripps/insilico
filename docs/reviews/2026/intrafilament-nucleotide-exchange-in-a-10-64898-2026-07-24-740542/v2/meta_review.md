# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** minor

## Synthesis
The panel converges on a well-executed, multi-method biophysical study of MreB filament dynamics, with genuine agreement that ATP-hydrolysis-independent polymerization (E136A/D158A behaving like WT) is convincingly established (clarity, data_analysis, methodology, novelty, rigor, reproducibility all treat this as solid). Ethics is a clean pass (5/5). Scores cluster tightly in the 3-5 range (confidence-weighted 3.91/5), with reproducibility (3/4) and rigor (3/4) as the two lower marks, both flagging the same underlying issue from independent angles. The debate sharpened rather than resolved one genuine tension: three reviewers (methodology, rigor, reproducibility) independently and convergently identified that the paper's central novelty claim - intrafilament (not end- or diffusion-mediated) nucleotide exchange - is not fully discriminated from an alternative in which subunits transiently dissociate and rebind via 2D membrane diffusion, a mechanism the authors themselves invoke elsewhere in the manuscript to explain the elongation-rate plateau. The advocate's best counter (homogeneous ATP* incorporation in Fig. 3H, capping experiment in Fig. 4G-H) survived partially: the skeptic correctly noted that homogeneous incorporation is equally compatible with lateral-diffusion-mediated rebinding, so this critique was not closed by debate, only narrowed to 'plausible but not proven.' A second, separable critique - that the Monte Carlo model is presented only in prose, with ~11 fitted parameters, no equations, code, sensitivity analysis, or goodness-of-fit metric (reproducibility) - also survived the debate essentially intact; the advocate's reply (shared parameter set validated across genotypes/conditions) mitigates but does not resolve the overfitting/unverifiability concern. Literature review finds citations substantially accurate but flags that the 'first report of nucleotide cycling' framing needs narrowing relative to ParM precedent, which reinforces rather than duplicates the mechanistic ambiguity above. Reproducibility also raises independent, unresolved disclosure gaps: undeposited raw data and code, unspecified TSOAX tracking parameters, and unquantified residual ATPase activity in the 'ATPase-dead' mutants (Fig. 3A) - any of which is a concrete, actionable fix rather than a challenge to the core finding.

## Decisive Factors
No reviewer recommended rejection, and the confidence-weighted average (3.91/5) together with the verdict distribution (1 accept, 5 minor, 2 major, 0 reject) sits squarely in the 'minor revision' band; I see no manuscript-specific reason the panel missed that would justify going below their collective judgment. I therefore recommend minor revision, consistent with the numerical signal, rather than major revision or reject. The two most defensible reasons to lean toward 'major' instead - the unresolved exchange-vs-diffusion-rebinding ambiguity underlying the paper's headline novelty claim, and the unverifiable, prose-only Monte Carlo model - are both real and survived the debate, but neither is fatal to the paper's overall soundness: the debate established that the exchange interpretation is well-motivated and consistent with converging (if imperfect) evidence, and the model criticism is a verifiability/disclosure gap rather than evidence the model is wrong. Both are resolvable by concrete revisions the reviewers already specified (position-resolved FRAP or direct nucleotide-composition assay to discriminate exchange from diffusion-rebinding; deposited code/equations with sensitivity and goodness-of-fit analysis for the model; narrowed novelty framing relative to ParM; quantified residual mutant ATPase activity; deposited raw data with accession numbers; specified TSOAX parameters). None of these require new conceptual work or overturn the conceded, load-bearing finding that hydrolysis is dispensable for polymerization. Given In Silico's standard that admitted, fixable limitations should not be penalized as if they were new flaws, and that the venue rewards candor, this profile matches minor revision: the authors should soften the 'first report' claim to 'consistent with' pending confirmatory experiments, and address the disclosure gaps, but the core contribution stands on the evidence presented.
