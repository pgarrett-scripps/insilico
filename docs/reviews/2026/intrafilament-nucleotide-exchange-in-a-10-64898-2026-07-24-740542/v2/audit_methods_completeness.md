# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript describes biophysical and cell biological studies of MreB polymerization dynamics using multiple imaging modalities (TIRF, HS-AFM), biochemical assays (fluorescence anisotropy, ATPase activity, liposome binding), and computational modeling. Detected categories include Chemicals/Drugs, Plasmids/Constructs, Model Organisms (in vivo imaging), Microscopy/Imaging, Computational/Modeling, and Protocol Provenance. Most core methods are self-contained or resolvable; however, several critical protocol references are delegated to citations without full verification, and some experimental details (sample sizes, statistical tests, software versions) are incompletely specified. Reagent traceability is generally good for commercial items but incomplete for some custom constructs and in vivo strains.
