# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This is an original methodological research manuscript describing a rapid multi-protease workflow (RIPUP) for histone PTM analysis by mass spectrometry. It falls squarely within In Silico's scope: the claims are empirical and checkable, the methods are described in detail, and the supporting evidence (raw MS data deposited to ProteomeXchange/PRIDE under PXD073683, custom analysis scripts on GitHub) is accessible to readers. It is not a clinical trial, diagnostic or treatment guidance, or a non-research submission. No fundamental flaw is evident on its face that would warrant short-circuiting the review process; the manuscript is complete, coherent, and presents a substantial body of data with clearly stated limitations. The manuscript should proceed to full review, where the panel can assess the strength of the evidence and the soundness of the quantitative and statistical analyses.
