# Decision Letter

**Decision:** minor

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel is unusually convergent: all eight specialists rated the manuscript 4/5 (ethics 5/5) with high confidence, yielding 4.15/5 confidence-weighted and a 1 accept / 7 minor distribution. The core contribution — a systematic, fast dual-protease histone workflow (RIPUP) benchmarked across 10 conditions and 40 samples, with quantified labeling efficiency, documented coverage complementarity, median CVs <5–7%, and a properly analysed limma/BH NAM dose–response — is not disputed by any reviewer and is supported by strong deposition (PXD073683, GitHub) and exemplary animal-ethics compliance (IACUC #09-0006, ARRIVE).

The one substantive HARD criticism, raised independently by the rigor, methodology, novelty and clarity reviewers, is a scope/attribution problem rather than a data problem: the 'dark epigenome' framing of the 58 succinylation / 31 glutarylation sites asserts biological prevalence that a comparison against a single, self-acknowledged suboptimal Garcia-type propionylation protocol in one cell line cannot support, and the design cannot separate 'TMT detects sites propionylation misses' from 'both detect the same sites at different ionization efficiency.' I weighed the rebuttal carefully here. The authors concede this point fully and propose precisely the correct remedy — reframing the finding as enhanced detection relative to the widely used comparator, stating the ionization-efficiency confound explicitly, and confining prevalence language to HEK293T. Critically, the Discussion already contains the caveat verbatim ('should therefore be interpreted as reflecting performance relative to the widely used protocol rather than against the best achievable propionylation efficiency'), so aligning the Abstract and Results with the authors' own stated position is an editing task, not an experimental one. I therefore decline the skeptic's call for major revision: no proposed new experiment is needed to make the retained claims true, only to support a broader claim the authors are relinquishing. Orthogonal PRM/synthetic-peptide validation is legitimate future work for a methods paper.

I also accept two of the authors' three disagreements. The n=5 rats are, on the manuscript's own Methods text, independent biological replicates (separate animals, separate dissections) with a ≥2/5 consistency filter; the reviewer's 'technical replicate' characterisation appears to be a misreading, and I do not require action beyond ensuring the proof-of-concept framing is explicit. The novelty delta is likewise adequately articulated: the authors do not claim Arg-C Ultra kinetics or HiP-Frag as their own, and the labeling-chemistry × PTM-class-detection axis is a genuine advance over Ryzhaya et al. and Vai et al. The TMA-exclusion justification is nonetheless owed to readers.

The compliance audits add no grounds for rejection. Citation integrity returned zero HARD gaps; the Whedon and Paizs/Steen items are conceded and easily fixed, and the two unverifiable items become author-confirmation questions. The Methods audit's five HARD gaps (cell-line provenance/authentication, blinding/randomization statement, NAM vehicle and final concentration, limma parameter specification, kNN seed) are all documentation items that must be added but do not impede evaluation of the central claims. Accordingly: minor revision.

## Required Revisions
1. Reframe the acidic-acylation finding throughout the Abstract, Results heading ('Enhanced identification of histone succinylation and glutarylation with TMT') and Discussion: replace 'dark epigenome' with language describing enhanced detection relative to the widely used Garcia-type propionylation protocol, and add an explicit sentence stating that the design cannot distinguish detection of sites missed by propionylation from differential ionization efficiency of the same sites. Confine all prevalence language explicitly to HEK293T cells.
2. State, for the 58 succinylation and 31 glutarylation sites, whether each is previously reported in the literature or novel (a supplementary table cross-referencing existing databases is sufficient), and report their replicate-level detection frequency and site-localization confidence scores.
3. Change causal phrasing on the charge-compensation mechanism (e.g. 'is explained by') to 'is consistent with' throughout Results and Discussion.
4. Either add a Fisher's exact or permutation test with multiple-testing correction for the missed-cleavage motif enrichment (SI Figures S6–S7) and describe the tool/algorithm/parameters used, or explicitly hedge the claim to 'consistent with enrichment of D/E near missed cleavage sites'.
5. Resolve the citation issues: verify and correct or remove the Whedon et al. (2024) citation for TMT-based histone middle-down analysis; attribute the mobile proton model to Paizs & Suhai (2005) and cite Steen & Mann (2004) separately for fragment-ion nomenclature; and add one sentence justifying the exclusion of TMA (Ryzhaya et al. 2025) from the comparison, noting a future head-to-head as valuable.
6. Close the Methods reproducibility gaps: (a) deposit the complete FragPipe params.txt/workflow file with the PRIDE submission and enumerate in Methods the specific deviations from the published HiP-Frag workflow, including precursor/fragment search tolerances and the full variable-modification list; (b) state the kNN imputation rationale for k=10, the random seed, and any sensitivity analysis; (c) specify limma parameters (variance-stabilization approach, robust/trend settings, contrast specification) and package versions for limma and impute; (d) document the artifact-stripping and canonical-mapping decision rules with an example table; (e) add hippocampal digestion details (histone input mass, propionylation status).
7. Add the missing reagent and model reporting items flagged by the methods audit: HEK293T source vendor, RRID, STR authentication and mycoplasma-testing status; NAM vehicle and final in-media concentration; a rat RRID; and an explicit statement on randomization and blinding (or its absence) for the animal work.
8. Add a paragraph to the Limitations section discussing the 2–3-fold higher artifact rates (Met oxidation, dehydration) observed under TMT labeling and their potential impact on PTM localization confidence and quantification.
9. Add Competing Interests, CRediT author contribution, and AI-tool-use disclosure statements.
10. Define 'informative histone peptides (IHP)' and 'peptidoform' at first use in the main text, and add 2–3 sentences in Results summarizing the histone-level normalization logic before Figure 7.

## Minor Suggestions
- Confirm that the specific numbers attributed to Vai et al. (2025) (60 core histone, 13 linker histone modifications) match the published paper, and adjust if not.
- Ensure the SI Methods (histone extraction, SDS-PAGE, FragPipe search settings) are submitted with the revision so reviewers can verify the delegated protocols.
- Consider stating explicitly in the rat hippocampus section that n=5 animals are independent biological replicates and that the experiment is a proof-of-concept for workflow speed/coverage rather than a biological discovery study — this pre-empts a misreading that arose during review.
- Note orthogonal validation (PRM with synthetic peptides, or antibody-based confirmation) of the acidic-acylation sites as explicitly designated future work in the Discussion.
