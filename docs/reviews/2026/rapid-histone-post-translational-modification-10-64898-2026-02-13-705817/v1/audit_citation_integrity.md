# Citation Integrity Auditor

This audit checked reference resolvability, claim–citation support, and quotation fidelity for load-bearing citations across a representative sample of the manuscript's 73 references. ## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Claim–citation support] Vai et al. 2025 (Ref. 20) – novel PTM discovery numbers (60 core, 13 linker histone modifications)** — Citation is to Vai A et al., *Molecular & Cellular Proteomics* 2025, 24(11):101080. The specific numbers (60 core histone, 13 linker histone modifications) cannot be confirmed from the abstract alone. The manuscript relies heavily on HiP-Frag framework and claims about its discoveries. Recommend author confirmation that these numbers match the published paper.
- **[Claim–citation support] Paizs & Suhai 2005 (Ref. 26) – mobile proton model of peptide fragmentation** — Citation is to Paizs B & Suhai S, *Mass Spectrometry Reviews* 2005, 24(4):508–548. The specific mechanism described (mobile proton model) is attributed to this reference, but the exact formulation and whether it is the primary source for this model cannot be confirmed from search results. This is a foundational claim for the TMT charge compensation mechanism. Recommend author confirmation.
