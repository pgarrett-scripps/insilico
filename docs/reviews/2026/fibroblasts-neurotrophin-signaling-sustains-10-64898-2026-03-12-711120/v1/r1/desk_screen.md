# Desk Screen Report

## Submission Assessment for In Silico

### Overview
This manuscript investigates neurotrophin signaling in fibroblasts and its role in pathological vascular maturation in rheumatoid arthritis (RA). The work combines spatial transcriptomics, cell culture, and pharmacological approaches.

### Critical Issues

**1. Manuscript Completeness and Presentation**

The submission is severely compromised by formatting and structural problems:

- The document appears corrupted or improperly converted, with fragmented text, misaligned figures, and incomplete figure legends throughout
- Section headers are present but content is often missing or unintelligible (e.g., "Materials and Methods" contains only subsection titles with no actual methods)
- Figure captions are incomplete or truncated (e.g., "Fig. S2" caption cuts off mid-sentence)
- Multiple figures lack proper numbering or appear out of sequence
- Tables referenced in text (Table 1, Table 2) are listed but not provided with content
- The "Results" section heading is present but the actual results narrative is largely absent or embedded within figure descriptions

**2. Scope Compatibility**

The work is *in scope* for In Silico: it is original empirical research with testable claims, combining human tissue analysis with mechanistic cell culture studies and pharmacological validation. The claims are appropriately scaled to the evidence type.

**3. Fundamental Soundness**

The experimental design appears reasonable where discernible:
- Spatial transcriptomics on human RA synovial tissue
- Co-culture systems with endothelial cells and fibroblasts
- Genetic knockdown and overexpression approaches
- Pharmacological inhibitor testing

However, the manuscript's current state prevents adequate evaluation of:
- Complete methodological detail
- Full results and statistical analyses
- Proper figure interpretation
- Data availability and reproducibility statements

### Verdict

The manuscript addresses a legitimate scientific question with potentially sound methodology, but **cannot be evaluated in its current form**. The presentation is too compromised to send to reviewers responsibly. This appears to be a file conversion or submission error rather than a fundamental scientific flaw.

---

**DESK DECISION: reject**

**Rationale for Authors:**

This manuscript cannot proceed to review in its current state due to severe formatting and completeness issues. The document appears corrupted or improperly converted, with fragmented text, incomplete figure legends, missing methods sections, and truncated captions throughout. While the scientific premise is sound and in scope, reviewers cannot adequately evaluate the work without:

1. A complete, properly formatted manuscript with intact Results section
2. Full figure legends and properly numbered figures
3. Complete Materials and Methods with sufficient procedural detail
4. Accessible tables and supplementary information

**Recommendation:** Please resubmit after correcting the file formatting and ensuring all sections are complete and legible. Verify the conversion from your source document (likely Word or LaTeX) has preserved all text and figure placement.
