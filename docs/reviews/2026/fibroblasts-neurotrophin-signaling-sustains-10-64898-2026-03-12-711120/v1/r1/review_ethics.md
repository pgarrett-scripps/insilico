## ETHICS & COMPLIANCE ASSESSMENT

This manuscript involves human synovial tissue samples and requires evaluation of ethics compliance.

**Human subjects and identifiable data:**

The manuscript states that "Human synovial tissue samples were obtained from Brigham and Women's Hospital (MGB IRB no. 2019P002924) and Flinders Medical Center (Protocol#396.10)." IRB approval is named with committee identifiers and protocol numbers for both sites. The authors note that tissue was collected from patients undergoing arthroplasty or synovectomy procedures and from healthy donors. No identifiable data are presented in the manuscript or figures. This category is adequately addressed.

**Competing interests and funding:**

The manuscript discloses funding sources: "This work is supported by a NIH NIAMS R01AR085028, Arthritis Foundation Rheumatoid Arthritis Research Program, a Burroughs Wellcome Fund Career Awards for Medical Scientists to K.W., a Brigham and Women's Hospital Department of Medicine - Broad Institution collaborative research Award, a Brigham and Women's Hospital Llura Gund Award for Rheumatoid Arthritis Research. K.W. is supported by an NIH-NIAMS K08AR077037." No competing interests statement is provided. This is a HARD gap: the authors must explicitly state whether competing interests exist or declare none.

**Summary:**

IRB approval is properly documented with identifiers. Funding is fully disclosed. A competing interests declaration is missing and must be added.
