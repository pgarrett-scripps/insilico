# Journal Recommendations

## As-is (current quality)
- **bioRxiv**
  - Fit: The manuscript is already posted as a preprint, which is the appropriate home for the current, unrevised version: a large curated mutation atlas with mechanistic framing that the panel judged unsupported.
  - Realism: Preprint servers apply only basic screening, so posting is essentially assured, but this confers no peer-reviewed status.

## After required revisions
- **Tuberculosis (Elsevier)**
  - Fit: A dedicated Mtb journal that routinely publishes mutation catalogues, drug-resistance surveys and in silico structural analyses of katG, rpoB, pncA and embB; the reframed hypothesis-generating atlas fits its remit squarely.
  - Realism: Realistic after a thoroughgoing reframing, deposited data/code and the required statistical testing, though reviewers there will know the resistance-catalogue literature well and will press on novelty versus WHO's catalogue.
- **PLOS ONE**
  - Fit: Judges soundness rather than impact, which suits a descriptive resource paper built on multi-tool bioinformatics triangulation, and it enforces exactly the data/code availability and methods-completeness items the editor requires.
  - Realism: Good odds once the mechanistic-selection claims are removed and the ranking algorithm is made auditable; the main risk is reviewers objecting to unvalidated in silico rankings if any predictive claim survives.
- **BMC Infectious Diseases**
  - Fit: Publishes systematic literature-derived surveys of drug-resistance mutation prevalence with regional (WHO-region) breakdowns, matching the atlas and epidemiological components of the paper.
  - Realism: Plausible if the paper is presented primarily as a systematic-review-style resource with proper CIs, denominators and de-duplication disclosure; the docking/structural component would need to be clearly secondary and hedged.

## Alternative outlets
- **Infection, Genetics and Evolution**
  - Fit: Specialty outlet for pathogen mutation, molecular epidemiology and evolution studies; would accommodate the global mutation-spread analysis and conservation/site classification even in a narrower form.
  - Realism: A reasonable fallback, but editors there are attuned to selection-versus-ascertainment arguments and will reject any residual 'guided evolution' framing.
- **Journal of Biomolecular Structure and Dynamics**
  - Fit: Routinely publishes homology modelling plus AutoDock studies of resistance mutations in bacterial drug targets, which is the methodological core of the structural half of this work.
  - Realism: Achievable if the paper is repackaged around the structural/docking analysis with redocking validation, though this would mean discarding much of the epidemiological atlas.
- **Data in Brief**
  - Fit: An outlet for the curated 821-mutation, 31,073-isolate dataset itself, deposited under a DOI with full curation methods, decoupled from the contested mechanistic interpretation.
  - Realism: High likelihood of acceptance for the dataset alone, but it publishes no inferential claims and would need to accompany or follow a research article.

## Notes
The decisive obstacle is framing, not topic: any venue will reject the 'universal survival strategy' claim given the survivorship-bias critique, so the reframing to a hypothesis-generating resource must precede submission anywhere. Also note that overlap with the WHO mutation catalogue and TB-Profiler-era WGS work will be the first question at every TB-specialist venue.
