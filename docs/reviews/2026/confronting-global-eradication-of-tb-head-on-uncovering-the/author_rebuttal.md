## Concessions
- **methodology / data_analysis / reproducibility / rigor** — The lethal/moderate/mild/neutral ranking is never operationally defined; 235 of 821 mutations have undisclosed classification logic.
  - Proposed change: We will replace the prose description in Section 3.6.2.5 with an explicit decision tree (new Figure 13 plus pseudocode in Methods) specifying: (i) the exact input from each of the five dimensions (ConSurf bin, site 1/2/3, SIFT/PROVEAN/PolyPhen-2 majority call, I-MUTANT+mCSM stability concordance, ΔΔG_binding sign and magnitude); (ii) the majority rule applied when tools disagree (2-of-3 for function; agreement required between I-MUTANT and mCSM for stability, otherwise 'indeterminate'); (iii) how each of the 821 mutations, including the 235 with discordant functional calls, maps to a rank. A new Supplementary Table S12 will list every mutation with all five raw inputs and the resulting rank, so Table 2's counts can be reconstructed line by line. We will also add a sensitivity analysis reporting how the 340/284/185/12 counts change under alternative thresholds (ConSurf cutoff 6 vs 7; unanimous vs majority functional concordance) and report Fleiss' kappa across SIFT/PROVEAN/PolyPhen-2.
- **rigor / methodology** — Ascertainment/survivorship bias: the corpus contains only viable, phenotypically resistant isolates, so 'prevalent mutations are mild' is partly entailed by the sampling frame.
  - Proposed change: We accept this as a design-level limitation that we did not adequately confront. We will (i) add a dedicated Limitations subsection stating explicitly that the dataset is conditioned on survival and detected resistance, and that the observed enrichment of mild mutations among prevalent variants is therefore consistent with both active selection and survivorship filtering, which this design cannot distinguish; (ii) remove 'universal strategy', 'guided evolutionary pathway', 'Mtb favours/prefers/chooses', and 'uncovering the root' from the title, abstract, results, and discussion, replacing them with correlational phrasing ('prevalent resistance mutations are predominantly located in non-conserved, binding-proximal positions and are predicted to have mild-to-moderate effects'); (iii) reframe the paper's stated contribution as a hypothesis-generating atlas plus in silico characterisation rather than mechanistic discovery.
- **data_analysis** — No statistical testing of the central hypothesis, no CIs, no heterogeneity test on WHO-region comparisons, no multiple-comparison control.
  - Proposed change: We will add: (i) a chi-square test of independence between mutation-frequency tertile (high/medium/low) and conservation category (HC/C/V), with effect size and 95% CI, directly testing the 'frequent mutations are in non-conserved regions' claim; (ii) a chi-square test for heterogeneity of mutation-category distribution across the six WHO regions, with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control across all regional pairwise comparisons; (iii) Wilson 95% CIs on every reported single-mutation frequency in Table 1 and Supplementary Table S3; (iv) a Spearman correlation (rho, p) between per-position mutation count and number of distinct AAS, replacing the current qualitative claim in Section 3.4.
- **reproducibility / ethics** — No public data or code deposition; no funding, COI, or AI-use statements.
  - Proposed change: We will deposit the curated mutation dataset (Supplementary Tables S1, S3, S5-S12), all 603 mutant PDB models, ConSurf/SIFT/PROVEAN/PolyPhen-2/I-MUTANT/mCSM raw outputs, and AutoDock grid parameter files and docking logs on Zenodo under CC-BY with a citable DOI, and the aggregation/frequency/ranking scripts on GitHub with a tagged release archived to the same DOI. We will add Funding, Competing Interests ('none declared'), Data and Code Availability, and AI-tool-use statements before the References.
- **reproducibility** — Docking and modelling parameters incomplete (grid centring, conformer selection, run replication, GROMACS convergence, MODELLER selection criteria).
  - Proposed change: Methods will be expanded to state: grid box centred on the COACH-D consensus ligand-binding centroid of the corresponding wild-type structure and held identical across all mutants of a given target; 50 independent GA runs per docking with the lowest-energy pose of the most populated cluster reported; RMSD-clustering threshold 2.0 A; random seeds recorded and deposited; GROMACS steepest-descent minimisation to Fmax < 1000 kJ/mol/nm with maximum 50,000 steps; MODELLER loop refinement level and the explicit molpdf/DOPE/GA341 selection thresholds. Where crystal structures exist (katG-INH 1SJ2, pncA 3PL1), we will report redocking RMSD as a docking validation control.
- **methodology / novelty** — ~226 emb mutations are ranked despite absent structural models for embA/embB and only the embC C-terminal domain being available.
  - Proposed change: All emb mutations lacking a validated structural model will be explicitly flagged 'structurally uncertain' in Table 2 and Supplementary Tables S8-S12, their stability and docking-derived rank components will be reported as missing rather than imputed, and Table 2 will be presented both with and without emb mutations so that no conclusion depends on them. The limitation will be moved from the Methods subsection into the Results and the Limitations section.
- **novelty / literature** — 'First comprehensive study' claim is not differentiated from Portelli et al. (2018); 2020-2025 WGS/ML resistance-prediction literature is absent.
  - Proposed change: We will delete the unqualified 'first' claim and add a Related Work subsection with a comparison table against Portelli et al. ## Disagreements
- **rigor** — The claim 'All mutations reduced drug binding affinity' is unsupported.
  - Response: We agree the abstract sentence must be hedged as a docking prediction rather than a measurement, and we commit to that change. We disagree, however, that the underlying comparison was not made: wild-type reference energies are reported in the text for every target (RIF/rpoB -8.23, INH/katG -5.53, PZA/pncA -4.73, EMB/embC -4.03 kcal/mol) and each mutant value in Fig. 12 and Table 1 is less negative than its wild-type reference. The revision will state the wild-type baselines in Table 1 itself and will explicitly caveat that differences of ~0.1-0.5 kcal/mol lie within typical AutoDock uncertainty and should not be interpreted as rank-ordering individual mutants.
  - Manuscript reference: Section 'Mutations in drug binding site cause the most damage to drug binding' and Fig. 12
- **data_analysis** — No power analysis justifies the claim that the 30-year data reveal 'globally realistic patterns'.
  - Response: A conventional power calculation is not well defined here because the study is not a designed comparison with a pre-specified effect size; it is a census of all mutation records meeting the inclusion criteria in the published literature. We will remove the phrase 'globally realistic patterns' as unsupported, report per-region isolate counts alongside Fig. 6 so readers can judge precision directly, and add Wilson CIs so under-sampled regions (Eastern Mediterranean, Africa for pncA) are visibly imprecise. We do not think an artificial post-hoc power statement would add information beyond those CIs.
  - Manuscript reference: Section 3.3, closing sentence: 'Considering our objective of probing into drug resistance mechanisms, the sample of 30-year mutation data could reveal globally realistic patterns.'
- **novelty** — The atlas offers no clear utility over existing databases (TB-Profiler, PhyResSE, TBDB).
  - Response: We will add the requested comparison, but we push back on the premise of equivalence. Those resources are genotype-to-phenotype variant catalogues; they do not carry, per mutation, the integrated five-dimension characterisation reported here (ConSurf conservation bin, domain membership, site-1/2/3 assignment relative to a predicted ligand-binding centroid, concordant function and stability calls, and a docking-derived ΔΔG). The revision will make this the explicit stated contribution and will offer the deposited tables in a format directly mergeable with those databases rather than competing with them.
  - Manuscript reference: Section 3.5 and Supplementary Table S3
- **data_analysis** — Data-handling decisions (duplicate isolates across studies, synonymous exclusion, weighting) are not disclosed.
  - Response: The study-level screening is fully documented, including duplicate-record removal (252 duplicates from 1,486 records) and the five inclusion criteria, and mutations were screened for non-synonymous status against H37Rv as reference in Phase 2. We accept that isolate-level duplication across independent publications and equal-weighting of isolates are not discussed, and we will add both to Methods with an explicit statement that isolate-level de-duplication across studies was not possible from published summary data, plus a sensitivity analysis excluding the largest study to show the frequency rankings are not driven by any single cohort.
  - Manuscript reference: Section 3.1 and Figure 4 (screening cascade); Fig. 3a Phase 2 ('screening of non-synonymous mutations')

## Load-bearing critiques
- Ascertainment/survivorship bias: because the corpus is conditioned on viable, phenotypically resistant isolates, the design cannot distinguish active selection for mild mutations from the absence of high-cost mutations in the sampling frame. This is not fixable by revision and invalidates the 'universal strategy'/'bacteria favour' framing; we can only remove those claims and reframe the paper as a descriptive, hypothesis-generating resource.
- No experimental or external validation of the lethal/moderate/mild/neutral ranking against measured fitness costs, MICs, or clinical outcomes. Formalising the algorithm makes the labels reproducible but not validated; if the editor requires the ranking to be demonstrated predictive, that cannot be delivered in this cycle.
