# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The manuscript presents the first chemoproteomic survey of the Trypanosoma cruzi serinome using fluorophosphonate activity-based protein profiling (FP-ABPP) in live epimastigotes, identifying 37 probe-enriched serine hydrolase-like proteins, 35 of which retain AlphaFold-validated catalytic geometry. This is a genuine, field-defining resource for Chagas disease target discovery. Two load-bearing critiques emerged from the panel: (1) absence of competitive ABPP to confirm active-site engagement, and (2) uncontrolled false discovery rate across seven probes. The authors' rebuttal credibly addresses both through claim modulation and computational re-analysis on existing data, without requiring new wet-lab experiments in this revision cycle. Specifically, they commit to: retitling from 'activity-based chemoproteomic map' to 'chemoproteomic survey of probe-enriched serine hydrolase candidates with in silico catalytic validation'; adding a Benjamini–Hochberg FDR sensitivity analysis using deposited R scripts (Zenodo) to quantify the false discovery proportion among the 37 proteins; and inserting an explicit limitation statement acknowledging that on-target engagement remains inferred. These concessions, combined with comprehensive fixes for all clarity, methods completeness, and reproducibility gaps identified by the panel and the editorial audits, resolve the fundamental concerns. The Methods Completeness audit revealed 16 HARD gaps (missing LC-MS/MS parameters, probe concentrations, cell line authentication, software versions, error-bar definitions, etc.) — all of which the authors have conceded to address in a consolidated Methods section and supplementary deposits. The Citation Integrity audit flagged seven unverifiable claim–citation links (WHO epidemiological statistics, PPI p-value attribution, homolog claims, copy-number assertions) that must be verified or corrected. The confidence-weighted panel score (3.79) and verdict distribution (1 accept, 4 minor, 3 major) support Major Revision. The authors' rebuttal demonstrates a clear path to a fully supported, reproducible resource paper. The decision is Major Revision with the mandatory requirements below.

## Required Revisions
1. Retitle the manuscript to reflect a 'chemoproteomic survey' rather than an 'activity-based chemoproteomic map' (e.g., 'Chemoproteomic Survey of the Trypanosoma cruzi Serinome by Fluorophosphonate Activity-Based Protein Profiling'). Update abstract, conclusions, and all framing accordingly.
2. Add a Benjamini–Hochberg FDR sensitivity analysis across all probe–protein tests using the deposited R scripts (Zenodo). Report the number of the 37 enriched proteins surviving FDR < 0.05 and the estimated false discovery proportion. Add a 'BH_adj_p' column to Supplementary Data 2-3 and describe the result in Results.
3. Insert an explicit Limitations paragraph in the Discussion stating that competitive ABPP (e.g., pre-treatment with broad-spectrum FP-biotin or PMSF) was not performed, so active-site-dependent labelling for each enriched protein remains inferred despite orthogonal support from AlphaFold catalytic geometry, FP-chemotype precedent, and Leishmania validation.
4. Create a consolidated Methods section with all subsections conceded in the rebuttal: 'Parasite culture and strain', 'FP-alkyne probe synthesis and characterization', 'Whole-cell ABPP labelling', 'Click chemistry and streptavidin enrichment', 'Sample preparation for LC-MS/MS', 'LC-MS/MS acquisition parameters', 'Proteomics data analysis', 'In silico serinome curation', 'Gene Ontology and PPI analysis'. Populate with every parameter currently missing per the Methods Completeness audit (16 HARD gaps): instrument model, acquisition mode (DDA/DIA), column, gradient, MS1/MS2 settings, search engine + version, database + version, FDR threshold, modifications, mass tolerances, lysis buffer composition, click chemistry reagent concentrations and incubation times, streptavidin bead amount, wash buffers, digestion enzyme and conditions, probe concentration/vehicle/schedule, cell line source/RRID/authentication/mycoplasma status, culture medium composition, software versions (R, limma, AlphaFold, Pfam/InterPro/CDD/Panther/MEROPS, STRING), random seed (set.seed(42)), error-bar definitions for all figures, and computational environment capture (conda environment.yml or Dockerfile deposited on Zenodo).
5. Generate a Dm28c→CL Brener orthologue mapping table (reciprocal best BLASTP, e-value < 1e-10, >80% identity) for all 37 enriched proteins. Deposit as Supplementary Data 6. Report mapping rate and exclude non-mapped proteins from STRING analysis in the revised text.
6. Fix all clarity defects conceded: abstract denominator (35/37 vs 35/56), Table 2 restructuring (move two non-enriched proteins to sub-table), Figure 4→Supplementary Figure S3 cross-reference, Figure 2 legend probe-class annotations (alkyl-FP 1–3, benzyl-FP 4, aryl-FP 5–7), Supplementary Data/Figure renumbering (Data 1–5, Figures S1–S3), copy-number columns per assembly (2018/2014/2017) in Table 2.
7. Resolve Reference 69 (Richter et al., Nat Commun 2026): replace with correct preprint DOI or update to actual publication year.
8. Verify or correct all unverifiable citation–claim links flagged by the Citation Integrity audit: (a) WHO/PAHO epidemiological statistics (6–8 million infected, 75 million at risk, 21 countries, 10,000 deaths/year) — confirm exact numbers in cited URLs or replace with PubMed-accessible WHO publications; (b) PPI enrichment p-value < 1×10⁻¹⁶ — reattribute to the authors' own STRING query, not to the STRING methods paper (ref 47); (c) lysophospholipase homologs in T. brucei/L. mexicana — provide correct citation or remove claim; (d) copy-number claims (e.g., lipase 17 copies, CPB1 12 copies) — ensure numbers are derivable from deposited genome assemblies and analysis scripts, or qualify as 'per our analysis of assemblies X, Y, Z'.
9. Deposit all Supplementary Data 1–5 and Supplementary Figures S1–S3 to the journal's supplementary system or a public repository (Zenodo) with DOIs. Update Data Availability Statement.
10. Deposit the 56 AlphaFold models with catalytic distance measurements (pLDDT, interatomic distances) on Zenodo with a DOI. Reference in Methods and Data Availability.
11. Provide genome assembly accession numbers for Dm28c 2014/2017/2018 in Methods (conceded: GCA_000209165.1, GCA_002183835.1, GCA_003709585.1).
12. Add IUPAC names or structural identifiers for all seven FP-alkyne probes to Figure 2 legend and/or a new Supplementary Table 1.

## Minor Suggestions
- Report culture medium composition (LIT medium supplements) in Methods.
- Report compute budget (CPU/GPU hours) for AlphaFold modelling and R workflows in Methods or a footnote.
- Consider adding a brief statement in the Discussion about stage-specific serinome dynamics (epimastigote vs. amastigote/trypomastigote) as a biological interpretation of the uncaptured 37%, referencing the Leishmania stage-dependent data (Isern et al., 2025).
- Ensure all figure legends define error bars (SD/SEM/CI) and replicate type (biological/technical).
- Add a sentence in Methods confirming that all R analyses used set.seed(42) for reproducibility (already conceded).
- Double-check that the Zenodo deposition for R scripts (doi:10.5281/zenodo.20626073) includes the FDR sensitivity analysis script and the conda environment file.
