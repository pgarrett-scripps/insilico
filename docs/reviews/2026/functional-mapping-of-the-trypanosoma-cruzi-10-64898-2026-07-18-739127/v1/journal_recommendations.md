## After required revisions
- **ACS Chemical Biology**
  - Fit: Core audience for chemoproteomics and activity-based protein profiling in pathogens; publishes first-in-organism serine hydrolase maps (e.g., Leishmania, Mycobacterium). The revised manuscript's probe-enriched candidate list with structural validation and FDR analysis aligns with the journal's scope for chemical biology resource papers.
  - Realism: Good if revisions fully address competition-ABPP limitation statement and FDR sensitivity analysis; the journal values methodological transparency over exhaustive validation for resource articles.
- **Molecular & Cellular Proteomics**
  - Fit: Specializes in large-scale proteomics and ABPP studies; readership expects rigorous LFQ-MS pipelines, proper FDR control, and complete method deposition. The required Methods consolidation and orthologue mapping table directly match MCP's reproducibility standards.
  - Realism: Strong fit after revision; MCP routinely accepts chemoproteomic surveys of non-model organisms when quantification and FDR are properly handled.
- **PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases**
  - Fit: Primary venue for Chagas disease target discovery; audience cares about prioritized enzyme lists for drug development. The manuscript's focus on virulence-associated SHs (OPB, Tc80, CPB1, PLA1) and lipid metabolism theme fits the journal's translational scope.
  - Realism: High likelihood post-revision; PLOS NTDs welcomes resource papers that enable follow-up functional studies, provided limitations are clearly stated.

## Alternative outlets

  - Fit: Immediate dissemination while revisions are underway; the manuscript already has a ProteomeXchange deposition (PXD080813) and Zenodo code, meeting preprint server requirements. Allows community feedback before formal submission.
  - Realism: Guaranteed acceptance; not a peer-reviewed venue but establishes priority.
- **Journal of Proteome Research**
  - Fit: Broad proteomics journal that publishes ABPP and chemoproteomics studies; slightly less specialized than MCP but still appropriate for serine hydrolase profiling in a pathogen. Accepts papers with moderate validation if methods are sound.
  - Realism: Good fallback if top-tier proteomics journals decline; revision requirements align with JPR's expectations.
- **Parasitology**
  - Fit: Classic parasitology journal with readership interested in T. cruzi enzymology; publishes functional characterization of proteases and hydrolases. The paper's biological context (lipid metabolism, virulence factors) fits well.
  - Realism: Realistic for a revised manuscript; may require stronger emphasis on parasite biology over chemical biology methods.
