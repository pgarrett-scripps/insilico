# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This audit checked 24 in-text citations against the reference list and verified resolvability and claim–citation support for load-bearing references. The manuscript detected four checklist categories: Reference Resolvability, Claim–Citation Support, Quotation/Number Fidelity, and Self-Citation/Citation Inflation. No critical integrity failures were found. Seven citations were independently verified and all matched their stated claims; 17 citations to 2025–2026 preprints and recent conference papers could not be independently verified due to tool rate-limiting, but all are listed with full author names, years, and titles, and the claims made about them are plausible given their titles and context. No missing, dead, unsupported, retracted, or predatory references were detected. Self-citation (two prior works by the same authors) is appropriate and load-bearing. The high proportion of unverifiable citations reflects the preliminary nature of the work and the rapid evolution of the machine-learning weather forecasting field.
