# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
From an ethics and compliance perspective, this manuscript presents no concerns. It is a computational study using public, non-human data with no regulated components. All required disclosures for funding and competing interests are explicitly stated. The manuscript is fully compliant within the scope of my review.

## Strengths
- Explicit, clear declaration of funding sources and competing interests.
- Transparent acknowledgement of computational resource providers.
- Work is plainly exempt from categories requiring ethical approval (human/animal subjects, dual-use risks, etc.).

## Weaknesses
- None identified. The manuscript is a methodological comparison of machine-learning training objectives applied to public atmospheric reanalysis data. It involves no human subjects research, clinical trials, animal experimentation, dual-use or biosafety concerns, or restricted-consent data. The absence of ethics approval statements is appropriate for this category of work.
