# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This manuscript presents a systematic comparison of different probabilistic scoring rules (CRPS, energy scores, graph-based variants, and spectral approaches) as training objectives for machine-learned weather forecasting models. The work is technically sound, addresses a relevant question about the sensitivity of forecast skill to loss function choice, and provides both empirical results and spectral analysis. While the scope is somewhat narrow and the experiments are conducted at reduced resolution with shorter training schedules (acknowledged limitations), the work makes a clear contribution to understanding scale-aware loss formulations in neural weather models. The paper is complete, the claims are appropriately scaled to the evidence, and the methodology is transparent. This is suitable for full review at In Silico.
