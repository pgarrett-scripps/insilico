# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript describes the development and cGMP manufacturing of N332-GT5 gp140 HIV vaccine candidate for first-in-human trials (HVTN144). From an ethics and compliance perspective, the work is well-positioned: it discloses funding sources, declares a relevant competing interest, provides clear authorship contributions, and appropriately references an active clinical trial. The work involves no human subjects research, animal studies, or dual-use pathogen work requiring additional ethics approvals beyond standard manufacturing oversight. The clinical trial itself (HVTN144) is prospectively registered and referenced, though the manuscript focuses on manufacturing rather than trial outcomes. No significant ethics or compliance gaps are identified.

## Strengths
- Funding sources explicitly disclosed: NIH CHAVD and Gates Foundation CAVD support clearly stated with award numbers and pass-through institutions.
- Competing interest declared: WRS's employment and shareholding at Moderna, Inc. is transparently reported.
- Authorship contributions clearly delineated by role (development, execution, analysis, structural work, glycan analysis, construct design, manuscript authorship), reducing risk of ghost or gift authorship.
- Clinical trial registration referenced: HVTN144 is identified with ClinicalTrials.gov identifier (NCT05217641), and the manuscript appropriately contextualizes the manufacturing work within the prospective trial framework.
- No human subjects research conducted in this manuscript: the work is manufacturing and analytical development; no human data, samples, or identifiable information are presented.
- No animal research: the manuscript references preclinical macaque studies (Steichen et al., 2024) but does not conduct new animal work; prior animal studies are cited appropriately.

## Weaknesses
- No explicit statement regarding data and code availability: the manuscript does not address whether raw data, analytical code, or process parameters will be made available to support reproducibility, nor does it discuss any restrictions based on proprietary or regulatory constraints.
- No acknowledgement of AI-tool use or non-author contributions: the manuscript does not state whether computational tools, AI-assisted writing, or other non-author contributions were used in manuscript preparation.

## Questions
- Will raw analytical data (e.g., LC-MS glycosylation profiles, viral clearance assay raw data, bioreactor fermentation logs) be made available in a repository or supplementary archive, and if not, what are the constraints (proprietary, regulatory, or otherwise)?
- Were any computational tools, machine-learning models, or AI-assisted writing systems used in data analysis, figure generation, or manuscript preparation? If so, please disclose their role and any relevant limitations.
- The manuscript references the HVTN144 trial (NCT05217641) as ongoing. Are there any data-sharing agreements, regulatory commitments, or trial-protocol restrictions that limit disclosure of manufacturing parameters or intermediate results described here?
- All authors have reviewed and approved the final version; are there any non-author contributors (e.g., statistical consultants, bioinformaticians, editorial support) who should be acknowledged?
