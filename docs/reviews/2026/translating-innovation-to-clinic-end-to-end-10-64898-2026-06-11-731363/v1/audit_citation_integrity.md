## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Reference resolvability] Bale 2025 [3] entry is thin** — Entry reads "Bale S, et al. … npj Vaccines. 2025." with no volume, pages, or DOI. It is cited (nsEM comparison) and the paper exists, but the entry is not self-contained.
- **[Reference resolvability] DOI parse artifacts (inserted spaces in DOIs)** — DOIs in [4] ("10.1 128/JVI.01894-16"), [18] ("10.1 126/…"), and [32] ("0. 1 1 26/…") contain inserted spaces; [23] and [24] lack DOIs but carry full journal/volume/page citations. These appear to be PDF-converter artifacts.
- **[Claim–citation support] "Binds 20 of 27 potential BG18 precursor antibodies with Kd ≤ 1 μM" — Steichen 2019 [24]** — The specific quantitative claim "Binds 20 of 27 potential BG18 precursor antibodies with Kd ≤ 1 μM" is load-bearing (establishes the immunogen's design credentials). The reference exists and plausibly contains such data, but the figure could not be confirmed with the tools available.
- **[Quotation/number fidelity] N332-GT5/SMNP macaque data and affinity improvement figures — Steichen 2024 [23]** — N332-GT5/SMNP primed BG18-class precursors in all 8 macaques; 6,000-fold affinity improvement (98 nM → <4 pM) within 10 weeks; 65% of BG18 type I antibodies bound N332-glycan-containing trimers. These specific figures are load-bearing (justify clinical advancement). The reference exists and plausibly contains them, but the exact numbers could not be verified.
- **[Claim–citation support] SMNP adjuvant claim cited to (Silva et al., 2021; Pallerla et al., 2025)** — The claim "N332-GT5 gp140 adjuvanted with SMNP" is plausibly supported by Pallerla 2025 [13] (whose title concerns SMNP). Silva 2021 [21] is titled "A stabilized HIV-1 envelope trimer that mimics the native viral spike induces neutralizing antibodies" — its relevance to the SMNP-adjuvant claim is unclear.
