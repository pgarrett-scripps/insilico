# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript contains 32 references spanning multiple categories: Reference Resolvability, Claim–Citation Support, Quotation/Number Fidelity, Self-Citation, and potential Retracted/Predatory Sources. Of these, only 1 reference (Steichen et al., 2019) is readily resolvable; 30 cannot be independently verified through available search tools, and 1 citation (CryoSPARC) appears in the text but is missing from the reference list. Three references are flagged as HARD severity issues because they support load-bearing claims: Steichen et al. 2024 (preclinical efficacy in 8/8 animals), Dey et al. 2018 (foundation for downstream process design), and Shukla & Aranha 2015 (viral clearance acceptance criteria). A year discrepancy is noted for Sanders et al. (cited as 2015 in text, 2013 in references). Multiple concurrent/in-press publications (Steichen 2024, Parks 2025, Pallerla 2025, Bale 2025) cannot be verified independently. Overall, the reference list appears comprehensive and directed toward legitimate peer-reviewed venues, but the audit cannot confirm that specific quantitative and methodological claims are actually supported by the cited works without access to full texts or verified identifiers.
