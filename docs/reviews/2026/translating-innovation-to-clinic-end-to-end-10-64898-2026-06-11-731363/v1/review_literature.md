# Related-Work & Citations Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript's citation record is fundamentally sound for a manufacturing-focused bioprocess paper. Foundational works on native-like Env trimers (Sanders et al. 2015, Dey et al. 2018) and the N332-GT design (Steichen et al. 2019, 2024) are appropriately credited, and recent clinical advances are included. However, two issues prevent acceptance without revision: (1) omission of Gulla et al. (2021), a directly competing cGMP manufacturing approach for HIV Env trimers using non-affinity purification, which represents a significant gap in related-work coverage that should be acknowledged and compared; and (2) a malformed DOI in the Parks et al. (2025) reference ("0.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831" should be "10.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831"). Additionally, Bale et al. (2025) lacks complete publication details despite being cited as a 2025 work. These are correctable with minor revision, and the work does not misrepresent prior art or silently omit foundational work to inflate novelty.

## Strengths
- Appropriate citation of foundational Env trimer work: Sanders et al. (2015) on BG505 SOSIP.664 and Dey et al. (2018) on cGMP production are correctly cited as the basis for downstream process development.
- Accurate attribution of N332-GT design lineage: Steichen et al. (2019, 2024) are properly credited for the germline-targeting design strategy and preclinical macaque immunogenicity data.
- Relevant inclusion of recent clinical data: Parks et al. (2025) on mRNA-encoded membrane-anchored HIV envelope trimers in a Phase 1 trial appropriately situates this work within the emerging clinical landscape.
- Appropriate citation of manufacturing platform work: Wolfe et al. (2021) on platform-based clinical production of HIV gp120 provides relevant precedent for downstream development.
- Viral clearance standards properly sourced: Shukla & Aranha (2015) is cited for industry benchmarks on retroviral log reduction, supporting the viral clearance study design.
- Citation verification: All spot-checked references (Dey 2018, Steichen 2019/2024, Parks 2025, Wolfe 2021) are confirmed in PubMed or bioRxiv with no fabricated or garbled entries detected.

## Weaknesses
- Omission of directly competing cGMP manufacturing method: Gulla et al. (2021) describes a non-affinity purification process for GMP production of prefusion-closed HIV-1 envelope trimers from clades A and C for clinical evaluation, representing a directly competing approach that is not cited or discussed. The manuscript should acknowledge this alternative approach and explain why the affinity-capture strategy was chosen over non-affinity methods.
- Malformed DOI in Parks et al. (2025) reference: The citation lists "0.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831" but should read "10.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831" (missing leading "1"), compromising citation hygiene and verifiability.
- Incomplete citation of Bale et al. (2025): Reference 3 lacks volume, issue, or DOI information despite being cited as a 2025 publication. Given the manuscript's date of June 16, 2026, complete citation information should be available.
- Limited discussion of alternative cell line platforms: The manuscript uses CHO cells but does not cite or discuss other mammalian cell line platforms (HEK293, PER.C6) used for HIV Env production in the literature, though this is a softer omission given CHO's established use.
- Glycosylation analysis methods not contextualized: While DeGlyPHER (Baboo et al. 2021, 2023) and LC-MS glycoproteomics (Watanabe et al. 2020) are cited, the manuscript does not reference or compare to other glycosylation profiling methods (Cao et al. 2017, Go et al. 2017, Behrens et al. 2017) that appear in the reference list but are not discussed in methods or results.

## Questions
- Why was the non-affinity purification approach of Gulla et al. (2021) not cited or compared? Does your affinity-capture strategy offer advantages (e.g., higher purity, better scalability) that should be explicitly discussed relative to this competing method?
- Can you confirm the correct DOI for Parks et al. (2025)? The reference currently reads "0.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831" but should likely be "10.1126/scitranslmed.ady6831".
- Can you provide complete citation information (volume, issue, DOI) for Bale et al. (2025)? Is this work directly comparable to your cGMP manufacturing approach, and if so, should it be discussed in the results or discussion?
- In Section 2.7.1, you employ both DeGlyPHER and LC-MS glycoproteomics. Why were these two complementary methods chosen over others (e.g., Cao et al. 2017, Go et al. 2017)? A brief justification would strengthen the methods section.
- Is there a separate publication on HVTN144 clinical outcomes or interim results? If so, should this be cited to clarify the scope of the present manuscript (manufacturing-focused) versus clinical findings?
- The manuscript cites both Steichen et al. (2019) for the original N332-GT design strategy and Steichen et al. (2024) for preclinical macaque immunogenicity. Are these distinct papers, or does (2024) supersede (2019)? Clarify the relationship for readers.
