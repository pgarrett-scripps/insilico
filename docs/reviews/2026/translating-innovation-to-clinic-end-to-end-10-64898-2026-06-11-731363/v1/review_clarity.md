# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
This is a well-organized, comprehensive bioprocess development manuscript with generally clear presentation of methods and results. The narrative flow from cell line development through manufacturing is logical and easy to follow. However, there are several HARD clarity issues in figure legends and table references that prevent complete standalone understanding, and some SOFT inconsistencies in terminology and quantitative framing that create minor friction. The central contribution—demonstrating scalable cGMP manufacturing of N332-GT5 gp140 while maintaining structural integrity—is stated clearly in the abstract and introduction, but the manuscript would benefit from tightening figure/table integration and standardizing how process parameters are described across scales.

## Strengths
- The abstract clearly states the central contribution: development of a cGMP-compliant process for N332-GT5 gp140 that maintains structural conformity and clinical-grade quality standards.
- The manuscript follows a logical progression: cell line development → upstream process optimization → downstream purification → characterization, with each section's purpose clearly motivated.
- Methods are generally well-organized with numbered subsections (2.3.1–2.7) that make it easy to locate specific procedures; the order of operations is recoverable for most unit operations.
- Quantitative claims are typically explicit: 'high-titer expression (>200 mg/L)', 'genetic stability through 60 population doublings', '>99% trimeric purity', '18-log and 11-log reductions for model retroviruses'.
- Figures 1 and 2 effectively summarize workflow and process evaluation strategy, providing visual scaffolding for the narrative.
- Table 1 clearly specifies Ambr®250 study parameters with explicit feed concentrations, temperature shifts, and pH setpoints, enabling reproducibility.
- The viral clearance section (Table 12) quantifies log reductions for each unit operation and sums them, making the contribution of each step transparent.

## Weaknesses
-  Figure 7 caption states 'BLI titer measurement' but does not define what the y-axis represents (mg/L is inferred from context in the text, not stated in the legend); the legend should explicitly state units and what 'BG18' and 'PGT145' represent (antibodies used as detection reagents) without requiring the reader to cross-reference section 2.1.2.
- Figure 14 panels (a), (b), (c) are referenced in text as showing 'Viable Cell Count (VCC)', 'Cell Viability (%)', and 'BLI titer', but the figure caption does not explicitly state which panel is which, forcing the reader to infer from the parenthetical labels in the caption text rather than from a clear legend structure.
- Table 2 header states 'only differences shown' for the MCB and GMP rows, but it is not immediately clear from the table structure which rows represent which scale/cell bank; the reader must parse the header text carefully. A clearer column structure (e.g., 'Parameter | 50-L RCB (XDR-50) | 50-L MCB Demo (XDR-200) | 50-L GMP (XDR-200)') would improve readability.
- Section 3.3.1 references 'Table 3' for robustness evaluations, but the table header uses inconsistent terminology: 'Theoretical WCPQ' and 'Theoretical WCY' are introduced without prior definition. While 'WCPQ' and 'WCY' are eventually explained in context (worst-case product quality, worst-case yield), they should be defined in the table legend or at first use in the Methods.
- Figure 16 shows 'Average step yields for each unit operation' but does not specify whether error bars represent standard deviation, standard error, or range; the legend states 'Depicted are (a) Process overview, (b) Average step yields...' but does not quantify the n or variability measure.
- The manuscript uses 'BDS' (bulk drug substance) and 'drug substance' interchangeably without a clear definition at first use; while context makes the meaning recoverable, an explicit definition in the Methods would improve clarity.
- Section 2.5.4 describes the final downstream process with multiple unit operations, but the connection between the 'Feasibility Run' (section 3.3.1) and the 'final process' (section 2.5.4) is not explicitly stated. It is unclear whether the final process incorporates all robustness findings or whether some steps were modified after feasibility; the text states 'After downstream process development and robustness evaluations, the process was finalized' but does not explicitly map which findings led to which changes.
- Figure 18 caption states 'Site-specific glycan content was determined by LC-MS on intact glycopeptides' but does not define the y-axis label or explain what the stacked bar categories represent (oligomannose, complex, hybrid, unoccupied) until the figure legend text; the figure itself is not self-contained.
- The term 'reference standard' or 'reference material' is used inconsistently: section 3.2.3 mentions 'IA VI/Scripps N332-GT5 reference material, 1.0 mg/mL, Lot: 19Apr0088' and 'KBI Reference Material lot # S-20210314-0001-SD2-E-M', but it is not clear whether these are the same material, different lots, or different sources; this ambiguity affects interpretation of titer comparisons across runs.

## Questions
- In Figure 7, what do the y-axis values represent numerically? The caption says 'BLI titer measurement' but does not state units (mg/L, µg/mL, etc.). Are 'BG18' and 'PGT145' the names of the antibodies used as detection reagents, and should this be clarified in the legend?
- Table 2 compares three runs (50-L RCB, 50-L MCB Demo, 50-L GMP), but the row labels are ambiguous. Can the authors clarify which row corresponds to which run, and would a clearer column-based structure improve readability?
- In section 3.3.1, what do 'WCPQ' and 'WCY' stand for? These abbreviations appear in Table 3 without definition in the table legend; they are eventually explained in the text, but should be defined at first use.
- Figure 16 shows error bars on the step yield data, but the legend does not specify whether these represent standard deviation, standard error, mean ± range, or another measure. What is the n for each unit operation?
- Section 2.5.4 describes the 'final process' for pilot and GMP scale, but how does this process differ from the 'Feasibility Run' process shown in Figure 3? Which robustness findings from section 3.3.1 led to changes in the final process?
- In section 3.2.3, three different reference materials are mentioned (IA VI/Scripps, KBI Reference Material S-20210314-0001-SD2-E-M, and KBI Reference Material P65). Are these the same material from different lots, or different preparations? How does the choice of reference standard affect the comparability of titers across the three runs?
- Figure 18 shows glycan composition at each PNGS, but the figure is difficult to parse without the legend. Can the authors clarify what each stacked bar category represents (oligomannose, complex, hybrid, unoccupied) and provide a color key in the figure itself rather than only in the caption?
- In section 3.3.2, the authors state that Superdex 200pg chromatography was removed from the process due to supply constraints. Table 10 shows that product quality was comparable with and without this step. However, does the removal of this step affect the ability to detect or remove any impurities that would have been removed by SEC? Should this be discussed as a potential limitation?
