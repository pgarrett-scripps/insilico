# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript presents a detailed account of the bioprocess development and cGMP manufacturing of the N332-GT5 gp140 HIV vaccine candidate. It is generally well-organized and the core narrative — cell line development through to GMP production and characterization — is recoverable. However, the text contains several HARD clarity issues, particularly in the results tables (Table 1 and Table 5), in the comparison of titers across runs that use different reference standards, and in several places where a claim is stated but the precise value, definition, or source is ambiguous. The number of SOFT issues is moderate but includes some redundancy between the methods and results sections, inconsistent terminology for the same process step, and one figure legend that is clearly garbled (Figure 13). A competent reader in the field can follow the argument, but not without stopping to resolve ambiguity at several key points. This manuscript requires moderate revision to meet presentation standards for a public, permanent review artifact.

## Strengths
- The manuscript includes explicit hold-time stability data for each process intermediate, with defined stability criteria (%HMW or %LMW change threshold and relative potency limit).
- The viral clearance study uses two model viruses (XMuLV and MMV) with clearly reported LRV values for each unit operation, meeting orthogonal clearance expectations.
- Negative-stain EM and two orthogonal mass spectrometry methods for glycan analysis provide converging evidence for the structural integrity of the final product.

## Weaknesses
- HARD — Central claim ambiguity in the abstract and results: The abstract states the process 'yielded >99% trimeric purity,' but the specific value reported in Table 11 for the cGMP batch is '%Main Peak: 99.4%.' The text does not state whether '>99%' is a rounded version of 99.4%, a different metric (e.g., averaged across batches), or a general claim about method performance. A reader cannot determine the exact measured value for the reported claim. The sentence in the results section that says the process 'reduced %HMW levels to < 1%, with % Main Peak > 98% (SE-HPLC)' for the pilot-scale run is also imprecise compared to the exact number in the table.
- HARD — Incomparable titer data across runs: The results section (pilot and GMP scale) reports that the Day 14 BLI titer differed between the 50L supply run (562 mg/L) and the 50L demonstration run (355 mg/L). The text then explains that the difference is 'mainly due to the reference material used,' citing three different reference standards across the three runs. A reader cannot compare the titers of these runs, and the text does not report whether a cross-calibration between reference standards was performed. This is a HARD clarity issue because the central metric of the upstream process is not comparable across the runs that are being compared.
- HARD — Table 1 (Ambr 250 process optimization parameters) has structural and labeling issues that prevent a reader from recovering the experimental design: (1) row 7 and row 9 appear to have the same label 'High inoc, 40% 7a slow, 34°C Day 5' but differ in one condition (34°C Day 4 vs 34°C Day 5) that is not unambiguously encoded in the table. (2) Row 8 is labeled '34°C Day 5' without full context. (3) Rows 10-12 appear to be pH shift conditions but the table column headers do not make clear whether these are additional rows or modifications to the preceding condition. The overall structure mixes conditions and row labels in a way that is not self-documenting.
- HARD — Table 5 lists 'BG18/PGT145 productivity ratio' and 'efficiency of furin cleavage' as ranking parameters but does not define the numerical criteria used to rank clones or the threshold between 'acceptable' and 'unacceptable.' The text states the ranking was 'based on growth characteristics, productivity, product quality and the presence of recombinant furin' but does not say which of the 24 clones passed which criterion, leaving the final selection of clone C235 underdetermined.
- SOFT — The abstract states 'Robust viral clearance exceeded 18-log and 11-log reductions for model retroviruses.' The precise values from Table 12 are '≥ 18.14' and '≥ 11.70.' Rounding '18.14' to '18' and '11.70' to '11' loses precision that is meaningful for a claim about a regulatory safety margin, and the word 'exceeded' implies the stated number is the lower bound, while the table reports the value as a lower bound already (≥). A reader could interpret the abstract claim differently from the table data.
- SOFT — Inconsistent naming for the same process step: The downstream process description uses 'UF/DF #1' and 'UF2' for two separate ultrafiltration/diafiltration steps (section 2.5.4.2 and 2.5.4.8), but also the text and figures refer to 'UF/DF1' and 'UF/DF3' elsewhere (e.g., Table 4: 'UF/DF1 Load' and 'UF/DF3 Load'). A reader cannot be sure which is which without cross-referencing multiple sections.

## Questions
- The abstract states the three-step purification 'yielded >99% trimeric purity with preserved quaternary structure and native-like antigenicity.' The manuscript reports a final SE-HPLC %Main of 99.4%. Is the reported '>99%' value a rounded form of this number, a different metric, or a general claim? Please quote the exact value used and its source.
- Sentence: 'Similar approaches for other candidate molecules should help shorten development timelines leading up to clinical trials and are well-suited for HIV-1 Env trimer process development.' Is this a speculation or is it supported by data in this or a referenced manuscript? Please clarify the nature of the claim.
- Table 1: The row 'Inoc @ 1.0e6, High inoc, 40% 7a slow, 34°C Day 5' in column 1 is followed by a row that reads '34°C Day 5' and then 'Control + 34°C Day 5'. The table has what appear to be two different rows with similar names. Can the authors confirm the row numbering and labels are correct, and clarify which parameters correspond to which row?
