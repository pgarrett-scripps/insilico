# Journal Recommendations

## As-is (current quality)
- **Biotechnology Progress**
  - Fit: This journal specializes in bioprocess scale-up, manufacturing documentation, and translational development. The manuscript's detailed upstream and downstream process characterization, robustness testing, and viral clearance data are core to the scope, and Biotechnology Progress does not require novelty claims beyond technical rigor and transparency—which this work demonstrates.
  - Realism: Moderately realistic; the editor's verdict of 'minor' indicates the core technical package is sound, and this venue's focus on reproducible scale-up and cGMP documentation matches the manuscript's actual contribution. However, the factual error ('multiple cGMP batches' vs. singular) and framing overreach would require corrections even at this tier.
- **Bioprocess and Biosystems Engineering**
  - Fit: Covers process development, optimization, and scale-up of biologics with emphasis on methodological rigor and detailed characterization. The 60-PD genetic stability assessment, worst-case robustness studies (Tables 3, 7–9), and 18+ log viral clearance margins align with the journal's scope for translational bioprocess work.
  - Realism: Realistic as-is; this venue emphasizes technical documentation and process comparability studies. The concern raised by reviewers—that absolute titer comparability is confounded by three non-equivalent reference standards—is precisely the kind of limitation this journal handles transparently. Acceptance depends on whether the current framing of 'reproducible paradigm' is acceptable or requires softening.
- **Vaccine**
  - Fit: Accepts manufacturing and development papers on vaccine candidates, including preclinical scale-up and clinical batch production. N332-GT5 gp140 is actively in HVTN144 clinical trials, and the manuscript reports the only GMP batch data linking preclinical promise to human dosing. The paper's transparency about process constraints (SEC removal due to supply chain) fits Vaccine's audience of developers and clinicians.
  - Realism: Moderately realistic as-is, subject to acceptability of the current framing. The 'multiple cGMP batches' error is a clear target for desk rejection, but once corrected, the paper's translational value and detailed batch characterization are well-matched to the journal's scope and editorial standards.

## After required revisions
- **Biotechnology and Bioengineering**
  - Fit: Premier bioprocess journal that regularly publishes large-scale manufacturing campaigns, process development, and detailed characterization of complex biologics. The revised manuscript—with corrected singular 'cGMP batch' language, disclosed reference-standard confounds, and explicit limitations on the SEC removal decision—will be a textbook example of honest, reproducible process documentation suitable for process scientists and manufacturing practitioners.
  - Realism: Very realistic after minor revisions address the claim-calibration issues. The underlying work (viral clearance margins, glycosylation profiles, structural integrity by nsEM, 60-PD genetic stability) is solid and at the right technical depth for this journal. The editor's reframing of identified issues as non-negotiable textual corrections rather than experiments indicates that compliance yields a publication-ready manuscript.
- **Journal of Biotechnology**
  - Fit: Publishes translational bioprocess and manufacturing papers with emphasis on cell culture, purification, scale-up, and clinical-grade production. After revisions, the manuscript's detailed upstream optimization, downstream robustness, and product characterization will match the journal's scope for end-to-end process translation from research to cGMP.
  - Realism: Very realistic after revisions. The journal welcomes papers that document manufacturing platforms for complex biologics, particularly when accompanied by comprehensive characterization and honest discussion of process constraints (e.g., supply-driven SEC removal). The minor revision verdict strongly supports acceptance at this tier once claim language is corrected.
- **npj Vaccines**
  - Fit: Nature's open-access vaccine journal covers vaccine development, manufacturing, and early clinical assessment. The N332-GT5/SMNP vaccine is in first-in-human trials (HVTN144), and the manuscript provides the manufacturing platform and batch characterization that link preclinical promise to human dosing—directly relevant to the vaccine development audience.
  - Realism: Realistic after revisions, particularly if the authors strengthen the clinical/translational narrative. The journal accepts manufacturing papers when coupled to clinical progression. The required revision to claim 'consistent performance across two cell banks and three sequential runs' rather than asserting 'reproducible paradigm' will fit npj Vaccines' standards for evidence-based claims.

## Alternative outlets

  - Fit: Preprint server widely used for vaccine development, bioprocess, and manufacturing manuscripts. The manuscript is already posted here and serves as an immediate reference for HVTN144 investigators, manufacturability documentation for collaborators, and a citable record of the batch-to-human translation prior to or alongside journal publication.
  - Realism: Already available; posting as-is on bioRxiv is low-friction and maintains accessibility while journal revision is underway. Preprints in the vaccine/bioprocess space have high visibility within the field.
- **Viruses (open access)**
  - Fit: Covers virology, vaccine development, and related bioprocess/manufacturing topics. Accepts translational papers documenting vaccine candidate manufacture and characterization, with a lower barrier to publication than top-tier venues. The manuscript's viral clearance data, glycosylation characterization, and structural integrity assessment fit the journal's scope.
  - Realism: Realistic as a fallback if higher-tier venues require more extensive revision than anticipated. Open-access model ensures wide dissemination to vaccine developers and manufacturing scientists, though the journal carries lower prestige than Biotechnology and Bioengineering or npj Vaccines.
- **Vaccine (engineering/manufacturing focus)**
  - Fit: Same journal as 'after_revision' tier, but listed here as an explicit fallback if the revised manuscript does not reach the confidence level for higher-impact outlets. Vaccine reliably publishes manufacturing and scale-up papers and provides a natural home for the work.
  - Realism: Reliable fallback; acceptance odds are high once the required revisions are made, given the journal's stated scope and the editor's verdict of minor rather than major revision.

## Notes
The manuscript's core technical contributions—60-PD genetic stability with three orthogonal metrics, worst-case robustness testing, ≥18 log viral clearance, orthogonal DeGlyPHER and LC-MS glycosylation analysis, and nsEM confirmation of native-like trimers—are sound and field-advancing. Reviewer consensus (1 accept, 7 minor; 4.15/5 confidence) confirms that the primary barriers to acceptance are claim-calibration and methodological transparency, not data quality. The single cGMP batch used for HVTN144 is already dosed in humans, making honest disclosure of single-execution limitations (e.g., SEC removal by unreplicated parallel comparison, titer confounding by three reference standards) essential. Venues are ranked by realistic acceptance after required editorial corrections; none should be approached until the factual error ('multiple cGMP batches') and overreach claims ('reproducible paradigm') are corrected per the decision letter. The final revised manuscript will be well-suited for venues in the Biotechnology and Bioengineering / npj Vaccines tier, where rigorous process documentation with transparent limitations is expected and valued.
