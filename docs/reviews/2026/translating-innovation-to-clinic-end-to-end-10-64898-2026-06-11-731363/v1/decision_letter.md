# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel's numerical signal (unweighted 3.43/5, verdict distribution 3 minor / 4 major) and the written specialist reports converge on a clear diagnosis: the core engineering is sound and the manufacturing achievement is real, but the manuscript as submitted overstates several claims relative to the evidence actually presented and contains critical reporting gaps. The four major votes (rigor, data_analysis, methodology, reproducibility) identify overlapping problems: (1) the genetic-stability claim (60-PD study) is described in Methods but entirely absent from Results — no data table, figure, or value appears; (2) titers across the three 50-L runs were measured against three different, unbridged reference standards, making the scale-up reproducibility claim formally uninterpretable; (3) the abstract's 'multiple cGMP batches' is contradicted by the single GMP batch described; (4) the glycan 'matching design specifications' claim has no stated specification and the two analytical methods disagree on occupancy at several sites. The skeptic's rebuttal is persuasive on these points: these are not mere 'reporting gaps' — bridging the reference standards and qualifying the viral-clearance scale-down model would require additional work beyond textual revision. However, the advocate is also correct that the viral clearance data are unusually comprehensive and conservatively reported, the practical innovation (SEC removal) is well-documented, and the candor about known limitations (reference-standard drift, >100% step yield) is a genuine strength. The literature and ethics auditors found no blocking issues. The citation integrity audit identified one HARD gap (missing reference Punjani et al. 2017 for cryoSPARC) and several resolvable citation errors. The decision is major revision: the required revisions are concrete, checkable actions that address the central evidentiary weaknesses. If the authors can supply the missing genetic-stability data, bridge the titer comparisons, qualify the viral-clearance scale-down, correct the overclaims, and fix the presentation errors, the manuscript would meet the In Silico bar and I would accept it.
