# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
This manuscript describes the first cGMP manufacturing campaign for the N332-GT5 gp140 germline-targeting HIV Env immunogen. The core effort — cell line development, upstream/downstream process development, and analytical characterization — is substantial and honestly reported. The panel's scores cluster in the 'major revision' range (5 major, 2 minor, 1 reject), with the reproducibility review (2.0) and the methodology review (3.0) identifying several load-bearing gaps that prevent full evaluation of the central quantitative claims. The Editorial Compliance Audits independently confirm 19 hard gaps in methods completeness and reagent traceability, plus several citation-integrity issues. The skeptic's argument — that the viral clearance, glycan occupancy, and titer-comparability claims cannot be reproduced from the manuscript as written — is well-founded and is the primary basis for the major-revision decision. These gaps are fixable: they require deposition of raw data (mass spec, viral clearance assay details), cross-calibration of reference standards, clarification of the design specification for glycosylation, and proper reporting of replication statistics. The requalification of overbroad language ('paradigm', 'blueprint', 'successfully administered') is also necessary. If the authors address all required revisions concretely, the manuscript will meet the bar for In Silico. I encourage resubmission.

## Required Revisions
1. Deposit raw mass spectrometry data (DeGlyPHER and LC-MS glycoproteomics) in a public repository (e.g., MassIVE, PRIDE) and provide the accession numbers in the manuscript.
2. Provide a per-unit-operation comparability table (column dimensions, bed height, linear flow rate, load ratio, plus step yield and product quality metrics) demonstrating that the scale-down model used for the viral clearance study is representative of the manufacturing-scale process.
3. Provide a complete description of the viral clearance study: virus strains used, spike titers, quantification method (e.g., TCID50, plaque assay), limit of detection per assay, cytotoxicity and interference controls, replicate structure (number of independent runs), and per-experiment LRV values with clear indication of whether reported values are measured reductions or assay-limited lower bounds.
4. Provide a table reconciling site-specific glycan occupancy across the two MS methods at every site where disagreement is observed (N185e, N611, N625, and any others), with replicate counts per site. State what the 'design specifications' for glycosylation are (occupancy threshold per site, oligomannose/complex ratio at key epitopes) and confirm that the GMP material meets them, or requalify the claim to 'consistent with the expected glycosylation profile' and explain the basis for that expectation.
5. Re-assay archived samples from all three production runs (50L material supply, 50L demonstration, 50L GMP) against a single common BLI reference standard and present the re-assayed titers, or restrict the titer-reproducibility claim to cell-growth and product-quality metrics that are measured on absolute scales.
6. Revisit the harvest-yield percentages: for the 50L material supply run, clarified harvest titer is reported as 399 mg/L, Day 14 bioreactor titer as 562 mg/L; 399/562 = 71%, not 91.4%. Provide the correct harvest-yield calculation with volumes used and clarify whether the denominator and numerator refer to different analytical methods (e.g., BLI vs. total protein).
7. Report n values (number of independent measurements, injections, or replicates) for every quantitative result in Tables 6–11 and Figures 15, 17–19. For BLI titer data (Tables 6, Figure 13), report the intra-assay CV and limit of quantification. For SE-HPLC and RP-HPLC purity values, report the number of injections.
8. Provide a table comparing the N332-GT5 process performance (step yields, overall yield, SE-HPLC %Main, residual HCP, residual DNA, viral clearance LRVs) against the published BG505 SOSIP.664 cGMP campaign (Dey et al. 2018) to allow readers to assess equivalence or improvement.
9. Provide the N332-GT5 gp140 coding sequence (as a GenBank accession or in supplementary material), the human furin ORF sequence, and the primer sequences used for transcript verification.
10. Provide RRID or catalog numbers for all antibodies used (PGT145, BG18_GL0, DEN3, 2G12), and confirm whether the 2G12 antibody's monoclonal nature, clonality, and lot number are available from Polymun Scientific.
11. Provide STR authentication data and mycoplasma testing results for the HD BIOP3 parental cell line, the research cell bank (RCB), and the master cell bank (MCB).
12. Provide software version numbers for CryoSPARC, Leginon, ProLuCID, Byos, IP2, DTASelect2, Census2, GlycoMSQuant, CFX Manager, SoloVPE, and the Octet/ForteBio instrument.
13. State the residual Triton X-100 level (µg/g or ppm) in the final drug substance and the LOD/LOQ of the quantification assay. The REACH restriction on Triton X-100 (EU phase-out 2025–2026) should be acknowledged, with a brief comment on whether alternative detergents are under evaluation.
14. Remove or requalify overgeneralising language ('paradigm', 'blueprint', 'advancing the field toward rational vaccine design') in the Abstract and Conclusions; reframe the paper as a case study of this specific immunogen.
15. Remove the claim that material was 'successfully administered' in HVTN144 from the Abstract, unless clinical safety or immunogenicity data from the trial are included. Retain the factual statement that clinical material was manufactured and released for the trial.
16. Reconcile the '>99% trimeric purity' claim in the Abstract with the SE-HPLC data: state the exact %Main value (99.4% for the cGMP batch) and clarify that the negative-stain EM observation of 'nearly 100% native-like trimers' is a qualitative structural assessment, not a quantitative purity measurement.
17. Add a data-availability statement confirming that all raw data supporting the findings are deposited in public repositories with accession numbers, or explain any restrictions. If any core data cannot be deposited, the corresponding claims must be narrowed accordingly.
18. Add a statement on product stability during long-term storage (≥6 months at -80°C or -20°C), or note that these studies are in progress.
19. Remove uncited references from the reference list: Hahn 2024, Klasse 2012, Kwong 2018, Pauthner 2017, Ramezani-Rad 2025, Sanders 2013. Correct the 'Sander' typo to 'Sanders' in Ref [33]. Fix DOI parse artifacts (inserted spaces) in Refs [4], [18], and [32]. Add the missing reference for Punjani et al. 2017 (Nature Methods) cited in Section 3.6.2.
