# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel is broadly favorable (weighted 3.77/5; six 'minor', two 'major'), and I agree with the meta-reviewer that the two 'major' verdicts should carry disproportionate weight here rather than being averaged away. Both dissenters sit at confidence 4 and occupy exactly the domains where the objections bite — statistics and reproducibility — and their critiques are mutually reinforcing: readers cannot adjudicate the undisclosed multiple-testing status precisely because raw data, processed tables, and code have not been deposited. Critically, the surviving objections attach to headline claims. The 78% fraction-specific figure rests on per-fraction ANOVA over ~6,123 proteins at uncorrected p<0.05; the Fig 9E human-mouse concordance rests on a post-hoc stipulation with no null model; the six-protein 'initiating features' claim is conditioned by an unjustified Day0 (N=1, pooled) vs Day7 (N=4) asymmetry. The authors concede all three and propose exactly the right remedies (BH-FDR re-run with re-derived figures, 10,000-iteration permutation null, pooling justification plus sensitivity analysis). These are re-analyses whose outcomes could change the central quantitative pillar, not wording edits — which is why 'minor' would understate what this revision entails, and why the decision is major rather than minor despite the numeric signal.

What keeps this well clear of rejection is that every identified problem is fixable with data already in hand, and the paper's core demonstration survives independent of them. I weighed the authors' two substantive disagreements and find both credible. On fractionation validation, the GO-enrichment pattern across fractions (P3/nuclear, P2/mitochondrial, S2/lysosomal-vesicular, S3/ER; Fig 2D, Table S3), reproduced across 27 independent human samples, is genuine orthogonal evidence that the scheme separates compartments as intended; formal marker-protein immunoblots would strengthen it but their absence does not render the protocol unvalidated, and the authors' offer to state this as a limitation is the proportionate response. On the VPS35/VPS29 retromer result, convergence of LC-MS peptide quantification, immunoblot on the same fractionated tissue, and a null across seven independently generated datasets from other laboratories does argue against a power artifact specific to this study; I accept this as a load-bearing positive finding, subject to the hedging the authors themselves propose. On novelty, the authors' reframing — that the claimed advance is the integrated scale and the fraction-specificity result, not the fractionation or QUAD techniques — is reasonable and should be stated plainly in the Introduction rather than left implicit.

The compliance audits add nothing that changes the verdict but several items that must be closed: MS data deposition (already conceded), mass tolerances, antibody dilutions, animal randomization/blinding statements, and PMIDs/DOIs for the two Jankowsky mouse-model references. None of these misattribute a load-bearing claim.

I will require that the revision report the re-derived FDR-corrected figures and the permutation p-value transparently, whatever they show. If the 78% figure collapses substantially or the 155-protein overlap is not distinguishable from chance, the manuscript must be reframed around what the corrected analysis supports — the VPS35/retromer demonstration remains publishable on its own terms — rather than preserving the current headline framing. A candid negative result on either analysis is acceptable; an unreported one is not.

## Required Revisions
1. Re-run the per-fraction differential analysis with an explicit multiple-testing correction (Benjamini-Hochberg as proposed), and report the re-derived 78%, 2,405, and 1,905 figures. If these change materially, revise the abstract, Results framing, and the claim that 'subcellular localization is a primary determinant of disease vulnerability' to match what the corrected numbers support. Apply the same correction to the Tau r>0.7 correlation screen and state how many of the 124 proteins survive. State the correction method and threshold in Methods and in every figure legend reporting p-values.
2. Deposit raw MS files (.raw/mzML) and search results to ProteomeXchange/PRIDE or MassIVE and quote the accession in a Data Availability statement; upload all 18 supplementary tables as structured machine-readable files; deposit WGCNA, correlation, and human-mouse integration scripts to a versioned GitHub/Zenodo repository with a DOI. Add a Code Availability statement.
3. Add the permutation-based null model for the Figure 9E human-mouse concordance (10,000 iterations, direction labels randomized within each dataset) and report the empirical p-value for the observed 155-protein overlap (27 P3, 128 S2). If the overlap is not significantly greater than chance, remove or explicitly requalify the cross-species validation claim and the derived 'initiating features' statement in the abstract.
4. Justify the QUAD Day0 pooling (N=1) vs Day7 (N=4) design in Methods, discuss its consequences for variance and power of Day7/Day0 ratios in the Discussion, and add the proposed supplementary sensitivity analysis using unpooled Day0 replicates where available.
5. Correct the Methods/figure-legend statistical discrepancy: state that one-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc was used for QUAD genotype comparisons, remove the Student t-test language, and specify the family size (number of pairwise comparisons) for each Tukey correction.
6. Replace causal language with hedged formulations throughout the abstract, Results, and Discussion ('patterns consistent with retromer mislocalization', 'suggestive of nuclear transport dysfunction', 'may involve alterations in protein degradation'), and add an explicit limitation stating that reciprocal abundance and degradation-rate data cannot distinguish active trafficking/synthesis changes from passive aggregation or altered solubility in an insoluble-enriched P3 fraction.
7. Remove 'pre-pathological' for the 2-month APPswePS1delta9 timepoint (given ~5-fold Aβ42 elevation, Fig S4C) and substitute 'earliest timepoint examined' or 'early, low-Aβ-burden timepoint'; requalify the six-protein finding as 'candidate early perturbations' rather than 'initiating features of AD'.
8. Move the AsymAD composition of the control group (11/14 NA samples) into Results immediately after the cohort description, reframe it as a limitation rather than an advantage, and add the proposed sensitivity comparison of AD vs. the 3 pathology-free controls, reporting whether VPS35, the Tau-Braak correlation, and retromer discord replicate.
9. Add the following statements: Funding; Competing Interests; IRB approval/exemption determination and informed-consent status for the Shiley-Marcos ADRC postmortem samples; and the IACUC protocol approval number for the mouse work (the current AAALAC/IACUC sentence lacks a protocol identifier).
10. Close the Methods-completeness HARD gaps identified by audit: (a) state randomization of animal assignment and blinding status during analysis (or state explicitly that none was performed); (b) give antibody dilutions and application for each immunoblot antibody (VPS-35 sc-374372, TAU-5 AHB0042, Ubiquitin CST 431224), with host species, clonality, and RRIDs where available; (c) specify precursor and fragment mass tolerances used in the ProteomeDiscoverer search.
11. Specify the exact WGCNA parameters in Methods (soft-thresholding power, minimum module size, merge cut height, correlation type, clustering/distance metric) and the missing-value filtering criteria applied before analysis; likewise state Webgestalt organism, database version, and multiple-testing correction.
12. Correct the reference list: remove the duplicate/erroneous Eftekharzadeh citation (refs 45 and 86 with conflicting volume/page), and either revise the APP sentence to reflect precisely what Seyfried et al. (ref 82) report or cite the correct primary source. Add PMIDs/DOIs for Jankowsky et al. 2004 (Hum Mol Genet 13:159-170) and Jankowsky et al. 2001 (Biomol Eng 17:157-165), which are load-bearing for the mouse model.
13. Standardize control-group nomenclature to 'AD' vs 'NA (non-AD, comprising AsymAD and pathology-free controls)' throughout text and figures, relabel Figure 1's 'ND' to 'NA', and define all group labels at first use in Results.

## Minor Suggestions
- State explicitly in the Introduction that the claimed advance is the integrated scale and the fraction-specificity finding rather than conceptual novelty of fractionation or QUAD per se — the rebuttal's framing on this point is persuasive and should appear in the manuscript.
- Add the proposed marker-protein validation limitation sentence (e.g., LAMP1, TOM20 immunoblots not performed) alongside the GO-enrichment argument; if marker blots on residual material are feasible, they would substantially strengthen the fractionation claim, but they are not required for acceptance.
- Provide vendor catalog number, purity, and dietary concentration for AHA, and vendor/concentration for biotin-alkyne DADPS, rather than delegating entirely to prior publications.
- Supply key parameters for protocols currently delegated to references (click chemistry, QUAD workflow, immunoblot densitometry and normalization) so the Methods are interpretable without retrieving the cited papers.
- Add PMIDs/DOIs for all references where feasible, including Thul et al. 2017, Bai et al. 2013, Lin et al. 2001, and Mayeux & St George-Hyslop 2009, and verify the 6.7 million / 55 million prevalence figures against the cited source.
- Specify TMT reagent lot numbers and the specific protease/phosphatase inhibitor cocktail catalog numbers.
