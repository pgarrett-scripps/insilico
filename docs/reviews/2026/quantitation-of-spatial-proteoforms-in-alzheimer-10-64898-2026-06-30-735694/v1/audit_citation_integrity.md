# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript triggers three compliance categories: Reference resolvability, Claim–citation support, and Quotation/number fidelity. Of 15 load-bearing citations checked, 10 were verified as resolvable with PMID/DOI and plausibly supporting stated claims. Two HARD gaps exist in methodological references (Refs 15 and 16 on the transgenic mouse model) that lack PMID/DOI identifiers, blocking independent verification of critical experimental inputs. Three additional SOFT gaps involve unverifiable claims where citations lack identifiers or exact statistics cannot be confirmed from abstracts alone. No retracted or predatory sources were identified. The manuscript's novel findings (spatial proteoforms, QUAD degradation data) are the authors' own and do not depend on unverifiable prior work.
