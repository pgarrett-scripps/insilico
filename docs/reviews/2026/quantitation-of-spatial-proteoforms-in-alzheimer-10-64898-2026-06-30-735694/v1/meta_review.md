# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel is broadly positive about the study's ambition and its central empirical observation — that subcellular fractionation of AD hippocampi surfaces disease signal invisible in bulk tissue. Six reviewers land at 'minor', two (data_analysis, reproducibility) at 'major', and the split is substantive rather than stylistic: it falls along the axis of whether the paper's headline numbers are currently verifiable.

What the whole panel agrees on: the fractionation and TMT-LC-MS execution is competent (methodology, rigor); the VPS35/VPS29 retromer result is the strongest single finding, supported by LC-MS, immunoblot, and its absence in a 7-dataset unfractionated meta-analysis (rigor, novelty, and — importantly — explicitly conceded by the skeptic); ethics oversight for human and animal work is disclosed; and the writing is organized and followable (clarity, 4@5).

The genuine tension is between the advocate's framing of the statistical gaps as 'classic revision-stage fixes' and the data_analysis (3@4) and reproducibility (3@4) reviewers' position that these gaps touch the load-bearing claims. The skeptic's rebuttal survived contact with the advocate on three specific points: (1) the 78% fraction-specific figure derives from ANOVA over ~6,123 proteins per fraction at p<0.05 with no disclosed multiple-testing correction — the advocate offered no counter beyond calling it 'derived'; (2) the human–mouse concordance (Fig 9E, 155 proteins) rests on a post-hoc mathematical stipulation with no null/permutation model, so the 'unlikely from noise' claim is asserted rather than shown; (3) the QUAD Day0 pooling (N=1) versus Day7 (N=4) asymmetry is neither justified nor discussed, and it directly conditions the six-protein 'initiating features' claim highlighted in the abstract. The reproducibility reviewer independently adds a hard blocker: no data-availability statement, no ProteomeXchange/MassIVE accession, no WGCNA parameters, and no code — for a study whose entire contribution is a large reanalyzable proteomic resource, this is not cosmetic.

Separately, methodology, novelty, and rigor converge on an interpretive problem the advocate conceded but under-weighted: 'mislocalization,' 'produced by,' and 'initiating features' are causal readings of reciprocal abundance ratios in cross-sectional post-mortem tissue, with no orthogonal localization or solubility assay to separate active trafficking from passive aggregation in an insoluble-enriched P3 fraction. Compounding this, the 2-month APPswePS1delta9 timepoint already shows ~5-fold Aβ42 elevation, so 'pre-pathological' is not accurate as written. The AsymAD composition of controls (11/14) is flagged by four reviewers as narrowing the comparison to symptomatic-vs-asymptomatic AD, and rigor notes the authors' framing of this as an advantage is logically inverted.

## Decisive Factors
The confidence-weighted average (3.77, six 'minor') would nominally point to minor revision, and I am diverging upward to major for two reasons. First, the two 'major' verdicts are not low-confidence outliers — both sit at confidence 4 and are the two reviewers whose remit is precisely the domain where the objections bite (statistics and reproducibility). Their critiques are also mutually reinforcing rather than independent: the undisclosed correction status cannot be adjudicated by readers precisely because raw data, processed tables, and code are not deposited. Second, the surviving critiques attach to the paper's headline claims, not its periphery. If FDR correction was not applied to the per-fraction ANOVA, the 78% figure — the manuscript's central quantitative pillar and the justification for the whole spatial framework — could change materially; that is a reanalysis, not a wording edit. Likewise, supplying a permutation null for Fig 9E and addressing the Day0/Day7 asymmetry are new analyses. A revision requiring re-run statistics on the primary result and mandatory data deposition exceeds what 'minor' should signal.

What keeps this from 'reject' is that all identified problems are fixable with existing data and that the VPS35 result — corroborated across LC-MS, immunoblot, and a null in bulk meta-analysis, and conceded even by the skeptic — is a real, publishable demonstration of the paper's thesis that survives regardless of how the causal language is softened.

Required for acceptance: (1) state and, if necessary, apply multiple-testing correction to all differential analyses (ANOVA per fraction, Tau r>0.7 correlations, GO families) and report how the 78% and 124-protein figures change; (2) deposit raw MS data with an accession, all processed tables, and analysis code; specify WGCNA parameters and missing-value filtering; (3) supply a permutation/null model for the Fig 9E human–mouse overlap; (4) justify or sensitivity-test the Day0 pooling and reconcile the t-test vs ANOVA discrepancy between Methods and figure legends; (5) soften causal language throughout ('consistent with' for mislocalization/NCT dysfunction; drop or requalify 'pre-pathological' and 'initiating' given 2-month Aβ42 elevation); (6) move the AsymAD control composition prominently adjacent to the main claim and, if power permits, report a sensitivity comparison; (7) add funding, competing-interests, data-availability, IRB/consent, and IACUC protocol statements; (8) address clarity items — consistent group nomenclature, self-contained figure legends, error bars and n in Figs 3B and 8E/9A, and resolve the duplicate Eftekharzadeh citation and the Seyfried APP attribution.
