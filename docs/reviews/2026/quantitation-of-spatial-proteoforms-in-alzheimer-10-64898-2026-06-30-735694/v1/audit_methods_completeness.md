# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript employs multiple high-throughput and targeted methods: subcellular fractionation of human postmortem brain and mouse cortex, TMT-based quantitative proteomics via LC-MS, QUAD (protein degradation labeling via AHA), WGCNA clustering, immunoblotting, and ELISA. Key categories triggered are model organisms (transgenic mice), human subjects (postmortem brain samples), antibodies (immunoblots), mass spectrometry (TMT-LC-MS proteomics), and chemicals/reagents (AHA, TMT, click-chemistry reagents). Most core methods are described with reasonable detail, but several critical protocol delegations lack resolvable citations, and some reagent identifiers (particularly for antibodies and the AHA diet preparation) are incomplete or unverifiable. Sample sizes are stated for human (n=27) and mouse cohorts, but statistical test details and error-bar definitions are inconsistently reported. No data-availability or code-availability statement is provided. The manuscript is a preprint (bioRxiv) with a stated posting date of July 4, 2026, but no DOI resolution or data repository accession is mentioned.

- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Sex of animals** — 'Both females and male mice were used for this study.' Day7 samples pooled from 'one male and one female mouse cortex' per replicate.
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Age at treatment/analysis** — Mice analyzed at 2, 5, and 12 months of age.
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Sample size (n) per group and definition (biological vs technical replicates)** — 'For each timepoint, the Day0 samples for each genotype with an equal number of male and female mice were pooled for each fraction. - **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Instrument and acquisition mode** — 'Orbitrap Eclipse Tribrid mass spectrometer (Thermo). Samples were injected directly onto a 25 cm, 100 μm ID column packed with BEH 1.7 μm C18 resin (Waters). Samples were separated at a flow rate of 300 nL/min on an EasynLC 1200 (Thermo).' Acquisition: 'Full MS1 scans were collected in the Orbitrap at 120k resolution. The cycle time was set to 3 s... CID MS/MS in the ion trap. MS3 analysis with multinotch isolation (SPS3) was utilized for detection of TMT reporter ions at 60k resolution.'
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Sample preparation, digestion, and enrichment protocol** — Protein precipitation with methanol/chloroform, reduction with TCEP, alkylation with chloro-iodoacetamide, trypsin digestion overnight at 37°C. For AHA samples: click chemistry with biotin-alkyne DADPS, neutravidin bead enrichment. Protocols described in main text and supplementary methods.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Search engine and version** — 'ProteomeDiscoverer 2.5 was used to analyze the MS/MS data to identify and quantified peptides.'
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Protein database and version** — 'The MS spectra was searched using the Uniprot mouse protein database with isoforms (version v2024-01-24) or Uniprot human protein database with isoforms (version v2022-08-03) and a common contaminant proteins list.'
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] FDR threshold and modifications searched** — 'The decoy database was the reverse of this Uniprot database to filter identifications to a 1% FDR.' Static modifications: 'TMT on lysine and peptide N-terminal (+304.2071Da) and cysteine carbamidomethylation (+57.021464 Da) for the human analysis. For the mouse analysis, an additional differential modification on methionine (79.0711) was searched for the DADPS modification on AHA.'

- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Quantification method (TMT reporter ion integration, normalization)** — 'Reporter ion distributions specific to the lot number of the TMT reagent were employed as correction factors.' TMT 16plex used; reference channels created by pooling peptides from all samples.
- **[Chemicals / drugs / reagents] TMT reagent lot and batch details** — 'TMT 16plex 0.5mg vials were resuspended in 20ul of acetonitrile.' Lot-specific correction factors applied, but specific lot numbers not listed in the manuscript.
- **[Chemicals / drugs / reagents] Protease and phosphatase inhibitor cocktails** — 'phosphatase and protease inhibitors (Pierce)' and 'protease and phosphatase inhibitors (Roche, Indianapolis, Indiana)' mentioned; specific product names/catalog numbers not provided.
