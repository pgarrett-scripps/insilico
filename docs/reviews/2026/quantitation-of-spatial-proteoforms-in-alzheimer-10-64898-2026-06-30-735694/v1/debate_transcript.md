# Debate Transcript

## Advocate — round 1

This manuscript makes a genuine, well-supported contribution: it demonstrates that subcellular fractionation reveals disease signal invisible to bulk tissue proteomics. The headline finding—75% of proteins detected in >1 fraction yet 78% of significant AD changes restricted to one fraction (Figure 4C)—is not merely asserted but derived from ANOVA across 6,123 proteins and independently corroborated by WGCNA (Figure 2E, 3A), two orthogonal statistical frameworks the methodology reviewer notes 'converge on endosomal-lysosomal and protein-folding pathways.' The VPS35/VPS29 retromer finding is especially strong: LC-MS shows the P3/S2 reciprocal pattern, immunoblot independently confirms it (Figure 7D), and critically the unfractionated 'total' sample and a 7-dataset meta-analysis (ref 27) show no change—precisely the 'signal rescued from bulk noise' the abstract promises, and even the harshest reviewers (novelty, rigor) don't dispute the empirical pattern, only its causal interpretation. The QUAD cross-species validation identifying six proteins (BIN1, NSFL1C, HSPA12A, CACNA2D3, DNAJA4, DNAJA4/DNAJA4) at the 2-month timepoint, all independently linked to vesicle transport or proteostasis in prior literature, is a nontrivial convergence unlikely from noise alone.//Reviewers correctly flag that causal language ('mislocalization,' 'produced by,' 'initiating features') outpaces the observational design, and that AsymAD-heavy controls complicate interpretation. These are real but classic revision-stage fixes: soften abstract verbs to 'consistent with/suggest,' state the AsymAD composition prominently near the main claim, and add the missing FDR-correction disclosures the data_analysis reviewer requests. None of these undermine the core empirical contribution—the fraction-specificity finding and retromer/VPS35 result stand on their own regardless of causal framing.

## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate leans hardest on two numbers—the 78% fraction-specific figure and the six-protein QUAD concordance—but both rest on statistical foundations the data_analysis reviewer shows are unverifiable. The 78% claim comes from ANOVA across 6,123 proteins per fraction with p<0.05, and the Methods never state whether Bonferroni/FDR correction was applied. As the reviewer notes, uncorrected testing at this scale would yield ~300 false positives per fraction by chance alone—enough to substantially distort the very ratio the advocate calls 'not merely asserted but derived.' This is FIXABLE in principle (disclose or rerun with correction), but until then the headline statistic cannot be taken at face value, which weakens the paper's central pillar, not a peripheral one.

The six-protein QUAD 'initiating features' claim has a deeper problem: the reproducibility reviewer flags that Day0 samples were pooled (N=1) while Day7 kept N=4, an asymmetric design not discussed or justified, and the data_analysis reviewer notes the human-mouse concordance test (Figure 9E) has no null model—we don't know how many overlaps are expected by chance. Advocate calls this 'nontrivial convergence unlikely from noise alone,' but that's an assertion, not a demonstration; no permutation test is offered.

I concede the VPS35/immunoblot result and the meta-analysis null-in-bulk-data point—those are genuinely load-bearing and well corroborated. But the paper's two most quantitatively central claims currently lack the statistical scaffolding needed to trust the magnitudes reported, which is a FIXABLE but currently unresolved gap, not a minor nitpick.

**Key points:**
- The 78% fraction-specific claim (Figure 4C) rests on ANOVA with undisclosed multiple-testing correction across 6,123 proteins; uncorrected p<0.05 would generate ~300 false positives per fraction, undermining the paper's central quantitative pillar (FIXABLE via disclosure/reanalysis).
- The QUAD Day0/Day7 sampling asymmetry (N=1 pooled vs N=4) is undisclosed as a limitation and directly affects the six-protein 'initiating features' claim the advocate cites as strong cross-species convergence.
- The human-mouse concordance analysis (Figure 9E, 155 proteins) uses a post-hoc mathematical stipulation with no null/permutation model, so 'convergence unlikely from noise alone' is asserted, not demonstrated.
- Concession: VPS35/VPS29 immunoblot confirmation and its absence in the unfractionated meta-analysis is a genuinely strong, well-corroborated finding that survives scrutiny.
- These statistical gaps are fixable in revision but currently mean the paper's two headline numbers cannot yet be trusted at face value.
