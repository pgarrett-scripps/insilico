# Debate Transcript

## Advocate — round 1

The specialist reviews converge on a clear verdict: this is a substantial, technically ambitious, and genuinely novel dataset whose core descriptive findings are real, but whose mechanistic language outruns the evidence. That is a revision problem, not a rejection problem. The methodology and rigor reviewers both concede the central data are sound — the VPS35 immunoblot (Fig. 7D) is repeatedly cited as a genuine strength, and the fractionation-before-TMT design is acknowledged as a real advance over unfractionated analyses. The ethics reviewer explicitly states the gaps are 'documentation gaps, not evidence of misconduct, and all are fixable with statements.' The literature and novelty reviewers found no preempting work and confirmed the load-bearing citations resolve to real sources. The path to acceptance is concrete and enumerated: (1) apply BH-FDR to the human ANOVA and recompute the 78% figure and discord enrichment on the filtered list; (2) fit a mixed model with mouse as random effect for the QUAD comparisons; (3) run permutation/hypergeometric nulls for the 78% claim, the 155-measurement overlap, and the six-protein 2-month set; (4) requalify 'mislocalization' and 'nuclear transport dysfunction' to acknowledge the insolubility alternative the authors themselves document (increased hydrophobicity, ubiquitination, 37 overlapping insoluble-fraction proteins); (5) add the missing ethics, funding, COI, and data-availability statements. None of these requires new experiments — they are reanalysis, requalification, and documentation. The one point the skeptics have genuinely established is that the mechanistic claims (mislocalization, initiating features) are not currently licensed; I concede that and fold it into the revision plan. But the descriptive contribution — a four-fraction human AD hippocampus dataset plus three-age QUAD degradation in the same two fractions — stands and is worth publishing.

## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate's core move is that every HARD issue is 'fixable by reanalysis or requalification, not new experiments.' That is true for some — FDR correction, mixed models, permutation nulls are reanalysis. But it quietly converts the paper's central claims into untested hypotheses. The headline that 'subcellular localization is a primary determinant of disease vulnerability' rests on the 78% figure, and the statistics reviewer shows that with ~4,500–6,000 proteins per fraction at α=0.05, up to ~40% of the 2,405 reported changes could be false positives. Recomputing 78% on an FDR-filtered list is not a cosmetic fix; it may dissolve the headline claim entirely. Likewise the 'mislocalization' of VPS35 and the retromer: the methodology reviewer is right that P3 is the NP-40-insoluble pellet, so the S2→P3 shift is equally consistent with insolubility — and the authors' own data (increased hydrophobicity, ubiquitination, 37 overlapping insoluble-fraction proteins) favor that reading. The immunoblot confirms the abundance pattern, not the mechanism. Requalifying 'mislocalization' to 'insolubility' is not a wording change; it changes what the paper claims. The six-protein 'initiating features' claim is untested against chance, and the 2-month mice carry mutations from conception — 'pre-pathological' means only low Abeta. The advocate concedes the mechanistic overclaims but then treats requalification as a trivial step; it is the difference between a descriptive dataset and the paper's stated conclusions. The descriptive data are worth publishing — but only after the load-bearing claims are either supported or explicitly demoted.

**Key points:**
- The 78% headline may not survive FDR correction — up to ~40% of the 2,405 changes could be false positives, so recomputation is not cosmetic.
- P3 is the NP-40-insoluble pellet; the S2→P3 shift is equally consistent with insolubility, and the authors' own hydrophobicity/ubiquitination/insoluble-overlap data favor that reading — the immunoblot confirms abundance, not mechanism.
- The six-protein 'initiating features' claim is untested against chance, and 2-month mice carry mutations from conception, so 'pre-pathological' means only low Abeta.
- Requalification is not a wording change — it demotes the paper's stated conclusions to descriptive findings, which is the real decision here.
