  - Fit: High-volume specialty journal with a clinical-research focus. Accepts comprehensive proteomic studies on disease mechanisms and biomarkers.   - Realism: Realistic (~35–45% odds) at current quality, provided the editors deem the retromer and 2-month mouse findings sufficient to carry the paper through a relatively brief review. The major verdict suggests this is marginal—expect major revision requests even if not immediately rejected.

  - Realism: Not a rejection fallback—a strategic option. bioRxiv is appropriate at any juncture; it ensures the work is publicly visible and citable while the authors prepare the final peer-reviewed submission.

  - Fit: Open-access general biology journal that emphasizes methodological rigor, reproducibility, and transparency. Lower novelty bar than selective journals, but high reproducibility bar—well-suited for this revised manuscript.
