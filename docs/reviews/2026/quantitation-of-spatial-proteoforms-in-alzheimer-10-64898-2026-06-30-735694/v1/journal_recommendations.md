# Journal Recommendations

## As-is (current quality)
- **Journal of Proteome Research**
  - Fit: The paper's core is a spatially fractionated TMT-LC-MS proteomics study of AD human hippocampus plus a QUAD degradation analysis in a mouse model — squarely within JPR's scope of quantitative proteomics and biological applications.
  - Realism: Moderate-to-good: the descriptive four-fraction dataset and orthogonal VPS35 validation are publishable, though the load-bearing mechanistic claims would need requalification to pass review.
- **Molecular & Cellular Proteomics**
  - Fit: MCP publishes quantitative mass-spectrometry studies with subcellular resolution and methodological depth, matching the fractionation-plus-TMT and QUAD design.
  - Realism: Moderate: the dataset is substantial and novel, but reviewers would likely demand the FDR and statistical corrections before acceptance.
- **Proteomics**
  - Fit: A specialty journal for applied proteomics that regularly publishes AD brain fractionation studies and would value the four-fraction human dataset and degradation kinetics.
  - Realism: Reasonable at current quality given the descriptive contribution, with the mechanistic claims scaled back.

## After required revisions
- **Alzheimer's & Dementia**
  - Fit: Once the mechanistic claims (mislocalization, initiating features) are requalified and the FDR/statistical issues resolved, the spatial-proteoform framing and human-mouse integration would fit this translational AD journal.
  - Realism: Moderate: competitive venue, but the corrected descriptive dataset plus orthogonal validation would be a credible submission.
- **Molecular Neurodegeneration**
  - Fit: The paper's ELP/protein-folding focus and the human-mouse degradation comparison align with this journal's interest in AD mechanisms and proteostasis.
  - Realism: Moderate: would require the load-bearing claims to survive reanalysis or be explicitly demoted to descriptive findings.
- **Journal of Neurochemistry**
  - Fit: A broad neuroscience journal receptive to quantitative proteomics of AD brain tissue and mouse models, fitting the revised scope.
  - Realism: Moderate-to-good after the required statistical and documentation revisions are addressed.

## Alternative outlets
- **bioRxiv**
  - Fit: A preprint server where the manuscript can be deposited immediately with full data availability, appropriate for the descriptive dataset while the mechanistic claims are being requalified.
  - Realism: Immediate; no peer review, but it makes the dataset citable and open to community scrutiny.
- **Journal of Alzheimer's Disease**
  - Fit: A specialty AD journal that accepts descriptive proteomic datasets and would tolerate the requalified claims if the headline venues are out of reach.
  - Realism: Reasonable: less competitive than the top-tier AD journals, with a receptive audience for fractionation-based proteomics.

## Notes
The manuscript is a strong descriptive dataset whose headline mechanistic claims (78% fraction-specificity, P3/S2 mislocalization, six-protein 'initiating features') are currently unsupported by the evidence per the panel; these must be requalified or reanalyzed before top-tier AD venues are realistic. In Silico itself is a plausible overlay-venue fit given the public-review model and the paper's checkable claims, but the authors' intended target was not scored here.
