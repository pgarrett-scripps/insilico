# Reproducibility Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript presents a spatially resolved proteomic study of Alzheimer's disease combining human post-mortem hippocampal fractionation with mouse QUAD degradation analysis. The work is ambitious and addresses an important question, but reproducibility is significantly hampered by incomplete data availability, missing computational details, and insufficient procedural specification. While the biological fractionation protocol is reasonably described, the LC-MS analysis, bioinformatic pipeline, and statistical thresholds lack critical parameters. Most critically, no statement indicates whether raw mass spectrometry data, processed protein quantification tables, or custom analysis code are deposited or will be made available. These gaps prevent independent replication of the core findings.

## Strengths
- The biological fractionation protocol is described with sufficient detail for reagents, buffers, and centrifugation parameters (Methods: 'Brain fractionation' section), enabling reproduction of the wet-lab fractionation step.
- TMT labeling and LC-MS instrument configuration are specified with model numbers (Orbitrap Eclipse Tribrid), gradient parameters (1–25% B over 120 min, etc.), and MS3 settings, supporting instrument-level reproducibility.
- ProteomeDiscoverer version (2.5) and database sources (Uniprot with specific version dates: v2024-01-24 for mouse, v2022-08-03 for human) are stated, aiding software reproducibility.
- Statistical methods are named (ANOVA, Student t-test, one-way ANOVA with Tukey's test) and some thresholds are given (1% FDR, p < 0.05), providing a foundation for reanalysis.
- Supplementary tables are promised (18 tables listed), which, if deposited, would contain quantified protein data and module assignments needed for figure reconstruction.

## Weaknesses
- No statement of data availability: The manuscript contains no Data Availability statement, no accession numbers for mass spectrometry data (e.g., ProteomeXchange, MassIVE), and no indication whether raw or processed data will be deposited. This is a HARD blocker for reproducibility of all LC-MS-derived results.
- Raw mass spectrometry data not specified as available: The manuscript does not state whether raw .raw files or mzML conversions will be deposited, nor does it provide a timeline or repository (e.g., 'upon publication' is insufficient per reproducibility standards). This prevents independent reprocessing of spectra through ProteomeDiscoverer.
- Processed protein quantification tables not explicitly deposited: While Supplementary Tables 1–18 are referenced, the manuscript does not confirm these will be made publicly available (e.g., as supplementary files, a repository, or a data portal). Without these, downstream analyses cannot be verified.
- WGCNA parameters underspecified: The Methods state 'WGCNA was performed by MetaNetwork' but do not specify critical parameters: soft-thresholding power selection method, minimum module size, merge threshold for module merging, or the correlation type (Pearson vs. Spearman). Reference 19 (Carr et al. 2022) is cited but the manuscript should state the exact parameters used here.
- GO enrichment thresholds inconsistently reported: The manuscript uses '5% FDR filter' in some places (e.g., Figure 3A caption, Supplementary Table 5) but does not state this uniformly across all enrichment analyses. The exact FDR method (Benjamini–Hochberg, etc.) is not named.
- Protein filtering criteria not fully specified: The manuscript states 'Proteins with missing values were removed from the analysis' (WGCNA section) but does not specify the threshold—was a protein required to have quantification in all samples, all fractions, or all timepoints? This affects reproducibility of the module assignments.
- Click chemistry and AHA enrichment procedure incompletely detailed: The Methods reference 'as previously described' for click reactions (ref. 18) and neutravidin enrichment (ref. 17). While these are resolvable, the manuscript should state key parameters here: click reaction temperature (stated as 30°C then overnight at 4°C, but incubation duration not specified), biotin-alkyne concentration, neutravidin bead wash conditions (number of washes, buffer volumes), and elution conditions (5% formic acid volume, number of elutions). Without these, the QUAD protocol cannot be independently executed.
- Day0 pooling strategy for QUAD introduces confounding: The Methods state 'For each timepoint, the Day0 samples for each genotype with an equal number of male and female mice were pooled for each fraction. Each biological replicate (N=4) for the Day7 samples were pooled from one male and one female mouse cortex.' This means Day0 has N=1 (pooled) while Day7 has N=4, creating an asymmetric design. The statistical implications are not discussed, and the Day7/Day0 ratio calculation (dividing each Day7 by 'the median Day0 protein TMT value') is unclear—does 'median' refer to the median across the pooled Day0 replicates, or across Day7 replicates? This ambiguity undermines QUAD result reproducibility.
- Statistical test specification incomplete for QUAD: The Methods state 'Student t-test determined the significance protein differences between Day7/Day0 WT ratios and Day7/Day0 AD ratios' and later 'P-values calculated by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's multiple comparison test' (Figure 8 legend). It is unclear which test was used for which comparison, or whether multiple-testing correction was applied beyond the log2 transformation mentioned.
- Bioinformatic pipeline order and decision tree not fully specified: The manuscript performs WGCNA, ANOVA, correlation analysis, and enrichment in sequence, but does not specify: (1) whether ANOVA was performed on raw or normalized TMT intensities; (2) whether proteins were filtered by fold-change before ANOVA; (3) the exact order of operations for the human-mouse comparison (Figure 9E: 'using these stipulations, we identified 155 protein measurements'—what was the exact matching criterion and threshold?). Without this, the pipeline cannot be rerun.
- Custom code not mentioned as available: The manuscript does not state whether custom scripts for WGCNA, correlation analysis, or human-mouse integration are available, versioned, or deposited. MetaNetwork and WebGestalt are external tools, but any custom R, Python, or Excel-based processing is not addressed.
- Supplementary table contents not fully specified in Methods: While 18 supplementary tables are listed, the Methods do not describe which contain raw data vs. derived results, or the exact columns/structure. For example, Supplementary Table 14 is described as 'All the proteins quantified with their Day7/Day0 protein ratios' but it is unclear whether this includes raw TMT intensities, normalized values, or only ratios.
- Figure-to-data mapping unclear for key results: For example, Figure 3C (protein folding network) and Figure 7C (spliceosome network) are generated from STRING database visualization, but the input data (which proteins, which confidence threshold for STRING edges) is not specified in the Methods. The Methods mention 'Edges represented physical interactions assigned by STRING database with 0.9 confidence' only in the figure legend, not in the Methods section.
- Immunoblot quantification method not detailed: The Methods state 'The immunoblots were quantitated as previously described' (ref. 7, McClatchy et al. 2012) but do not specify: band intensity measurement software, background subtraction method, normalization approach (total protein stain, loading control), or whether replicates were averaged. This prevents independent quantification of Figures 5B, 7D, and S3.
- Negative control and edge-case handling not addressed: The manuscript does not discuss how proteins with very low abundance, high missing-data rates, or extreme fold-changes were handled. Were outliers removed? Were proteins with <2 peptides in a fraction excluded from that fraction's analysis? The statement 'at least two peptides' applies to the global dataset but not necessarily to each fraction.
- No random seed or reproducibility statement for computational steps: If any step involved randomization (e.g., module detection in WGCNA, clustering in enrichment analysis), no seed is stated, and no statement indicates whether results are deterministic or averaged over runs.

## Questions
- Will raw mass spectrometry data (.raw files or mzML) be deposited in ProteomeXchange or a similar public repository, and if so, under what accession number and timeline?
- Will all processed protein quantification tables (Supplementary Tables 1–18) be made publicly available as supplementary files or in a data repository, and if so, where and when?
- What were the exact WGCNA parameters used (soft-thresholding power, minimum module size, merge threshold, correlation type)?
- For the QUAD analysis, why were Day0 samples pooled (N=1) while Day7 samples were kept separate (N=4)? How does this asymmetry affect the statistical validity of the Day7/Day0 ratio comparisons, and was this design choice justified?
- In the QUAD Methods, the Day7/Day0 ratio is calculated by dividing 'each Day7 protein TMT measurement by the median Day0 protein TMT value'—does 'median' refer to the median across the pooled Day0 replicates or across Day7 replicates, and why was median chosen over mean?
- Which statistical test (Student t-test or one-way ANOVA with Tukey) was applied to the QUAD data, and was multiple-testing correction applied beyond log2 transformation?
- For the human-mouse protein correlation (Figure 9E, 155 proteins identified), what was the exact matching criterion (gene name, Uniprot ID, ortholog mapping) and the threshold for 'opposite trends' (e.g., correlation coefficient, fold-change direction)?
- Are custom scripts for WGCNA, correlation analysis, human-mouse integration, or other bioinformatic steps available in a versioned repository (GitHub, Zenodo, etc.), and if so, at what URL or DOI?
- In Figure 3C and 7C, what were the exact input datasets to STRING (which protein list, which confidence threshold for edge inclusion), and are these inputs deposited?
- For immunoblot quantification (Figures 5B, 7D, S3), what software was used to measure band intensity, how was background subtraction performed, and how were replicates normalized and averaged?
- Were proteins with extreme fold-changes or very high missing-data rates treated as outliers and excluded from any analysis? If so, what were the thresholds?
- If any computational step involved randomization (e.g., module detection), what random seed was used, and are results deterministic or averaged over multiple runs?
