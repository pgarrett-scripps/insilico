# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
This is a substantial, well-motivated spatial-proteomics study, but as a clarity review the manuscript currently contains several HARD presentation defects — most seriously a figure caption that is swapped relative to the in-text references for the paper's central quantitative claim, a self-contradictory volcano-plot legend, and two numeric inconsistencies in the Results. These are fixable, but they currently block a reader from verifying the load-bearing numbers, so I recommend major revision rather than acceptance.

## Strengths
- The narrative through-line (fraction-specific vulnerability → discordant proteoforms → degradation kinetics) is explicit and each experiment's purpose is stated before its results, so the reader can reconstruct the question being asked.
- The central claims are stated plainly and repeatedly (78% fraction-specificity, retromer mislocalization, six early proteins), and the six proteins are named in the text rather than left to a table.
- Quantitative statements are generally concrete (e.g., 'five times more Abeta42 at 5 months compared to 2 months', '155 protein measurements (27 in P3, 128 in S2)'), which makes the argument checkable.

## Weaknesses
- The paper's central quantitative claim — '78% of significant AD-associated alterations were restricted to a single fraction' — is cited to Figure 4C, but the Figure 4 caption assigns GO enrichment to panel C and the fraction-frequency data to panel D, the reverse of what the text says. Because this statistic is the evidence for the headline conclusion that 'subcellular localization is a primary determinant of disease vulnerability,' the reader cannot verify it from the figure as labeled; the caption and text must be reconciled.
- Figure 4A's legend is self-contradictory: 'Red circles indicate the proteins with a p-value < 0.05 whereas black circles indicate a p-value < 0.05.' One of these must be p > 0.05; as written a reader cannot tell which population is which on the volcano plot.
- The Results state 'There were 238 proteins that were more stable... and 1,323 proteins less stable... In sum, there were 1,436 proteins with altered degradation.' 238 + 1323 = 1561, not 1436; the stated total does not follow from its own parts.
- The Results state 'We observed 37 proteins enriched in our AD P3 fraction that were also identified in one of these studies. Three of these seven proteins were APP, MAPT, and APOE.' The count is given as both 37 and seven; a reader cannot tell how many proteins the overlap actually contains.
- The 2-month mouse timepoint is repeatedly called 'pre-pathological,' yet the same Results section reports Abeta42 present at 2 months ('five times more Abeta42 at 5 months compared to 2 months'). 'Pre-pathological' is never defined operationally, so the claim that the six overlapping proteins are 'initiating features' rests on an undefined threshold.
- Supplementary Table 18's caption cites 'Supplementary Figure 9F,' but no Supplementary Figure 9 exists; the referenced panel is Figure 9E/F in the main text.
- Terminology is inconsistent across the manuscript: the non-AD group is 'non-AD (NA)' in the text, 'non-demented (ND)' in the Figure 1 caption, and 'ND' again in Figure 6A/B, with 'AsymAD' and 'normal' used as sub-classifications. The same group should carry one symbol.
- Significance-threshold legends disagree between figures: Figure 7F gives '*** < 0.005' while Figures 6E and 8 give '*** < 0.001'; the Methods '4 WT Day7 measurements' is duplicated where '4 AD Day7 measurements' is meant; and the Methods reads 'S2, P2, S3, and P fractions were lysed' (should be P3).

## Questions
- For Figure 4C/D, which panel actually displays the 78%-fraction-specific statistic, and can the caption be corrected to match the text?
- What is the operational definition of 'pre-pathological' for the 2-month mice, given that Abeta42 is detectable at that age?
- Is the overlap with the two insoluble-fraction studies 37 proteins or 7, and which is plotted in Figure 7E?
