## Concessions
- **data_analysis** — Multiple-testing correction status for the per-fraction ANOVA (driving the 78% fraction-specific claim) and for the Tau r>0.7 correlation screen is undisclosed, and with ~6,123 proteins tested uncorrected p<0.05 could yield hundreds of false positives.
  - Proposed change: We will re-run the ANOVA in each fraction with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction (and re-derive the 78%/2,405/1,905 figures), apply FDR correction to the Tau correlation screen (report how many of the 124 r>0.7 proteins survive), and explicitly state the correction method and threshold in Methods and in every figure legend reporting p-values.
- **reproducibility** — No data-availability statement, MS accession number, WGCNA parameter disclosure, or code availability is provided.
  - Proposed change: We will add a Data Availability section depositing raw .raw/mzML files and search results to ProteomeXchange with an accession number, upload all 18 supplementary tables as structured files, deposit WGCNA/correlation/human-mouse-integration scripts to a versioned GitHub/Zenodo repository, and add the exact WGCNA parameters (soft-thresholding power, minimum module size, merge cut height, correlation type) to Methods.
- **data_analysis** — The human-mouse concordance analysis (Figure 9E) uses a post-hoc mathematical stipulation for 'opposite trends' with no null/permutation model, so the significance of the 155-protein overlap is uninterpretable.
  - Proposed change: We will add a permutation analysis (randomly reassigning up/down direction labels within each dataset over 10,000 iterations) to generate a null distribution of expected overlap size and report the empirical p-value for the observed 155-protein (27 P3, 128 S2) concordance.
- **reproducibility** — QUAD Day0 samples were pooled (N=1) per genotype/timepoint while Day7 samples retained N=4 biological replicates, an asymmetry that is undiscussed and could affect the variance and power of Day7/Day0 ratio comparisons.
  - Proposed change: We will add a paragraph in Methods/Discussion explicitly justifying the pooling design (Day0 pooling controls exclusively for AHA incorporation baseline, not biological variance) and add a supplementary sensitivity analysis using individual (unpooled) Day0 replicates from a subset of timepoints where available to show ratio estimates are robust to this design choice.
- **reproducibility** — Methods state a Student t-test was used to compare Day7/Day0 ratios between genotypes, but Results/figure legends (Fig 8F, 9A) report one-way ANOVA with Tukey's multiple comparison test, an unresolved discrepancy.
  - Proposed change: We will correct the Methods text to accurately state that one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc correction was used for all QUAD genotype comparisons, remove the Student t-test language, and specify the family size (number of pairwise comparisons) used for the Tukey correction in each analysis.
- **rigor** — Abstract and Discussion use causal/mechanistic language ('retromer complex mislocalization,' 'nuclear transport dysfunction,' 'produced by alterations in protein degradation') that outpaces what cross-sectional abundance and degradation-rate data can establish.
  - Proposed change: We will revise the abstract and Discussion to replace causal verbs with hedged language (e.g., 'patterns consistent with retromer mislocalization,' 'suggestive of nuclear transport dysfunction,' 'may involve alterations in protein degradation') throughout, and add a limitations sentence in the Discussion explicitly stating that reciprocal abundance and degradation-rate correlations cannot distinguish active trafficking/synthesis changes from passive aggregation or altered solubility.
- **rigor** — The abstract calls the 2-month APPswePS1delta9 timepoint 'pre-pathological' and the six overlapping proteins 'initiating features,' but Aβ42 is already ~5-fold elevated at 2 months (Figure S4C), so the model is not pathology-free at this stage.
  - Proposed change: We will remove 'pre-pathological' from the abstract and Results, replace with 'earliest timepoint examined' or 'early, low-Aβ-burden timepoint,' and qualify the six-protein finding as 'candidate early perturbations that precede substantial downstream pathology in this model' rather than 'initiating features of AD.'
- **rigor / novelty** — The non-AD control group is 80% AsymAD (pathology-positive but cognitively normal), so the AD-vs-NA comparison is actually symptomatic-vs-asymptomatic AD, and the paper's inference that this strengthens dementia-specificity is stated only in the Discussion.
  - Proposed change: We will move the AsymAD composition (11/14 NA samples) and its interpretive consequence into the Results section immediately after cohort description (not just Discussion), rephrase the inference as a testable limitation rather than an advantage, and add a supplementary sensitivity analysis comparing AD vs. the 3 pathology-free 'normal' controls only, reporting whether key findings (VPS35, Tau-Braak correlation, retromer discord) replicate in this reduced comparison.
- **ethics** — No funding disclosure, competing interests statement, informed consent/IRB determination, or IACUC protocol number is provided.
  - Proposed change: We will add a Funding statement, a Competing Interests declaration, a statement confirming informed consent/IRB exemption status for the Shiley-Marcos ADRC postmortem samples (including exemption determination if applicable), and the IACUC protocol approval number for the mouse studies.
- **clarity** — Inconsistent terminology for control groups (NA, ND, AsymAD, 'normal') creates ambiguity about what comparison is being made.
  - Proposed change: We will standardize nomenclature throughout the manuscript and figures to 'AD' vs. 'NA (non-AD, comprising AsymAD and pathology-free controls)', relabel Figure 1's 'ND' to 'NA', and add a clarifying sentence in the first Results paragraph defining all group labels before they are used.
- **clarity** — Several figures (2E, 4A, 7C, 8A) lack sufficient legend detail to be interpreted standalone (fraction labels on volcano plots, node-size definition in networks, WGCNA clustering method, QUAD Day0/Day7 definitions).
  - Proposed change: We will revise the legends of Figures 2E, 4A, 7C, and 8A to explicitly label each panel/fraction, define node-size metrics used in STRING networks, state the WGCNA clustering method/distance metric, and define Day0/Day7 timepoints directly in the Figure 8A legend.
- **literature** — Apparent duplicate citation of Eftekharzadeh et al. (refs 45 and 86) with conflicting volume/page numbers, and an unsupported generalization attributed to Seyfried et al. (ref 82) about APP not being altered in unfractionated LC-MS.
  - Proposed change: We will verify and correct the reference list to remove the duplicate/erroneous Eftekharzadeh citation, and revise the APP-related sentence to accurately reflect what Seyfried et al. report or remove the attribution if unsupported, citing the correct primary source instead.

## Disagreements
- **methodology** — The fractionation protocol is 'not validated' for AD brain tissue because no marker-enzyme assays or immunofluorescence confirm compartment separation, so observed differences could reflect altered sedimentation rather than true redistribution.
  - Response: GO enrichment of proteins unique to each fraction provides orthogonal, unbiased validation that the fractions capture distinct compartments as intended: P3 enriched for nuclear complexes, P2 for mitochondrial components, S2 for lysosomes/vesicles, and S3 for ER. This pattern matches decades of established differential-centrifugation biology and is internally consistent across 27 independent human samples, making a systematic disease-driven confound in compartment assignment across all fractions unlikely. We agree formal marker-protein validation (e.g., LAMP1, TOM20) would strengthen this further and will add it as a stated limitation, but we do not agree the core fractionation scheme is unvalidated.
  - Manuscript reference: Figure 2D and TableS3... P3 was signiﬁcantly enriched in complexes associated with the nucleus, whereas P2 contained mitochondrial components... Lysosomes and vesicles were enriched in the S2 fraction, whereas the S3 fraction was enriched for the endoplasmic reticulum (ER).
- **novelty** — The 'spatial proteoforms' framework is not conceptually novel because subcellular fractionation, its application to brain tissue, and QUAD were all established by the authors' own prior work, so the paper is incremental rather than a new contribution.
  - Response: We agree the individual methods (fractionation, QUAD) are not new; the contribution we claim is the demonstration, at unprecedented scale in human AD tissue (6,123 proteins, 4 fractions, 27 subjects) integrated with longitudinal in vivo degradation kinetics across three ages in a mouse model, that most disease-associated changes are fraction-specific and that this specificity yields disease-relevant signals (VPS35/retromer, Ran-GTPase/NCT, six early-perturbation proteins) invisible to the seven unfractionated datasets we directly compared against. This synthesis, not the individual techniques, is the claimed advance, and we will state this explicitly in the Introduction rather than imply conceptual novelty of the fractionation approach itself.
  - Manuscript reference: A meta-analysis of seven proteomic TMT datasets on human unfractionated AD non-hippocampal brain tissue conﬁrmed 748 (31%) AD changes in our dataset... This aberrant VPS35 AD expression pattern... Consistently, a meta-analysis of seven published LC-MS datasets on human unfractionated brains failed to observe a change in VPS35.
- **data_analysis / rigor** — The VPS35/VPS29 retromer finding and its absence in the unfractionated total sample could reflect underpowered bulk analysis rather than genuine spatial mislocalization.
  - Response: This finding is corroborated by three independent lines of evidence converging on the same direction and magnitude: LC-MS peptide quantification (up in P3, down in S2), immunoblot on the same fractionated tissue, and a null result across seven independently generated unfractionated datasets from other laboratories (not merely our own bulk sample), which argues against a simple power artifact specific to our study. We will retain this as a load-bearing positive result but will add explicit hedging that mislocalization versus sequestration/PTM cannot be fully distinguished without imaging, as reviewers requested.
  - Manuscript reference: This aberrant VPS35 AD expression pattern between fractions was conﬁrmed by immunoblot analysis (Figure 7D)... Consistently, a meta-analysis of seven published LC-MS datasets on human unfractionated brains failed to observe a change in VPS35.

## Load-bearing critiques
- If FDR/Bonferroni correction, once applied to the per-fraction ANOVA underlying the 78% fraction-specific claim, substantially collapses the significant protein set, the manuscript's central quantitative pillar and framing ('subcellular localization is a primary determinant of disease vulnerability') would require a materially different headline result, not just softened language.
- If a permutation-based null model shows the 155-protein human-mouse concordance (Figure 9E) is not significantly greater than chance overlap, the cross-species validation and the abstract's claim of 'initiating features of AD' from the six early-timepoint proteins would lose its evidentiary basis this cycle.
