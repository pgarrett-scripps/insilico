# Related-Work & Citations Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript accurately cites foundational ferroptosis and lipid metabolism literature, appropriately positions its novel findings on trans fatty acids against contrary biochemical data, and uses primary sources for key empirical claims. No major citation defects or omissions detected.

## Strengths
- Accurately cites primary literature for key mechanistic claims (ACSL4, GPX4, FSP1, diPUFA phospholipids).
- Acknowledges and cites contrary findings (slower oxidation of trans PUFAs, trans-arachidonate suppressing ferroptosis) rather than ignoring them.
- Uses recent literature (2023-2025) for rapidly evolving areas like diPUFA phospholipids and FSP1-lipid droplet connections.

## Weaknesses
- The 2025 Cancer Research paper by Park et al. cited for MUFA suppression of ferroptosis could not be independently verified from abstract alone; authors should confirm it supports the specific claim.
- The 2026 reference to Joseph in 'Ferroptosis and Oxidative Stress' journal should be verified for publication status and relevance to FINO2/FIN56 mechanisms.
- The citation of Harayama 2023 (Biochimie review) for 'understanding remains incomplete' is appropriate but a primary research gap statement might be stronger.
- The FALCON screen method citation (Wieder 2023) is used for a different biological question (ferroptosis vs lipotoxicity); this is fine but could be clarified in the text.

## Questions
- Does the Park et al. 2025 Cancer Research paper directly demonstrate cis MUFA suppression of ferroptosis, or is it a correlative lipid composition study?
- Is the Joseph 2026 reference a published paper or a preprint, and does it specifically address FINO2/FIN56 mechanisms as cited?
