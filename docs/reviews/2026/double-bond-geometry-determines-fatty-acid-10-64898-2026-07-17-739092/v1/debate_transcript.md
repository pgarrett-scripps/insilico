## Advocate — round 1

This manuscript deserves acceptance. The unbiased FALCON screen (Fig 1B,C) identifying trans-FAs as top ferroptosis sensitizers — linoelaidic acid (trans-PUFA) and petroselaidic acid (trans-MUFA) — is a genuine discovery that overturns the dogma that MUFAs suppress ferroptosis. The mechanistic depth is exceptional: complementary SCD loss-of-function (CAY10566, Fig 3C,D) and gain-of-function (inducible OE, Fig 3E) prove petroselaidic acid requires SCD-dependent Δ9-desaturation to a trans/cis-PUFA, directly demonstrating double-bond geometry redirects metabolic fate. Lipidomics (Fig 6) reveals two distinct remodeling pathways converging on ferroptosis-susceptible pools: linoelaidic acid enriches diPUFA-PLs and sn-1 18:2-PLs (Fig 6E,F), while petroselaidic acid enriches arachidonate-PLs (Fig 6G-I). Orthogonal validation across inducers (RSL3, ML162, ML210, erastin-2, IKE, BSO), inhibitors (Fer-1, DFO, idebenone), and cell lines (H460, U-2 OS, HT1080, NSCLC panel, Fig 2, Supp Fig 2) rigorously establishes ferroptosis specificity.

The supraphysiological screen concentration (1 mM) is standard for discovery screens and hits were validated at 100 μM (Fig 2). The SCD mechanism is causally established by genetic epistasis — in vitro biochemistry would strengthen but is not required for the cellular claim. The generalization in the title/abstract should be qualified to "C18 fatty acids in cancer cells" but the principle is established. This work defines a new structural determinant of ferroptosis with therapeutic implications for SCD-high tumors.

**Key points:**
- Unbiased screen identifies trans-FAs as potent ferroptosis sensitizers, contradicting MUFA dogma

## Skeptic — round 1

The central mechanistic claim — that petroselaidic acid's ferroptosis sensitization requires SCD-dependent conversion to 6E,9Z-linopetroselaidic acid — is FATAL for three reasons the advocate did not rebut. First, the 'rescue' in Fig 3D is confounded: CAY10566 alone increases RSL3 killing (Suppl Fig S4G,H); the combination returning to BSA levels could reflect cancellation of opposing effects, not specific blockade of a metabolite. No experiment distinguishes these (Methodology reviewer). Second, the 'trans 18:2' metabolite is identified only by GC-FID retention time; no MS/MS or NMR confirms it is 6E,9Z-18:2 vs. other trans-18:2 isomers (Methodology reviewer Q1). Third, the phospholipid remodeling convergence (Fig 6) is purely correlative — no perturbation of ACSL4, LPCAT3, or MBOATs tests necessity of diPUFA/sn-1 incorporation (linoelaidic) or arachidonate enrichment (petroselaidic). sn-1 assignments are inferred from shotgun lipidomics without positional standards, yet presented as fact in Fig 7 and the abstract (Rigor reviewer). These gaps undermine the claim that geometry 'determines' metabolic fate.

Statistical rigor is absent for load-bearing claims: FALCON screen hits lack replicate number, statistical threshold, or multiple-testing correction (Data Analysis reviewer); Fig 3D equivalence claim has no n, test, or powered equivalence analysis; Fig 6 lipidomics has no stats or FDR for dozens of species. FIXABLE but essential.

Reproducibility: no deposition of lipidomics raw data, GC-FID chromatograms, or FALCON screen raw images/code (Reproducibility reviewer). FIXABLE but required.

Conceded: orthogonal validation of ferroptosis phenotype across inducers/cell lines (Fig 2, Suppl Fig 2) is strong. Abstract overstatement of petroselinic acid suppression (only with FSP1i, Fig 2G) is FIXABLE. Supraphysiological screen concentration is standard for discovery; validation at 100 μM is adequate.

Verdict: mechanistic core not established; requires structural validation of metabolite, unconfounded epistasis, and causal remodeling tests before acceptance.

## Skeptic — round 2

The advocate concedes the orthogonal ferroptosis validation (Fig 2, Suppl Fig 2) — accepted. Three load-bearing flaws remain FATAL:

1. **SCD mechanism unconfounded?** Fig 3E lacks WT+dox control (Data Analysis reviewer). Without it, "potentiation" could reflect doxycycline toxicity or off-target SCD effects. Fig 3D's "rescue to BSA levels" is still consistent with cancellation of opposing effects (SCDi pro-ferroptotic + petroselaidic acid pro-ferroptotic = BSA). Gain-of-function does not resolve this ambiguity.

2. **Metabolite structurally confirmed?** No. GC-FID retention time + SCD dependence ≠ 6E,9Z-linopetroselaidic acid. Could be 6E,12E-18:2 (elongation/desaturation) or other trans-18:2 isomers. Methodology reviewer Q1 unanswered. Abstract claims "directly demonstrating that double-bond geometry can redirect fatty acid metabolic fate through altered recognition by lipid metabolic enzymes" — overreach without structural ID.

3. **Remodeling convergence causal?** Fig 6E–I shows correlation only. sn-1 assignments inferred from shotgun lipidomics without positional standards (Rigor reviewer), yet Fig 7 and abstract present as fact. No perturbation of ACSL4/LPCAT3/MBOATs tests necessity. "Converge on phospholipid remodeling that expanded pools" (Abstract) presents correlation as mechanism.

Statistical rigor absent for screen hits (no replicates/FDR), Fig 3D equivalence (no n/test), Fig 6 lipidomics (no stats/FDR) — Data Analysis reviewer shows these are pervasive, not "standard revision items." Reproducibility: zero data deposition (Reproducibility reviewer).
