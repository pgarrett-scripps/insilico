## Disagreements
- **methodology** — Primary FALCON screen tests only two cancer cell lines with single GPX4 inhibitor, limiting generalizability
  - Response: The FALCON screen was designed as a discovery platform; hits were extensively validated across 6 ferroptosis inducers (RSL3, ML162, ML210, erastin-2, IKE, BSO), 3 ferroptosis inhibitors (Fer-1, DFO, idebenone), and 12 cell lines including NSCLC panel, HT1080, HEK293, MEFs, and primary elephant seal endothelial cells (Figures 2, S1, S2). This orthogonal validation exceeds typical screen follow-up and establishes generalizability beyond the initial two lines.
  - Manuscript reference: Linoelaidic acid and petroselaidic acid also sensitized cells to the chemically distinct GPX4 inhibitors ML162 and ML210 (Supplementary Figure S1E,F)... pre-treatment with linoelaidic acid sensitized a broad panel of cell lines to RSL3, indicating that the ferroptosis-enhancing activity of trans fatty acids is not restricted to H460 and U-2 OS cells (Supplementary Figure S2H-J).
- **methodology** — Metabolic tracing uses endpoint GC-FID without isotope tracing or flux analysis
  - Response: The temporal GC-FID profiling (Figure 3A, Supplementary Figure 4) at multiple timepoints (0, 4, 8, 24 hr) provides kinetic evidence for metabolic conversion. While isotope tracing would add flux quantification, the SCD-dependence (Figure 3C,D,E) and regioselectivity argument (Figure 3B) establish the pathway. The claim that linoleic acid does not convert to arachidonic acid in 24h is supported by direct measurement (Supplementary Figure 4B) and FADS2 inhibition having no effect (Supplementary Figure 4C).
  - Manuscript reference: Inhibition of FADS2... had no effect on RSL3 dose response in BSA, linoleic acid, linoelaidic acid, or petroselaidic acid pre-treated H460 cells (Supplementary Figure S4C).
- **methodology** — Targeted oxidized phospholipid analysis restricted to PC species with standards, omitting PE/PI classes
  - Response: We acknowledge this limitation in the Discussion: 'interpretation of these results is limited by the targeted nature of the assay... it remains possible that oxidation of additional phospholipid classes... contributes to its ferroptosis-sensitizing activity.' The PC hydroperoxide data nevertheless reveal a clear stereochemical signature (Figure 5D) supporting trans-fatty acid incorporation and oxidation. Expansion to PE/PI classes is a future direction.
  - Manuscript reference: Given our earlier finding that petroselaidic acid undergoes SCD-dependent conversion and promotes extensive phospholipid remodeling, it remains possible that oxidation of additional phospholipid classes or molecular species... contributes to its ferroptosis-sensitizing activity.
- **novelty** — Petroselaidic acid's robust ferroptosis sensitization not fully reflected in targeted oxidation data
  - Response: This is explicitly acknowledged as a limitation: 'the comparative lack of oxidation detected following petroselaidic acid treatment suggests that its ferroptosis-sensitizing activity likely reflects broader metabolic remodeling... not reflected in our targeted analysis.' The lipidomics data (Figure 6H,I) show petroselaidic acid remodels multiple phospholipid classes toward arachidonate-containing species, providing a mechanistic basis for sensitization beyond the measured PC hydroperoxides.
  - Manuscript reference: the comparative lack of oxidation detected following petroselaidic acid treatment suggests that its ferroptosis-sensitizing activity likely reflects broader metabolic remodeling of the membrane lipidome that is not reflected in our targeted analysis.

## Load-bearing critiques
- SCD-dependent conversion mechanism for petroselaidic acid requires unconfounded genetic epistasis (WT+dox control, catalytic-dead rescue) and structural validation of 6E,9Z-linopetroselaidic acid metabolite
- Phospholipid remodeling convergence claims require either causal perturbation experiments (ACSL4/LPCAT3/MBOAT KD) or explicit qualification as correlative with tempered causal language in Abstract/title/Figure 7
- Statistical rigor must be added for all load-bearing claims: FALCON screen hit-calling criteria, Figure 3D equivalence test, Figure 6 lipidomics FDR correction
