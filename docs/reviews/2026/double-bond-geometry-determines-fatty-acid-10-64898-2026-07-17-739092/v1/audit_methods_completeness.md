# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript describes a study on how fatty acid double-bond geometry influences ferroptosis sensitivity through metabolic fate and phospholipid remodeling. Methods include a fatty acid screen (FALCON), cell viability assays, lipidomics (GC-FID, LC-MS/MS), immunoblotting, lipid droplet imaging, flow cytometry, and live-cell imaging. Several reagent identifiers and protocol details are missing or unverifiable, particularly for cell line authentication, plasmid constructs, mass spectrometry repository deposition, microscopy instrument settings, flow cytometry gating strategy, and delegated protocols referenced without full details.
