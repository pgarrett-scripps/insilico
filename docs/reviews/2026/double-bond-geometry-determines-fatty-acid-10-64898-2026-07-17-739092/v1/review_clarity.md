# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript reports an important discovery that trans-unsaturated fatty acids are potent ferroptosis sensitizers and that double-bond geometry redirects metabolic fate via SCD-dependent desaturation and distinct phospholipid remodeling pathways. However, significant clarity defects impede evaluation of key evidence: Figure 3A's caption contradicts the described time course and duplicates a lipid species, the sn-1 positional assignment from shotgun lipidomics lacks methodological substantiation, and the abstract overstates the baseline effect of petroselinic acid. These are HARD clarity issues that require correction before the metabolic mechanism can be reliably assessed.

## Strengths
- The experimental logic progresses cleanly from an unbiased screen through orthogonal validation (viability, peroxidation, metabolic tracing, lipidomics) to a testable model.
- Multiple ferroptosis inducers, genetic models, and rescue experiments rigorously establish that the observed cell death is ferroptosis.
- The discussion candidly acknowledges limitations, including the modest oxidation signal for petroselaidic acid and the correlative nature of the lipidomic convergence.

## Weaknesses
- Figure 3A caption states "at time t = 0 hr" but the text describes "prolonged incubation" and the panel appears to show a time course; it also lists "trans 18:1, 18:2, and trans 18:2" with "trans 18:2" duplicated, making the actual measurements and timeline unrecoverable. This directly obscures the metabolic tracing evidence for SCD-dependent conversion of petroselaidic acid.
- The results claim quantification of "phospholipids containing 18:2 at the sn-1 position" by shotgun lipidomics (Lipidyzer), but the Methods do not describe sn-positional resolution for this platform, leaving a central structural assignment unsubstantiated.
- The abstract states petroselinic acid "suppressed ferroptosis," but the results show it only exhibited protection when FSP1 was pharmacologically inhibited (Figure 2G), overstating the baseline finding and misrepresenting the cis/trans comparison.
- Quantitative claims throughout the Results use vague qualifiers ("substantially greater," "markedly increased," "pronounced leftward shift\)) without reporting fold-changes or effect sizes in the main text, forcing readers to infer magnitude from figures.
- The FALCON screen used 1 mM free fatty acid — a supraphysiological concentration — without justification or discussion of physiological relevance, limiting interpretation of the screen's hit selection.
- Figure 2E caption describes "dose responses" for cell death inhibitors but lists only single concentrations, creating confusion about what the panel displays.
- The FALCON acronym expansion uses non-standard capitalization ("ONtologies\)) that may distract readers.

## Questions
- What time points does Figure 3A actually represent, and can the caption be corrected to match both the data and the text's description of prolonged incubation?
- Does the Lipidyzer shotgun lipidomics platform resolve sn-positional isomers, and if so, is this documented in the cited method or a supplementary protocol?
- Were the commercial trans fatty acid preparations (linoelaidic acid, petroselaidic acid) verified for purity and absence of cis isomers that could confound the stereochemistry-specific effects?
