# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript discloses human-subjects research involving clinical biospecimen-derived profiles from melanoma patients. The authors state that ethics and IRB oversight are handled by the collaborating clinical institution and that a data transfer agreement is in place (Appendix G.3), but the specific IRB committee name, protocol number, and informed-consent status are withheld for double-blind review and promised in the camera-ready version. This is a HARD compliance gap: an IRB approval identifier and explicit consent statement are required before publication, not deferred to camera-ready. The de-identification and data-use safeguards are adequately described, and the authors transparently acknowledge the privacy restrictions preventing public release. No competing interests, funding sources, or authorship-integrity issues are evident. The work is otherwise compliant on secondary-data and data-sharing fronts.

## Strengths
- Explicit statement that ethics and IRB oversight are in place and handled by the collaborating institution (Appendix G.3).
- Clear data transfer agreement documented between institutions (Appendix G.3).
- Transparent disclosure that cohort tensors are de-identified clinical biospecimen-derived profiles under a proprietary agreement and cannot be redistributed publicly (Appendix G.1).
- Appropriate safeguards stated for handling sensitive human data: anonymization of institution and collaborator identifiers for double-blind review (Section 4.1, Appendix G.3).
- No evident conflicts of interest, funding disclosures, or authorship-integrity violations raised by the text.

## Weaknesses
- HARD: IRB committee name and protocol number are withheld for double-blind review and deferred to camera-ready version (Appendix G.3: 'For double-blind review, identifying protocol numbers and committee names are withheld'). IRB approval identifiers are a required disclosure before publication, not a camera-ready addition; this must be provided now or the manuscript cannot proceed to acceptance.
- HARD: No explicit informed-consent statement is present. The manuscript states that 'Ethics and IRB oversight for human biospecimens are handled by the collaborating clinical institution' but does not confirm whether informed consent was obtained, waived, or covered under a broad biobank protocol. This must be explicitly stated.
- SOFT: Funding sources are not disclosed anywhere in the manuscript. While the work may be unfunded or internally supported, an explicit statement ('no external funding' or listing of grants) should appear in a standard location (e.g., acknowledgements or a funding statement).

## Questions
- What is the name of the IRB committee and the protocol number that approved this research? These identifiers must be disclosed in the manuscript before publication, not deferred to camera-ready.
- Was informed consent obtained from all participants whose biospecimens were used? If consent was waived, what was the justification and which IRB approved the waiver?
- Are there any funding sources, grants, or institutional support that should be disclosed? If the work is unfunded, please state this explicitly.
- The manuscript states data are 'under a collaborator institution's proprietary agreement' — does this agreement permit the secondary use of these data for this specific research purpose, and is that use documented in the IRB approval?
