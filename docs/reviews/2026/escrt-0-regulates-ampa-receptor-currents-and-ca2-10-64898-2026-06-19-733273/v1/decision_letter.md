# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel's numerical signal (3.12/5; 3 minor, 3 major, 2 reject) and the debate converge on a clear picture: the core findings are novel and the activity-dependence result (Bic up, TTX down) is well-supported by a clean manipulation, but several load-bearing causal claims are not supported by the evidence as currently presented. The most consequential problems are: (1) the AMPA/NMDA ratio is computed as peak AMPAR over NMDAR at 100 ms with no raw component amplitudes, no synaptic NMDAR data, and no surface GluA1 in the slice preparation, so 'Hrs levels impact AMPAR kinetics' is not earned as worded; (2) the LTD-like CaMKII/PSD-95/gephyrin claims rest on a constitutive developmental knockout (Hrsf/fSyn1-Cre, deletion from E13), and the acute shRNA/AAV-Cre arms are in different systems (culture/slices) and never measure CaMKII, PSD-95, or gephyrin, so developmental compensation cannot be excluded; (3) the phosphoproteomics reports 16 hits at p<0.05 out of 924 peptides, below the ~46 false positives expected by chance, so the GO/KEGG enrichment is built on noise and must be reframed as hypothesis-generating unless hits survive FDR correction. These are fixable with new analyses and requalification, so the manuscript is not rejected, but the revision requires substantive work. The advocate's claim that the acute arms resolve the knockout confound conflates two different experimental systems and is not accepted. Reproducibility gaps (raw electrophysiology traces, live PXD accession, per-replicate biotinylation values) and the missing IACUC protocol number are deposit/reporting tasks folded into the required revisions. The clarity defects (unparseable enrichment sentence, undefined legend symbols, direction-without-quantity claims) are minor and listed as suggestions.

## Required Revisions
1. Report the raw AMPAR and NMDAR current amplitudes separately (not just the ratio), with both components measured at matched time points, and state the per-group n, effect sizes, and whether comparisons are paired or unpaired. If the reduction is not resolved to the AMPAR component, requalify the claim 'Hrs levels impact AMPAR kinetics' accordingly.
2. Address the AMPA/NMDA ratio confound: provide synaptic (not whole-cortex lysate) NMDAR subunit data and/or surface GluA1 measured in the same slice preparation, or explicitly state the limitation that a reduced ratio could reflect increased NMDAR current.
3. Resolve the developmental-knockout confound for the CaMKII/PSD-95/gephyrin claims: either measure these proteins in the acute shRNA or AAV-Cre systems (which currently measure neither), or requalify the LTD-like interpretation to acknowledge that the constitutive Hrsf/fSyn1-Cre deletion (from E13) cannot separate acute Hrs function from developmental compensation.
4. Apply multiple-testing correction (e.g., Benjamini-Hochberg) to the phosphoproteomics and report how many of the 16 differentially abundant phosphopeptides survive; if none survive, reframe the phosphoproteomics and its GO/KEGG enrichment as hypothesis-generating rather than supporting glutamatergic transmission.
5. Deposit the raw electrophysiology traces and per-cell dataset (rise time, decay slope, AMPA/NMDA ratio, slow-current classification) with the analysis script, and confirm the PXD073715 accession is live with the TMT normalization and search parameters consolidated into a single reproducible pipeline document.
6. For the 'fast vs slow' AMPAR EPSC classification, report the per-sweep distribution of decay times across all neurons (rather than the dichotomized split), demonstrate that stimulation parameters and recording-quality metrics (series resistance, input resistance) were matched between groups and that slow currents are not polysynaptic (e.g., low-intensity or paired-pulse stimulation), and state whether classification was performed blind.
7. Report the per-replicate values and n for the surface-biotinylation experiment (absolute surface signal and input GluA1 separately, given the large SEM of 50 ± 7% vs 90 ± 24%), and state transduction efficiency for the Cre vs YFP arms.
8. Provide the IACUC protocol number(s) covering the mouse experiments, rat primary cultures, and organotypic slice work.
9. For the 'no difference' claims (PKCα/γ, PP2B, PKA substrates unchanged), either add a power analysis or equivalence test, or requalify these as 'no significant difference detected in this sample size'.

## Minor Suggestions
- Fix the unparseable sentence on Hrs enrichment in S3/S1 — state explicitly what the bracketed values are (ratios, normalized intensities) and what they are compared against.
- Define all symbols and abbreviations at first use in figure legends (S1, S3, PSD2, CHMP2B) and define the color coding in the Figure 3 traces.
- State the actual quantity being compared in the activity-dependent Hrs claims (e.g., 'normalized Hrs/α-tubulin intensity') rather than direction plus p-value alone.
- Requalify the abstract 'sensor...fine-tune' framing: the activity-dependence experiment shows Hrs levels track activity, but nothing shows Hrs levels in turn modulate activity; state the closed-loop claim as a hypothesis.
- Differentiate explicitly from the authors' 2023 prion-disease paper (Lawrence et al.) by listing the specific non-disease findings this work adds, and acknowledge the presynaptic activity-dependence work (Sheehan 2016, Birdsall 2022) as the prior art the 'first evidence' claim distinguishes from.
- Confirm the sources of three specific attributions: the 'more than twice the affinity for ubiquitin' value (Mayers 2011), the Hgs mRNA regional expression claim (Tamai 2008), and the NMJ neurotransmitter-release finding (Watson 2015); re-cite if the sources do not report them.
- State whether the 2.7-fold Hrs overexpression is within or beyond the physiological range, and whether the pGluA1-S831/PKC effect persists at a lower, more physiological dose.
- Report the per-condition n and test used for the three-group activity-dependence comparison (untreated, Bic, TTX), and address the unequal variance across conditions.
- Report inter-rater or test-retest reliability for the STED pixel-classifier segmentation, and state whether colocalization and slow/fast classifications were performed blind.
- Report the raw per-sweep distributions and the number of cells in each fast/slow category per group, and reconcile the small scaffold changes (12-17% gephyrin/PSD-95) with the large pCaMKII reduction (60%) before describing an 'LTD-like' state.
- Add the foundational Hrs identification reference (Komada & Kitamura, 1995) and confirm every reference in the list resolves, since the parsed list carries no DOIs.
- State whether the phosphoproteomics KEGG hits support the glutamatergic claim specifically, given the enrichment is dominated by GABAergic-synapse and tight-junction terms.
