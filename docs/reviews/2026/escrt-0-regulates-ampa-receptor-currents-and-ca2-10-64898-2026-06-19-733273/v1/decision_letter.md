# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel (n=8, verdict distribution: 1 accept, 2 minor, 5 major; confidence-weighted score 3.56/5) appreciates the manuscript’s convergent evidence for Hrs at the post-synapse and its activity-dependent regulation, but identifies several load-bearing weaknesses that prevent acceptance without major revision. The phosphoproteomics analysis uses uncorrected p<0.05 across 924 tests; the 16 nominally significant hits are below the expected false-positive count (~46) and cannot support pathway-level claims without FDR correction. The AMPAR fast/slow EPSC classification lacks stated blinding and its thresholds are not justified; the subsequent kinetic analyses (rise time, decay slope) conflate population proportions with per-synapse changes. The surface biotinylation experiment lacks a Cre-expression control (AAV-Cre on WT neurons), so the attribution to Hrs loss is not earned. The AAV-shHrs and AAV-Hrs-HA constructs are not fully specified (target sequences, species, plasmid deposits). The bidirectional regulatory claim would require overexpression electrophysiology. The abstract states ‘reduced amplitude’ when only an AMPA/NMDA ratio is shown. These gaps are all addressable but require new analyses, controls, or qualifications. The editorial compliance audits found no HARD gaps; the required revisions above capture the concrete, checkable demands ordered by importance. The manuscript is not ready for acceptance as written, but its core findings are plausible and merit the opportunity for major revision.

## Required Revisions
1. Phosphoproteomics correction: Report adjusted p-values (Benjamini–Hochberg FDR=0.05) for the phosphopeptides in Figure 4. Re-do GO/KEGG enrichment on the set of hits that survive correction, or clearly state that the pathway analyses are exploratory and built on uncorrected candidates.
2. Electrophysiology classification: State whether the fast/slow AMPAR EPSC classification was performed blind to condition. Report the number of sweeps per neuron and justify the 10%/20 ms/25 ms thresholds. Re-analyse rise time, decay time and decay slope in the fast-only subset of each group and report whether the differences survive within that subset.
3. AMPA/NMDA ratio: Report the decay time constant of NMDAR EPSCs for both groups to exclude the alternative that Hrs depletion alters NMDA receptor kinetics rather than AMPAR abundance. Also report the AMPA/NMDA ratio using peak-to-peak measurement as a sensitivity check.
4. Surface biotinylation: Include an AAV-Cre on wild-type (Hrs+/+) neuron group to control for Cre-expression effects. State the number of independent biological replicates for every condition in Figure 3N. Report the interaction p-value (Cre × Bic) from the 2×2 design.
5. Reagent traceability: Deposit (in the manuscript or a public repository) the shRNA target and scrambled-control sequences, vector backbones, promoters, and titers for both the lentiviral and AAV knockdown reagents. Specify the species of the Hrs cDNA used in AAV-Hrs-HA, the tag position, and deposit the plasmid. Deposit the R scripts that produced Figures 4 and S5, and state the rule used to select the single UniProt ID when a phosphopeptide maps to several.
6. Causal language and bidirectionality: Qualify claims about Hrs ‘regulating’ AMPAR currents throughout the abstract and results to reflect that the electrophysiological evidence is from knockdown only. The word ‘bidirectional’ should be restricted to the Hrs protein-level data (Figure 2) and not extended to the functional consequences unless overexpression electrophysiology is added.
7. Correct the abstract: Replace ‘reduced amplitude’ with the correct measure (reduced AMPA/NMDA ratio). Quantify the LTD-like state language as ‘consistent with an LTD-like depressive state’ rather than ‘suggestive of LTD’.
8. Series resistance reporting: Report mean ± SEM series resistance and capacitance for included neurons in both groups in the electrophysiology experiments.
9. Multiple comparison correction for western blots: Report (or add a methods statement) whether any correction for multiple testing was applied across the many immunoblot comparisons, or justify the per-test approach.
10. Phosphoproteomics validation: The pCaMKII reduction should be checked against the phosphoproteomics data; if the parent proteins of the 16 hits changed in total abundance, report this to confirm the phospho/normalisation strategy is not confounded.
