# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This is an original research manuscript in neuroscience examining the role of the ESCRT-0 protein Hrs at the post-synapse. The claims are checkable from the manuscript itself and its deposited materials (mass spectrometry data deposited in ProteomeExchange, PXD073715). The work is within scope for In Silico: it is an empirical study whose conclusions can be evaluated from the presented evidence. The manuscript is complete, intelligible, and presents a coherent set of experiments (biochemistry, imaging, electrophysiology, proteomics, and in vivo manipulation). While the claims are broad and some may outrun the evidence, that is precisely what full review should assess rather than a threshold problem evident on the face. No grounds for desk rejection.
