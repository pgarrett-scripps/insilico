# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript is essentially compliant on ethics and compliance grounds. Animal research is the triggered category; the IACUC approval at UCSD is stated and names the committee, but no protocol number is given. Funding is fully disclosed and competing interests are explicitly declared as none. No new human data is presented — the human prion disease references are citations to prior published work. The only required fix is the protocol identifier.

## Strengths
- The animal approval statement is present, names the IACUC at UCSD, and states adherence to the NIH Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals.
- Funding sources are fully disclosed (NIH grants, Chan Zuckerberg Initiative).
- Competing interests are explicitly declared as none.
- Animal sex and age are reported for the mouse experiments (female Hrsf/f mice; male C57BL/6J for AAV transduction).

## Weaknesses
- [Load-bearing] The animal approval statement lacks the IACUC protocol number(s). The Materials and Methods state that all animal experiments were "approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC) at the University of California, San Diego (UCSD)," but no protocol identifier is given. This is a SOFT request per the review standard — the approval is named but the identifier is missing. Provide the protocol number(s) covering the mouse breeding and experiments, the rat primary cultures (P1 pups), and the organotypic hippocampal slice work.
- No new human subjects data is presented; the human prion disease references in the introduction and discussion are citations to prior published work (Lawrence et al., 2023), so no consent statement is required for this manuscript.
- Biosafety approval for the AAV vector work is not stated; this is standard practice for AAV research and typically not a required manuscript statement, but worth a line if institutional policy requires it.
- The rat primary cultures fall under the same IACUC statement but should be explicitly covered by the protocol number requested above.
