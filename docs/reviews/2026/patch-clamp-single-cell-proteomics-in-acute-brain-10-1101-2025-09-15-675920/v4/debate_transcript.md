# Debate Transcript

## Advocate — round 1

The panel converges on one real weakness — the n=3 correlation and n=1-per-condition comparisons cannot carry the causal weight the text sometimes gives them. I concede that point fully: adjusted R²=0.998 on three points is a mathematical artefact, and the capacitance–protein correlation is confounded by retrieval damage (neuron #6 was aspirated before its during-retrieval capacitance was measured). The authors should report the in-situ-to-retrieval capacitance delta per neuron and scale every such claim to "three observations," not "a finding."

But the skeptic's case mistakes the paper's contribution for its weakest statistic. The contribution is the framework (Figure 1) and the shotgun collection strategy — and neither depends on the n=3 correlation being "true." The framework maps retrieval outcomes to interpretability; the shotgun design avoids the selection bias that data_analysis, methodology, and rigor all correctly praise. That is a real, checkable methodological contribution that stands regardless of whether capacitance predicts yield.

The honest reporting is itself a strength the reviews underweight: all 12 attempts including two torn neurons are analyzed, the 9/12 non-gigaseal outcome is disclosed, and limitations are candid. The "decoupling" claim (Fig 5C–D) is a null result in n=6 — but the authors themselves flag it as preliminary, and the data do disfavor the "torn neurons were simply smaller" alternative, which rigor notes.

The fixable items are procedural, not fatal: resolve the placeholder SI DOI, give the GitHub repo a commit hash, name the SynGO version and FDR method, report PCA variance explained. These are revision tasks. The framework, the honest shotgun design, and the deposited data justify acceptance of a proof-of-concept whose claims are scaled to its sample.

**Key points:**
- Concede the n=3 correlation is overfit and confounded by retrieval damage; require the in-situ-to-retrieval capacitance delta and scaled language.
- The core contribution is the Figure 1 framework and shotgun collection strategy, which survive regardless of whether the correlation is 'true.'
- Honest reporting of all 12 attempts (incl. torn neurons) and the 9/12 non-gigaseal outcome is a genuine strength, not a weakness.
- The 'decoupling' null is preliminary and the data disfavor the 'smaller-to-begin-with' alternative, supporting the authors' own hedged framing.
- Remaining issues (placeholder DOI, unversioned code, SynGO parameters, PCA variance) are procedural and fixable in revision, not fatal.

## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate's concession is more damaging than they allow. The paper's central empirical claim — the "quantitative bridge" between proteomics and electrophysiology — IS the n=3 capacitance correlation (Fig 3D). Strip that of evidentiary weight and the framework's only validated content collapses into a descriptive taxonomy (Fig 1). A taxonomy is a contribution, but it is not what the abstract claims: "patch-SCP can be used to assess soma retrieval" is presented as a demonstrated capability, not a schema.

The shotgun strategy does not escape this. As novelty and rigor note, its value depends on a standard for "good" retrieval — and that standard is defined by the same n=3 gigaseal neurons. Classifying #6 and #10 as "low-context" because they lack synaptic enrichment is circular: the reference set is the very cells whose enrichment pattern is being asserted as the norm.

The spike-integrity/synaptic association (Fig 4) is not merely underpowered at n=1 per condition — it is partly built into the design. Spike integrity and synaptic protein recovery are both consequences of the same retrieval damage, so the "association" is confounded by construction.

The "decoupling" claim (Fig 5C–D) is a null result in n=6; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, and pooling heterogeneous outcomes dilutes any real effect.

The 25–50% soma-loss estimate has no stated derivation. The placeholder SI DOI and unversioned code mean the load-bearing numbers cannot be recomputed.

These are fixable in revision — but only by downgrading the claims to observations, which is precisely the requalification the abstract and conclusions resist.

**Key points:**
- The 'quantitative bridge' claim IS the n=3 correlation; conceding it guts the paper's only validated empirical content, leaving a descriptive taxonomy.
- The shotgun strategy is circular: its 'good retrieval' standard is defined by the same n=3 gigaseal neurons used to classify other cells as low-context.
- The spike-integrity/synaptic association is confounded by construction — both are consequences of the same retrieval damage.
- The 'decoupling' claim is a null result in n=6; the 25-50% soma-loss estimate has no derivation.
- Fixable in revision only by downgrading claims to observations — which the abstract and conclusions resist.
