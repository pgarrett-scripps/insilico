DESK DECISION: proceed

This manuscript is a methodological proof-of-concept study combining patch-clamp electrophysiology with single-cell proteomics in acute brain slices. It is clearly within scope for an open overlay journal reviewing original research: the claims are checkable from the manuscript and its deposited data (raw mass spectrometry files, videos, code), and the work is presented as preliminary with claims scaled accordingly.

The central claim — that retrieval quality, rather than in situ electrophysiology alone, shapes proteomic recovery — is supported by a small but internally consistent dataset, and the authors are appropriately cautious about sample size and generalizability. The framework they propose is testable and the negative/ambiguous outcomes are reported rather than hidden. The manuscript is complete, intelligible, and includes sufficient methodological detail to be evaluated by specialist reviewers.

No threshold problem is evident on its face. The study should proceed to full review.
