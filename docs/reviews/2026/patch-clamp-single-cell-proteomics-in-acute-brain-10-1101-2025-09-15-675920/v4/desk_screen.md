# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This manuscript presents a methodologically sound framework for integrating patch-clamp electrophysiology with single-cell mass spectrometry proteomics in acute brain slices. The work addresses a genuine technical challenge—variability in neuronal soma retrieval and its impact on proteomic recovery—through systematic experimentation and honest reporting of limitations. The authors develop a conceptual framework that contextualizes different retrieval outcomes, demonstrate that soma capacitance correlates with protein yield, and show that retrieval integrity (not just in situ electrophysiology) predicts synaptic protein recovery. The study includes appropriate controls, comprehensive bioinformatic analysis using SynGO, detection of functionally relevant proteins (ion channels, GPCRs), and transparent discussion of compartmentalization and sampling biases. Methods are clearly described, data are deposited in ProteomeXchange, and code is made available. While the sample size is small (n=12 neurons) and explicitly acknowledged as exploratory, this is appropriate for a proof-of-concept methodological study. The work makes a meaningful contribution to an emerging field by clarifying how to interpret patch-SCP outcomes and should proceed to full peer review.
