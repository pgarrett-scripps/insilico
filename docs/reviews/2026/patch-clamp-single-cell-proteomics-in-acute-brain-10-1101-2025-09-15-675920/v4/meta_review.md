# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel agrees on two things. First, this is a transparent, honestly-reported, technically competent proof-of-concept: ethics (5@5) finds no compliance issues, reproducibility (4@4) confirms deposited raw MS data (PXD068359/MSV000099156), Zenodo videos, and specified DIA-NN pipeline, and methodology (4@5) credits the 'shotgun' all-outcomes inclusion strategy as a principled defense against selection bias. Second, and equally unanimous, the quantitative backbone is thin: clarity, data_analysis, methodology, reproducibility, novelty, and rigor all independently flag the Figure 3D capacitance\u2013protein correlation (n=3, R\u00b2=0.998, F=1577) as uninterpretable-as-presented, the undocumented '25\u201350% soma loss' estimate as an unsupported quantitative claim, and the absence-of-effect claims in Figure 5C\u2013D (n=6) as underpowered null results presented as evidence of decoupling.\n\nThe genuine split is between the higher scorers (methodology, clarity, data_analysis, reproducibility at 4) who treat these as fixable presentation/framing problems in an appropriately-hedged exploratory paper, and the lower scorers (rigor, novelty, literature at 3) who argue the abstract and title assert demonstrated, generalizable findings that the n=3 dataset cannot license, and that novelty positioning is compromised by the uncited concurrent Johnson et al. (2026) aspiration-patch-proteomics preprint.\n\nThe debate sharpened this. The advocate's strongest move \u2014 that the qualitative insight (protein counts \u2260 biological quality) survives independent of the fragile R\u00b2 \u2014 did not fully survive the skeptic's rebuttal: the same three gigaseal-preserved neurons carry both the capacitance correlation and the spike-integrity/synaptic-enrichment story, and methodology's HARD confound (neuron #4 is simultaneously largest, best-spiking, and most enriched) means soma size and retrieval integrity are not disentangled. That said, the skeptic conceded transparency, and explicitly labelled every one of its objections FIXABLE \u2014 by rewriting the abstract/title, adding the missing citation, and reporting the statistics honestly. No reviewer identified data fabrication, ethical failure, or an irreparable design flaw; the Figure 5 n=12 shotgun dataset, SynGO patterns, and torn-neuron negative controls retain value as a benchmarking resource even after the n=3 claims are downgraded.

## Decisive Factors
The confidence-weighted average (3.79, with 1 accept / 4 minor / 3 major) sits at the minor/major boundary; I land on major revision rather than minor because the required changes are not cosmetic. Three HARD issues converge and each demands substantive work, not copyediting: (1) the abstract, title, and Results must be rewritten to reposition every quantitative claim (capacitance\u2013protein correlation, spike-integrity/synaptic-enrichment association, 'decoupling' from underpowered nulls) as hypothesis-generating, with explicit disclosure of n=3/n=12 and the unresolved soma-size confound \u2014 rigor, methodology, and novelty all converge here; (2) the unsourced '25\u201350% soma loss' estimate must be derived or withdrawn (flagged independently by clarity, data_analysis, and rigor); (3) statistical reporting must be completed \u2014 named multiple-testing correction and family size for SynGO, PC variance explained, per-category n, descriptive statistics, and either leave-one-out/bootstrap sensitivity on Figure 3D or its demotion from a primary finding. Additionally, the Johnson et al. (2026) preprint must be cited and differentiated, and the advance over Choi et al. (2022) articulated (literature 3@4, novelty 3@4). Reproducibility's request to deposit the raw electrophysiology traces underlying the correlations is reasonable and should be met.\n\nI weight ethics (5@5) as clean but non-discriminating, and give methodology's 4@5 substantial weight \u2014 it is the highest-confidence substantive review and it rates the work positively while still logging HARD design limitations, which is precisely the profile of a paper worth publishing after its claims are brought into line with its evidence. Rejection is not warranted: the framework, the all-outcomes benchmarking dataset, and the transparency are real contributions to a field that needs quality metrics. But the paper cannot go forward asserting a demonstrated quantitative bridge built on three confounded neurons.
