The manuscript's honest reporting of negative results (Figure 3E, Figure 5C-D) and torn-neuron controls demonstrate scientific integrity rather than overclaiming intent.

## Skeptic — round 1

The advocate says the qualitative insight (protein counts ≠ biological quality) survives independent of the fragile n=3 capacitance correlation. That's not true — it's the same three neurons driving both stories, and the methodology reviewer flags a HARD confound the advocate never addresses: 'the design does not isolate whether poor synaptic enrichment in #6 is due to smaller soma size... mechanical damage... or biological differences.' Neuron #4 is simultaneously the largest, the best-spiking, and the most synaptically enriched — so 'spike integrity predicts synaptic recovery' (a headline abstract claim) is confounded with 'bigger soma yields more protein' throughout the entire n=3 gigaseal-preserved dataset. This isn't a separate weakness alongside the R²=0.998 problem; it's the same three data points wearing two hats, and the rigor reviewer independently confirms the abstract presents this as demonstrated ('we demonstrate that patch-SCP can be used to assess soma retrieval') rather than as an unreplicated, confounded trend. That is FIXABLE only by substantially rewriting the abstract/title and Results framing to downgrade every claim to hypothesis-generating — genuinely fixable, but it changes what the paper is allowed to claim about itself, which the current abstract does not do.

Separately, the unacknowledged concurrent aspiration-proteomics preprint (literature, novelty reviewers, both HARD) undermines the priority/novelty framing but is FIXABLE with citation and differentiation.

I concede the reproducibility/transparency strengths are real and not in dispute. But transparency about data doesn't cure overclaiming about what that data shows.

**Key points:**
- Same n=3 neurons drive both the capacitance correlation AND the spike-integrity/synaptic-enrichment story, so the 'independent qualitative insight' the advocate cites is confounded by soma size (methodology reviewer, HARD)
- Abstract/title state findings as demonstrated rather than as unreplicated, confounded trends (rigor reviewer, HARD) — fixable only via substantial rewrite of core claims, not cosmetic hedging
- Missing concurrent Johnson et al. 2026 preprint undermines novelty/priority claims (literature + novelty reviewers, HARD) but is fixable via citation and differentiation
- Concede: reproducibility and transparency (deposited MS data, videos, code) are genuine strengths not in dispute
