# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript describes a patch-clamp electrophysiology combined with single-cell mass spectrometry proteomics (patch-SCP) study in rat brain slices. Detected categories include model organisms, mass spectrometry (proteomics), and computational/bioinformatics analysis. The work is largely well-documented for the core techniques. Model organism details (species, strain, source, age, sex, n, IACUC approval) are present. Mass spectrometry methods are comprehensively specified (instrument, acquisition mode, sample prep, search engine with version, database, FDR, repository accession). However, several protocol-provenance items are delegated to citations without full verification of their contents from the manuscript alone, and some electrophysiology-specific parameters lack complete detail. Statistical testing and error-bar definitions are largely absent or unclear. Data and code availability statements are present but incomplete in scope.

## Categories checked
- Model organisms / in vivo

- Computational/bioinformatics analysis
- Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)

**HARD gaps (blocking): 4** · SOFT gaps: 2 · unverifiable: 2

## HARD gaps — reproduction blockers
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Randomization and blinding statement for patch-clamp recordings and sample selection** — No statement regarding randomization of neuron selection, blinding of experimenters during recording or retrieval, or blinding during proteomic analysis. The manuscript states 'we did not impose selection or exclusion criteria for either soma retrieval or MS analysis' but does not address randomization or blinding procedures.
- **[Computational/bioinformatics analysis] Statistical tests applied and error-bar definitions** — Correlations reported with F-statistic and p-value (e.g., 'F = 1577, p < 0.05, adjusted R² = 0.998, n = 3') but no statement of which statistical test was used (linear regression assumed but not named). GO enrichment uses 'Q-value < 0.05' threshold but the multiple-testing correction method is not explicitly stated (likely Benjamini–Hochberg but unconfirmed). Error bars are absent from most figures; ladder plots (Figure 3C) show individual data points without error representation. No definition of error bars in figure legends.
- **[Computational/bioinformatics analysis] Random seed or seed-averaging statement for reproducibility** — No mention of random seeds used in R, DIA-NN, or any other computational step. No statement regarding seed-averaging or reproducibility of stochastic analyses.
- **[Cross-cutting] Statistical test names and error-bar definitions** — Correlations reported with F-statistic and p-value (e.g., Figure 3D: 'F = 1577, p < 0.05, adjusted R² = 0.998') but the test name (linear regression) is not explicitly stated. GO enrichment uses 'Q-value < 0.05' but the multiple-testing correction method is not named. Figures lack error bars or their definitions; ladder plots show individual points without error representation. No figure legend explains error-bar type (SD, SEM, CI, etc.).

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Protocol reference for electrophysiology methods ('performed as previously described')** — 'Acute brain slices and electrophysiological recordings were performed as previously described [10, 26-31].' References [10, 26-31] are cited but their full contents cannot be verified from the manuscript alone. The manuscript does not state whether these references contain the complete protocol or only partial details. This is a delegated-reference situation; the core electrophysiology protocol is outsourced to citations.
- **[Protocol provenance] Electrophysiology protocol fully self-contained vs. delegated** — The core electrophysiology protocol is delegated to references [10, 26-31] with the statement 'Acute brain slices and electrophysiological recordings were performed as previously described.' The manuscript provides some details (slice composition, electrode fill, recording chamber) but does not fully specify the complete protocol in-house. Without access to the cited references, it cannot be confirmed whether they contain sufficient detail. This is a delegated-resolvable situation if the references are accessible, but unverifiable from the manuscript alone.

## SOFT gaps — recommended
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Power justification or sample-size justification** — No power calculation or justification for n=12 neurons. The authors acknowledge 'our limited sample size' in the Limitations section but provide no a priori justification.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Number of technical and biological replicates per sample** — Each neuron is analyzed once (n=1 per neuron). No technical replicates or repeated measurements of the same soma are mentioned. The manuscript treats each neuron as a single biological replicate (n=12 neurons total), but does not explicitly state whether any samples were run in technical duplicate or whether any quality-control replicates were included.

## Documented (for the record)
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Species, strain, source, age, sex, n per group** — 'Wistar rats (75 days of age, Charles River)' and 'All animal studies conformed to NIH Guidelines for the care and use of laboratory animals and were approved by the Scripps Research Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC; protocol no. 09-0006).' Total n=12 neurons retrieved across the study (neurons #1–12 mentioned throughout Results).
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] IACUC protocol number and approval statement** — 'IACUC; protocol no. 09-0006' stated in Methods under Electrophysiology.
- **[Model organisms / in vivo] Housing and animal husbandry details** — 'Wistar rats (75 days of age, Charles River) were given access to food and water ad libitum' in Methods.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Instrument model and acquisition mode** — 'Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometer (ThermoFisher Scientific) operating in data-independent acquisition (DIA) mode with FAIMS Pro using a single compensation voltage (CV = -50)' in Methods under LC-MS/MS Acquisition.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Sample preparation, digestion, and enrichment protocol** — 'Micropipettes containing the retrieved neurons were immediately transferred to a 384-well non-binding microplate (Greiner) containing 15 µL of 0.02% n-dodecyl-β-D-maltoside (DDM) in UHPLC-grade water (ThermoFisher)... Samples were digested at 37°C for 2 hours using 7 ng of sequencing-grade Trypsin (Promega), quenched with 2 µL of 0.1% formic acid, and stored at -20°C for later processing.' Peptide separation details also provided.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Search engine name and version** — 'Raw DIA files were analyzed using DIA-NN v1.8.1 in library-free mode with the "match-between-runs" option enabled' in Methods under Bioinformatic and Statistical Analysis.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Database name and version** — 'Searches were performed against the UniProt Mus musculus reference proteome (downloaded 2024)' in Methods. Note: the manuscript searched against mouse (Mus musculus) database despite using rat (Wistar) tissue; this is noted but not flagged as a missing item since the choice is stated.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] FDR threshold, variable modifications, and mass tolerances** — 'Protein identifications were filtered at 1% false discovery rate (FDR) at both the precursor and protein group level... with oxidation selected as a variable modification. Up to two missed tryptic cleavages were allowed.' Mass tolerances for DIA windows: '20 m/z wide with overlapping edges.' MS1 AGC target and injection time also specified.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Repository accession (PRIDE, MassIVE, or equivalent)** — 'The raw mass spectrometry data and search files have been deposited to the ProteomeXchange Consortium (http://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org) via the MassIVE partner repository with the MassIVE dataset identifier MSV000099156 and ProteomeXchange dataset identifier PXD068359' in Methods.
- **[Mass spectrometry (proteomics)] Quantification method (e.g., label-free, TMT, SILAC)** — 'Proteomic quantification was performed using DIA-NN's MaxLFQ implementation, which integrates peptide-level signals into protein-level intensity estimates across samples' in Methods.
- **[Computational/bioinformatics analysis] Bioinformatics tools and software versions for proteome analysis** — 'DIA-NN v1.8.1', 'R 4.3.1' with packages 'ComplexHeatmap, ggplot2, and UpSetR' specified in Methods. SynGO database used for GO enrichment (reference [16] provided).
- **[Computational/bioinformatics analysis] Code availability and custom script locations** — 'Custom scripts for figure generation and SynGO-based analysis are available at https://github.com/LarryThePharmacologist' in Methods.
- **[Computational/bioinformatics analysis] Data-availability statement** — Raw MS data and search files deposited to ProteomeXchange/MassIVE (accessions provided). Supplementary tables (protein-level DIA-NN output and SynGO results) stated to be available. Videos of patch-SCP attempts 'have been made publicly available in their full form on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18189812).' However, processed proteome matrices, PCA coordinates, and other intermediate analysis files are not explicitly stated to be available.
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Patch-clamp recording configuration and electrode specifications** — 'Whole-cell patch-clamp recordings were performed in neurons present in the medial subdivision of the PrL and clamped at −70 mV using a Multiclamp 700B amplifier, Digidata 1440A and pClamp10. Patch pipettes (3-6 MΩ) were filled with an internal solution composed of the following (in mM): 145 KGluconate, 0.5 EGTA, 2 MgCl2, 10 HEPES, 2 Mg-ATP and 0.2 Na-GTP' in Methods.
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Slice preparation protocol (temperature, composition, incubation)** — 'Coronal mPFC slices (300 μm) were prepared in an ice-cold high-sucrose cutting solution (sucrose 206 mM; KCl 2.5 mM; CaCl2 0.5 mM; MgCl2 7 mM; NaH2PO4 1.2 mM; NaHCO3 26 mM; glucose 5 mM and HEPES 5 mM) using a Leica VT1200S vibratome. Slices were incubated in 95% O2/5% CO2 equilibrated artificial cerebrospinal fluid (aCSF; NaCl 130 mM; KCl 3.5 mM; NaH2PO4 1.25 mM; MgSO4·7H2O 1.5 mM; CaCl2 2.0 mM; NaHCO3 24 mM and glucose 10 mM), first at 37°C for 30 minutes, then at room temperature for another 30 minutes' in Methods.
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Recording chamber and superfusion conditions** — 'Slices were then transferred to a recording chamber (Warner Instruments) and superfused at a flow rate of 2-4 ml/min' in Methods.
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Soma retrieval procedure and pressure/suction parameters** — 'During withdrawal of the patched neuron, light negative pressure (-50 to -140 mmHg) was applied through the pipette and adjusted to facilitate soma retrieval while preserving the high-resistance seal' in Methods. However, the exact protocol for breaking the seal, timing of retrieval, and detailed mechanical steps are not fully specified; reference is made to 'Videos S1–3' for visual documentation.
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Analysis software and parameters for passive/active properties** — 'NeuroExpress software (version 19.4.09.) developed and provided by A. Szücs was used for analysis [15].' Passive properties 'were determined for each neuron from linear fits and extrapolated at I = 0 pA using NeuroExpress' (Figure 3C caption).
- **[Electrophysiology (patch-clamp)] Definition of 'gigaseal preservation' and categorization of retrieval outcomes** — Figure 1 and accompanying text define three outcomes: (i) gigaseal preserved during retrieval, (ii) gigaseal lost during retrieval, (iii) no gigaseal. Torn/aspirated neurons are also categorized. However, the exact electrophysiological criteria for assigning neurons to each category (e.g., threshold for seal resistance, duration of whole-cell access) are not quantitatively specified.
- **[Protocol provenance] Soma retrieval procedure fully specified or delegated** — Soma retrieval is described in the Methods ('During withdrawal of the patched neuron, light negative pressure (-50 to -140 mmHg) was applied...') and documented in supplementary videos. The procedure is not fully delegated to a citation, though visual documentation (Videos S1–3) is the primary evidence of technique. The manuscript states 'Representative videos of soma retrieval for each neuron are provided as Supporting Information (Videos S1–3), showing live differential interference contrast (DIC) imaging during withdrawal,' making the procedure partially self-contained and partially visual-evidence-based.
- **[Protocol provenance] Sample processing and LC-MS/MS protocol fully self-contained** — Sample processing is fully detailed in Methods: 'Micropipettes containing the retrieved neurons were immediately transferred to a 384-well non-binding microplate (Greiner) containing 15 µL of 0.02% n-dodecyl-β-D-maltoside (DDM)...' through to MS acquisition parameters. No delegation to citations for this core method.
- **[Cross-cutting] Sample size n with clear definition (biological vs. technical replicates)** — n=12 neurons total (neurons #1–12). Each neuron is treated as one biological replicate. No technical replicates per neuron are mentioned. The manuscript states 'we visibly recovered 10 of 12 pyramidal somas' indicating the total attempt count.
- **[Cross-cutting] Software, tool, and instrument versions** — Versions provided for: DIA-NN v1.8.1, R 4.3.1, NeuroExpress v19.4.09, Multiclamp 700B amplifier, Digidata 1440A, pClamp10, Leica VT1200S vibratome, Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometer, Vanquish Neo UHPLC, IonOpticks Aurora XS column. However, some tools lack version info: ComplexHeatmap, ggplot2, UpSetR packages (R versions not specified); SynGO database version not stated (reference [16] provided but version not in Methods).
- **[Cross-cutting] Data-availability statement scope and completeness** — MS data and search files: ProteomeXchange/MassIVE accessions provided. Videos: Zenodo DOI provided. Supplementary tables: stated to be available. Code: GitHub URL provided. However, processed data matrices (PCA coordinates, heatmap data, protein-level quantifications beyond DIA-NN output) are not explicitly stated to be available. Supporting Information PDF and XLSX files are referenced but URLs/DOIs not provided in the main text.
