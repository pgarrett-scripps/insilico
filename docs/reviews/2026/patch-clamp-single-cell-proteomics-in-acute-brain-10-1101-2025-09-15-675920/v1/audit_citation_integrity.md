# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript contains 33 references spanning reference resolvability, claim–citation support, and quotation fidelity. Six references were confirmed as resolvable via PubMed/DOI with plausible claim support (Cadwell et al. 2016, Gatto et al. 2023, Koopmans et al. 2019, Zamponi et al. 2015, Demichev et al. 2020, Ghatak et al. 2024). Twenty-four references could not be verified due to tool search limitations but are likely resolvable via standard databases. One critical HARD issue was identified: Reference 31 (Guo et al. 2025 on saxitoxin synthesis) appears misattributed to the electrophysiology methods section and does not support the cited claim about acute brain slice preparation. One SOFT issue was identified: Reference 14 cites NeuroExpress software via ResearchGate, a non-peer-reviewed informal repository, rather than a formal publication or DOI. Overall, the citation infrastructure is sound for verified references, but Reference 31 must be corrected and Reference 14 should be upgraded before publication.
