# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
This is a purely computational modeling study with no human subjects, animal research, clinical trials, or biosafety-regulated work. The manuscript discloses funding sources (NSF, NIH, institutional support) and lists all authors with clear roles. No ethics-committee approval, informed consent, IRB review, IACUC protocol, or biosafety clearance is required or claimed. The work is in scope for In Silico and raises no ethics or compliance issues.

## Strengths
- Funding sources are fully disclosed in the Acknowledgments section with specific grant numbers.
- All authors are named with institutional affiliation and corresponding author contact information provided.
- The authors deposit code publicly on GitHub, enabling reproducibility and verification of computational results.
