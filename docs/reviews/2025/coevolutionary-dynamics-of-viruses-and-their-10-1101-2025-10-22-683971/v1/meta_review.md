# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel is unusually convergent: every specialist agrees the mathematical framework is sound, well-documented, and reproducible (public code, explicit parameters, extinction thresholds — reproducibility 4/4, data_analysis 4/4, clarity 4/4), and that the sensitivity analysis (10,000-sample LHS mapping four regimes) is a genuine, substantive contribution. Ethics raises no concerns. The disagreement is not about whether the model is competently built, but about whether its central empirical and mechanistic claims are supported by what is shown. Four reviewers (rigor, methodology, novelty, literature) independently converge on the same two structural problems: (1) the paper's only real link to experimental data — the DePolo et al. time-shift comparison in Fig. 6 — is a qualitative pattern match while the authors themselves admit the modeled resistance changes are linear-scale versus the orders-of-magnitude shifts observed experimentally; and (2) the paper never demonstrates that continuous phenotype-diffusion under strong mutation is *necessary* to produce the observed oscillations, rather than merely one sufficient mechanism among several (a discrete-mutation or simpler predator-prey null model is never run for comparison). Novelty and literature reviewers add that the framework is an incremental, if biologically well-motivated, extension of Alfaro et al. (2024), and that the abstract phenotype axes are never mapped to measurable biological quantities, leaving therapeutic claims aspirational. The debate sharpened rather than resolved this tension: the advocate characterized the DePolo mismatch and necessity gap as reword-and-frame fixes; the skeptic's rebuttal — that closing the DePolo gap requires actually building and testing a higher-dimensional 'corkscrew' extension, and that the necessity claim requires running an actual null-model comparison, not softer language — was not seriously contested by the advocate and lines up with what four independent reviewers already flagged. That critique survives contact with the advocacy and should carry weight.

## Decisive Factors
No reviewer recommended rejection (1 accept, 3 minor, 4 major; confidence-weighted mean 3.67/5), and nothing in the record identifies a fatal, unfixable flaw — the model's mathematics, reproducibility, and sensitivity analysis are solid strengths acknowledged across the panel and unchallenged in debate. That argues against rejection. However, the modal and confidence-weighted signal sits closer to 'major' than 'minor': the two load-bearing empirical/mechanistic claims (transient-resistance reproduction, and continuous-diffusion as the operative mechanism for strong-mutation coevolution) are flagged by four separate high-confidence reviewers as unsupported by the evidence given, not merely under-explained. The debate confirms these are substantive-work items — a higher-dimensional model test and a null-model comparison — rather than cosmetic rewording, which is why 'major' rather than 'minor' is the appropriate calibration. This tracks the panel's own verdict distribution (plurality major) and requires no fresh argument beyond what the specialists already raised.
