# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
From an ethics and compliance standpoint this manuscript is fundamentally sound: all human-derived materials come from named commercial vendors, no human-subjects or animal research is involved, and funding and competing interests are fully disclosed. The one substantive gap is the absence of a biosafety statement for work with live BSL-2 pathogens (GAS and GBS), which should be added. The pooled human serum source is named but its exempt status is not stated. Both are minor, fixable additions.

## Strengths
- Funding is comprehensively disclosed with grant numbers, and competing interests are explicitly declared as none.
- All human-derived materials (THP-1 cells from Sigma, pooled serum from Thermo Fisher) are traced to named commercial vendors, which is the correct basis for human-subjects exemption.
- Mass spectrometry data are deposited in MassIVE (PXD069795) with reviewer access, supporting transparency.

## Weaknesses
- No biosafety approval statement for live BSL-2 pathogen work. The central experiments infect THP-1 macrophages with live GAS M1T1 5448 (a clinical isolate from a necrotizing fasciitis patient) and GBS COH1 — both Risk Group 2 agents requiring BSL-2 containment. The manuscript contains no statement of institutional biosafety approval or the containment level used. Per the standard that silence is not compliance, this is a HARD gap: the authors should add a line naming their institutional biosafety committee approval and confirming BSL-2 practices. This is a one-line fix but is a required statement for NIH-funded pathogen work and for most journals publishing it.
- Pooled human serum: vendor named, exemption not stated. The manuscript uses "pooled normal human serum (Thermo Fisher Scientific)" for opsonization. Naming the commercial vendor is the right way to establish that the material is a commercial product, but the manuscript does not explicitly state that the product is IRB-exempt or that the vendor obtained appropriate donor consent. A one-line statement would close this.

## Questions
- Please provide the institutional biosafety committee approval and confirm BSL-2 containment for the live GAS and GBS experiments.
- Please confirm that the pooled human serum from Thermo Fisher Scientific is a commercial product exempt from IRB review, or provide the vendor's documentation of donor consent.
