# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
Nothing in this manuscript triggers an ethics or compliance review: the work uses a commercial cell line (THP-1, purchased from Sigma), a well-established GAS lab strain, and commercially sourced pooled human serum — no human subjects, identifiable data, clinical trial, animal work, or restricted-consent material. Funding is fully disclosed with grant numbers, competing interests are explicitly declared as none, and the proteomics data are deposited in MassIVE with an accession number. The only item worth a line is the absence of a biosafety statement for work with live GAS (a BSL-2 pathogen), which is a soft suggestion rather than a compliance failure.

## Strengths
- Funding sources are itemized with grant identifiers, and the competing-interests statement is explicit.
- Raw proteomics data are deposited with a working accession, satisfying the reproducibility expectation for this dimension.

## Weaknesses
- Soft: no biosafety statement appears for the live GAS infection work (BSL-2 agent); a one-line institutional approval note would be good practice, though this is not typically a journal requirement for this agent class.
