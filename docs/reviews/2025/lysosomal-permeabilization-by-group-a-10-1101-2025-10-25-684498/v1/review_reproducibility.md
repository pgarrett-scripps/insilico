# Reproducibility Reviewer

## Summary
This is a genuinely reproducible study as far as it goes — raw mass-spec data are deposited in MassIVE with reviewer credentials, the search parameters are given in unusual detail, and the isolation protocol is specified end-to-end. But the post-search analysis pipeline (normalization, imputation, filtering) is described only in prose, with no deposited code and two undefined thresholds, and one step in the isolation (the 30 kDa size cut) is ambiguous in a way that touches the paper's headline histone claim. Exact reproduction would require author contact; the presence/absence claims are probably recoverable from the deposit. Score 3 reflects that the gaps are real but fixable.

## Weaknesses
- Load-bearing.
- 1. **The post-search analysis pipeline is not executable from the text.** The results (Fig. 3, Supp. Tables 1–3) depend on a chain — NSAF log2 transform, per-sample mean scaling, correlation-slope normalization (citing Aguilan et al., a guide, not code), missForest imputation, then floor imputation for "proteins consistently absent in a sample type" — for which no script is deposited and the two imputation thresholds are undefined: what counts as "consistently absent" (2/3 replicates? 3/3?), and does "values below the least expressed protein" mean within a sample or across the dataset? The ≥2-peptide/≥2-replicate filters would recover the presence/absence lists, but Fig. 3A and Supp. Fig. 2D cannot be reproduced exactly without guessing. ProLuCID and DTASelect2 versions are also not stated. Remedy: deposit the R script with `sessionInfo()` at the MassIVE record or a versioned repository.
- 2. **The size-exclusion direction for the proteomics input is ambiguous, and it bears directly on the histone claim.** Methods state cytosolic fractions were passed through "30 kDa MWCO columns… to separate the content by size (all other data)" but never state which side of the membrane was analyzed. This matters specifically for the headline finding: monomeric histones are 10–23 kDa and would not appear in a >30 kDa retentate, yet they were identified and interpreted as nucleosome complexes. If the retentate was analyzed, say so explicitly; if the flow-through was, the nucleosome interpretation needs revision. This must be stated to reproduce the experiment.
- 3. **The MassIVE accession is inconsistent and unverifiable from the text.** The data statement cites "PXD069795" but the URL embeds "MSV000099561"; the two may be the same dataset (MassIVE↔ProteomeXchange), but as written a replicator searching by accession will find a different identifier. I cannot resolve the link from the text, so I cannot confirm the deposit's contents or completeness — lower confidence on all results that depend on it.
- Sweep.
- - No R version or heatmaply version is given anywhere (only missForest is versioned), so even with the script, the PCA/heatmap environment is not pinned. — SOFT
- - The rSLO dose range for Fig. 2A is not specified in Methods (only "high concentrations"), so the negative control cannot be re-dosed. — SOFT
- - The ΔSLO and ΔM1 strains are "previously described" with no deposit or availability statement; a replicator must obtain them from the original lab — that is methods_completeness's call on traceability, flagged here in one line. — attributed
- - The reduced IL-1β response to ΔM1 cytosol (Fig. 4A) could in principle reflect generally weaker permeabilization in ΔM1-infected cells rather than specific loss of M1; whether that confound is excluded is data_analysis's verdict, noted here only because the comparison's wording ("primarily responsible") outruns what I can assess from the text. — attributed
