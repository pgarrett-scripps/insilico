# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
The manuscript covers many required identifiers well but has several gaps. For the cross-cutting category, sample sizes, statistical tests, error bars, and software versions are clearly stated. The data-availability statement (MassIVE PXD069795) is present and verifiable. Code availability is SOFT and noted as not required (standard tools). The antibodies category is well-documented for vendor/catalog/clone/Host but critically lacks the working dilution for every antibody (HARD missing). Cell lines lack STR authentication (HARD missing) and mycoplasma testing (HARD missing); RRID is SOFT missing. Chemicals/drugs are well-documented with vendors and concentrations, although a few details (LPS concentration, exact rSLO doses) are ambiguous but present in figures. Mass spec parameters are comprehensively described with instrument, search engine, database, FDR, modifications, and tolerances, and the repository accession is provided. Protocol provenance is strong: all delegated references are resolvable, published papers. Overall, two HARD gaps—antibody dilutions and cell-line authentication/mycoplasma—would prevent a competent lab from exactly reproducing the immunodetection and cell-culture conditions.
