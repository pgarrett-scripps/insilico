# Reproducibility Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript presents a well-designed experimental study of stress-response evolution in Listeria monocytogenes, with solid molecular and genomic work. However, critical load-bearing results—particularly the genome-wide start codon analysis and the claim that altered start codons regulate virulence/stress genes—rest on data and code that are not currently available for independent verification. The authors promise deposition 'upon publication' and provide no accession numbers, repository links, or supplementary files. For a reproducibility review, this is a hard failure on the primary contribution.

## Strengths
- The in vitro evolution experiment is well-controlled with three independent replicates, clear sampling strategy, and appropriate phenotypic validation (acid survival, motility, gene expression).
- Whole-genome sequencing of the parental strain and six ARDs is deposited (PRJNA1305691), enabling verification of the rsbW mutations and their characterization.
- Translational reporter assays (Figures 4B–C) use appropriate controls and quantify the effect of start codon changes on protein expression with both fluorescence and Western blot confirmation.

## Weaknesses
- **Start codon analysis dataset and code unavailable.** The core contribution—analysis of start codon usage across 60,690 L. monocytogenes genomes—is not reproducible as presented. The authors state they created 'a local blast DNA database for each L. monocytogenes genome sequence' (line 546) and performed custom tblastn searches with specific parameters ('–seg' option enabled, line 547), but provide no code, no deposited database, no intermediate outputs, and no accession numbers for the genome set used. The exact date of NCBI download (24 May 2024, line 546) is noted but the resulting dataset is not archived. Without the processed start codon calls for all 60,690 genomes, the claim that 39 genes show flexible usage (1.8% of 2,180 conserved genes) and that 13 are virulence/stress-related cannot be independently verified. The alternative explanation—that the authors' tblastn parameters or gene-calling thresholds (e.g., the >95% presence threshold for 'conserved' genes, line 554) systematically identified or missed certain genes—cannot be ruled out. This is load-bearing: the entire population-level argument rests on this analysis.
- **Phylogenetic analysis of ST1 strains lacks reproducibility details.** The authors inferred phylogeny of 5,546 ST1 genomes using Parsnp2 (line 488) but do not specify the version, alignment parameters, or the exact genome set used (is it the same 60,690 or a subset?). The SNPs associated with branching points (line 492) are examined qualitatively but no tree file, SNP matrix, or reproducible pipeline is provided. Figure S1 and the evolutionary trajectory argument depend on this; without the tree and SNP calls, the claim that GadR R154Q 'occurred much earlier' (line 357) cannot be verified.
- **Transcriptional data (qPCR) not deposited.** Gene expression measurements (Figures 2C–G) are presented as fold-changes relative to MQ140025 but raw Ct values, standard deviations, and replicate-level data are not provided in supplementary tables or a data repository. The authors state 'three independent experiments were carried out and each with technical duplicates' (line 857) but do not deposit the underlying qPCR data, making it impossible to verify the statistical tests or reproduce the fold-change calculations.
- **Fluorescence and Western blot quantification data not provided.** Figures 4B–C and 5C show quantified results but the underlying pixel intensities, background corrections, and individual replicate measurements are not deposited; the authors note 'three independent experiments were performed' (line 515) but do not provide the raw image files or quantification spreadsheets.
- **Strain and plasmid sequences: MQ140025 genome deposited but ARD genomes not.** The complete genome of MQ140025 is available (PRJNA1305691) but the authors sequenced ARD1, 3, 5, 6, 10, and 12 with Illumina reads mapped to MQ140025 (line 485). These reads and the resulting variant calls are not deposited; the authors state 'Purified DNA samples were sent to Novogene' (line 481) but provide no SRA accession or supplementary VCF files, making it impossible to verify the rsbW mutations or check for additional variants.
- **Plasmid sequences pLM7UG1 and pLMGC1 not deposited.** The authors identify two plasmids in MQ140025 (lines 206–209) and compare pLMGC1 to Bacillus and Streptococcus plasmids, but do not deposit the MQ140025 plasmid sequences to GenBank or provide them as supplementary FASTA files; only the chromosome is in PRJNA1305691.
- **Reporter plasmid construction details incomplete.** The authors state that 'purified recombinant plasmids were electroporated into electrocompetent cells' (line 509) but do not provide the sequences of the three pIMK3-rsbW-eGFP constructs (ATG, ATA, ATT variants) or deposit them in a plasmid repository; verification of the intended mutations and absence of unintended changes is not possible.
- **Phylogenetic tree and SNP alignment not provided.** Figure S1 shows a tree of ST1 strains but the Newick file, alignment, or Parsnp2 output is not deposited; the branching points and SNP associations discussed in the Discussion (lines 357–365) cannot be independently inspected.

## Questions
- Where are the processed start codon calls (first three nucleotides) for all 60,690 genomes and the corresponding gene presence/absence matrix deposited, and under what accession?
- What is the exact version of Parsnp2 used, and are the core-genome alignment and SNP matrix available?
- Are the raw qPCR Ct values, Western blot images, and fluorescence plate reader data (with background corrections) available in a supplementary file or repository?
- Are the Illumina sequencing reads and variant calls (VCF) for ARD1, 3, 5, 6, 10, and 12 deposited in the SRA, and if not, why?
- Are the sequences of the three pIMK3-rsbW-eGFP constructs (ATG, ATA, ATT) and the pIMK3-plcB and pIMK3-lftR constructs available in a plasmid repository or supplementary file?
