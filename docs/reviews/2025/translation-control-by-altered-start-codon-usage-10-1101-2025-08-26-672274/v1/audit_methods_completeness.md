# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript describes an in vitro evolution study of Listeria monocytogenes under acid stress, followed by genomic analysis and translational reporter assays. Detected categories include: Model organisms (bacterial strains), Chemicals/drugs (acid pH conditions), Genomics/sequencing (Nanopore and Illumina sequencing, bioinformatic analysis), Antibodies/immunodetection (Western blot), Oligos/plasmids/constructs (translational reporters), and Computational/ML (phylogenetic and start-codon usage analysis). The manuscript provides substantial methodological detail but has gaps in critical areas: strain sources lack RRIDs or authentication; sequencing metadata (read length, depth, coverage) are absent; bioinformatic tool versions are incomplete; statistical tests and error-bar definitions are not consistently stated; and several delegated protocols cannot be verified from citations alone. Data availability is not explicitly addressed. These gaps impede reproducibility of key analyses.

## HARD gaps — reproduction blockers
- **[Model organisms] Bacterial strain source and authentication** — Strain MQ140025 is stated to have been 'isolated from an ear swab in 2014' and previously reported in Wu et al., 2022 [21], but no RRID, strain collection number, or authentication method (e.g., MLST confirmation, genomic fingerprinting) is provided. Reference strain F2365 is cited but not sourced. The manuscript states 'Strain MQ140025 presents as a sigB- strain likely because RsbU functions downstream of RsbX' but does not confirm this phenotype was verified before the study began.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Nanopore sequencing: platform, library-prep kit, read length, depth/coverage** — Manuscript states 'A library was prepared using rapid barcoding kit V14 (Oxford Nanopore)' and 'loaded onto a PromethION 2 Integrated (P2i) sequencer (Oxford Nanopore)' with 'MinKNOW (high-accuracy option)' (Materials and Methods, lines 453-456). However, no read-length statistics (N50, mean length, max length), total bases sequenced, or coverage depth are provided. The resulting genome size is stated as 2,902,539 bp for chromosome and 50,099 bp and 11,340 bp for plasmids, but coverage is not reported.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Illumina sequencing for ARD isolates: platform, library-prep, read length, depth/coverage** — Manuscript states 'Purified DNA samples were sent to Novogene and the resulting pair-end sequencing reads were mapped to the MQ140025 genome (Breseq)' (Materials and Methods, lines 481-482). No information on read length, insert size, total coverage depth, or quality metrics is provided. The Illumina platform (HiSeq, NovaSeq, etc.) is not specified.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Alignment and analysis tools with versions and key parameters** — Bioinformatic tools used: Autocycler (combining canu, flye, metamdbg, unicycler) for assembly (line 449); Prokka for annotation (line 450); BRIG and geneviewer for comparison (line 451); Mauve for chromosome comparison (line 472); tblastn for sequence comparison (line 468); Breseq for variant calling (line 482); Parsnp2 for phylogenetic inference (line 489). Versions are NOT provided for any of these tools. Key parameters (e.g., assembly parameters, BLAST e-value threshold, Parsnp2 settings) are not stated. This is a critical gap for reproducibility.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Start codon usage analysis: dataset version, exact methodology, tool versions** — Manuscript states 'A local blast DNA database was created for each L. monocytogenes genome sequence that was deposited at NCBI database (n=60,690; access date: 24th May 2024)' (Materials and Methods, line 545). The NCBI database version/snapshot is identified by access date only. The tblastn search parameters (e-value, word size, etc.) are not specified. The mlst tool for MLST assignment is cited but not versioned (line 550). The definition of 'conserved' genes (>95% presence, >57,655 genomes) is stated, but the exact filtering and counting methodology is not fully detailed.
- **[Antibodies/immunodetection] Western blot: protein loading control and quantification method** — Manuscript states 'For each sample, 10 ng protein was separated in SDS-PAGE (12% acrylamide-bisacrylamide)' (line 522) and protein concentration was determined using 'BCA assay (Pierce™ BCA Protein Assay Kits, ThermoFisher)' (line 521). However, no loading control (e.g., β-actin, GAPDH) is mentioned. The quantification method for Western blot bands is not described; it is unclear whether densitometry was used or if the blot is qualitative.
- **[Oligos/plasmids/constructs] Plasmid source and construction details for pIMK3 reporters** — Manuscript states 'codon-optimized eGFP sequence [35] was amplified by PCR using primers that were designed to include the regulatory regions under investigation (Table 2). These fragments along with pIMK3 plasmid were digested (NcoI and BamHI, FastDigest, Thermofisher) and ligated then transformed to E. coli TOP10' (Materials and Methods, lines 507-510). The pIMK3 plasmid is cited as previously described [34] but no Addgene number or full plasmid map/sequence is provided. The exact insertion sites (NcoI/BamHI coordinates in pIMK3) are not specified. The selection marker (kanamycin resistance is mentioned in culture conditions, line 441, but not explicitly tied to pIMK3 selection).
- **[Oligos/plasmids/constructs] Validation of translational reporter constructs** — Manuscript does not state whether the constructed plasmids were verified by sequencing, restriction digest, or other validation. The text states 'purified recombinant plasmids were electroporated into electrocompetent cells of L. monocytogenes' (line 511) but does not confirm that the correct inserts were present before transformation.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Phylogenetic analysis: dataset, tool, parameters, random seed** — Manuscript states 'The phylogeny of these ST1 genomes were inferred using Parsnp2 based on the core-genome SNP profile' (Materials and Methods, line 489). The dataset is '5546 ST1 genomes' (line 487). However, Parsnp2 version is not provided, and key parameters (e.g., alignment parameters, tree-building algorithm, bootstrap replicates if applicable, random seed) are not stated. The SNPs examined at branching points are mentioned but not fully detailed.
- **[Computational/ML/modeling] Start codon usage analysis: exact filtering, statistical tests, multiple-testing correction** — Manuscript defines 'conserved' genes as present in >95% of genomes and 'flexible SC usage' as SC different in >5% of sequences (Materials and Methods, lines 551-552). However, no statistical test is applied to determine significance of SC variation. No multiple-testing correction is mentioned despite analyzing 2180 genes. The threshold of 5% for 'flexible' usage appears arbitrary and is not justified.
- **[Cross-cutting] Statistical test and error-bar definition** — Figure 2 legend states 'Statistical significance was calculated between each strain to MQ140025 using paired two-tailed t-test (ns, not significant; *, P < 0.05; **, P < 0.01; and ***, P < 0.001)' (line 858). However, error bars in Figures 1, 2, 4, and 5 are not explicitly defined as SD, SEM, or CI. The choice of paired t-test is stated but not justified (why paired?). Other figures (e.g., Figure 1C-G) do not state what statistical test was used or what error bars represent.
- **[Cross-cutting] Software and tool versions** — Multiple bioinformatic tools are mentioned without versions: Autocycler, canu, flye, metamdbg, unicycler, Prokka, BRIG, geneviewer, Mauve, tblastn, Breseq, Parsnp2, mlst. The qPCR instrument software and analysis software are not named. The microplate reader software (Synergy H1) is not specified. This is a critical gap for reproducibility.

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Protocol provenance] Acid survival assay protocol** — Manuscript states 'Protocols for acid challenge experiments were adapted from a previous study [28]' (Materials and Methods, line 459). Reference [28] is Wu et al., 2023, cited as 'A novel RofA-family transcriptional regulator, GadR, controls the development of acid resistance in Listeria monocytogenes. mBio. 2023;14: e01716-23.' The manuscript does not specify which aspects were adapted or what deviations exist from the original protocol. The original reference cannot be verified from the manuscript alone.
- **[Protocol provenance] Transcriptional analysis (RNA extraction, RT-qPCR)** — Manuscript states 'Methods for transcriptional analysis were adapted from Wu et al., (2023)' (Materials and Methods, line 516). The specific protocol details (RNA extraction kit, qPCR conditions, primer efficiency) are partially provided (Qiagen RNeasy kit, TURBO DNase, 16S reference gene) but the original reference [28] is not fully resolvable from the manuscript. The statement 'reverse transcription and qPCR analyses were carried out as previously described [28]' (line 519) delegates critical details to a citation.
- **[Protocol provenance] Electroporation and plasmid transformation into L. monocytogenes** — Manuscript states 'electrocompetent cells of L. monocytogenes then plated on BHIkan agar plates and incubated at 30°C to select transformants, as previously described [34]' (Materials and Methods, lines 511-512). Reference [34] is Monk et al., 2008, a standard methods paper. The manuscript does not provide electroporation parameters (voltage, capacitance, time constant) or detailed competent-cell preparation. The protocol is delegated to [34] without stating deviations.

## SOFT gaps — recommended
- **[Protocol provenance] Fluorescence quantification assay conditions** — Manuscript states 'Reporter strains were grown in BHIkan at 37°C for 18 h with or without 1 mM IPTG, then 1 mL culture was washed twice using 700 µL PBS with chloramphenicol (5 µg/mL)' (Materials and Methods, line 527). The microplate reader is identified as 'Synergy H1, Agilent' (line 531) and fluorescence wavelengths as '489nm/518nm' (line 530). However, the gain setting, temperature during measurement (stated as 20°C, line 530), and plate type are not fully specified. The calculation '500 × (RFU/OD)' is stated but the rationale for the 500× factor is not explained.
- **[Protocol provenance] Immunoblot detection and quantification** — Manuscript states 'the membranes were incubated with chemiluminescence detection reagents (Amersham) for 5 min in the dark and visualized in a LI-COR C-DiGit chemiluminescence channel with "high sensitivity"' (Materials and Methods, lines 527-528). The exposure time, image processing, and densitometry method are not described. No statement on whether blots were quantified by densitometry or are presented qualitatively.
- **[Cross-cutting] Data availability statement** — The manuscript states 'The complete genome sequence of strain MQ140025 was deposited at NCBI database (Project accession: PRJNA1305691)' (line 457) but does not provide a comprehensive data-availability statement. Raw sequencing reads (Nanopore and Illumina) are not explicitly stated to be available. Supplementary tables (S1, S2) are referenced but not provided in the manuscript text. No statement on whether other data (e.g., raw fluorescence values, qPCR Ct values, phylogenetic trees) are available.
- **[Cross-cutting] Code availability for bioinformatic analysis** — The manuscript does not state whether custom scripts or analysis code (e.g., for start-codon usage analysis, phylogenetic tree construction, or data filtering) are available. The tools used (Parsnp2, tblastn, mlst) are standard but the specific commands, parameters, and any custom filtering scripts are not provided or made available.

## Documented (for the record)
- **[Model organisms] Strain genotype and background specification** — Strain MQ140025 is characterized as CC1, ST1, with lesions rsbU Q317* and rsbX N77K (stated in Introduction, line 107-108). F2365 is identified as CC1, ST1 reference strain. ARD isolates are described by their mutations (e.g., ARD1: rsbV G111C, rsbW M1M; ARD3: rsbW R23*; ARD5: rsbW Y37*; ARD6/10/12: rsbV G111S, rsbW M1M). However, the complete genotype of the parental strain before acid selection is not fully characterized until after sequencing.
- **[Chemicals/drugs/dosing] Acid stress conditions: pH, concentration, vehicle, duration** — Acid challenge experiments use 'acidified BHI (pH 2.5 or pH 3)' incubated at 37°C statically (Materials and Methods, line 459-460). In vitro adaptation protocol: 100 µL overnight culture mixed with 900 µL acidified BHI, incubated at 37°C statically at indicated time points (line 461-462). Survival experiments: 20 µL sample serially diluted in phosphate saline buffer (line 464). However, the method of acidification (HCl? other acid?) is not specified.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Reference genome version and build** — Reference strain F2365 is used for comparison (Figure 3A, line 206). The manuscript states 'the complete chromosome sequence of MQ140025 was compared to CC1 reference strain F2365' but does not cite a specific genome assembly version or NCBI accession for F2365. The MQ140025 genome is stated to be deposited at NCBI (Project accession: PRJNA1305691, line 457), but the reference F2365 accession is not explicitly given, though it is a well-known reference strain.
- **[Genomics/sequencing/omics] Repository accession for sequencing data** — MQ140025 complete genome sequence is stated to be 'deposited at NCBI database (Project accession: PRJNA1305691)' (line 457). However, the manuscript does not provide accessions for the raw Nanopore or Illumina reads, nor for the ARD isolate genome sequences. Only the project accession is given; individual assembly and read accessions are not listed.
- **[Antibodies/immunodetection] Western blot: primary antibody vendor, catalog #, clone, RRID, dilution, host species** — Primary antibody: 'rabbit anti-GFP, polyclonal, Invitrogen' diluted '4000× in TBST with 5% milk' (Materials and Methods, lines 524-525). Vendor and host species are provided; however, no catalog number or RRID is given. The antibody is polyclonal (not a clone), which is noted.
- **[Antibodies/immunodetection] Western blot: secondary antibody vendor, catalog #, dilution, host species** — Secondary antibody: 'mouse anti-rabbit, Santa Cruz, 5000× diluted in TBST' (Materials and Methods, line 526). Vendor and dilution are provided, but no catalog number or RRID is given.
- **[Oligos/plasmids/constructs] Primer sequences for translational reporters** — Table 2 provides primer sequences for rsbW ATG, ATA, ATT constructs; plcB ATG, GTG constructs; lftR ATG, GTG constructs; and eGFP amplification. Sequences are fully listed in the table (lines 827-828 and supplementary table). However, the table formatting is corrupted/unclear in the provided text, making some sequences difficult to parse with certainty.
- **[Cross-cutting] Sample size (n) and replication structure** — Acid survival experiments: 'All experiments were carried out three independent times, each with technical triplicates' (line 467). Transcriptional analysis: 'Three independent experiments were carried out and each with technical duplicates' (Figure 2 legend, line 858). Fluorescence quantification: 'Three independent experiments were performed' (line 532). Western blot: 'Protein samples were collected from three independent experiments' (line 520). However, the manuscript does not consistently distinguish between biological replicates (independent cultures) and technical replicates (repeated measurements of the same sample) in all figures and results sections.
