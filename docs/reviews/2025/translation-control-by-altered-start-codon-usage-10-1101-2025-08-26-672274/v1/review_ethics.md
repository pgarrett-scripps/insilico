# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
This manuscript describes a well-designed experimental and computational study of translational control via start codon usage in Listeria monocytogenes, with particular focus on stress response and virulence regulation. The work is sound, the claims are well-supported by evidence, and the ethical compliance profile is clean. The study involves bacterial strains only (no human subjects, animals, or sensitive dual-use work), funding is disclosed, and no conflicts of interest are declared. Minor improvements in data availability and acknowledgement transparency would strengthen the submission.

## Strengths
- The authors disclose funding sources (Science Foundation Ireland, Irish Department of Agriculture) and explicitly declare no conflicts of interest, meeting core transparency requirements.
- Whole-genome sequencing data and plasmid sequences are deposited at NCBI (Project accession PRJNA1305691), enabling reproducibility and verification of the central genomic claims.
- The experimental design includes appropriate controls (reference strains F2365 and EGD-e), replication (three independent experiments with technical replicates), and clear methodology sufficient for independent verification.

## Weaknesses
- The manuscript does not explicitly state that the work involves only non-pathogenic laboratory manipulation of L. monocytogenes or address biosafety containment level, despite working with a food-borne pathogen; while the work appears to be standard microbiology, a one-sentence biosafety statement would clarify compliance with institutional biosafety committee requirements.
- Supplementary data (Tables S1, S2 and Figures S1, S2) are referenced but their availability is not explicitly stated; the authors should confirm whether these are deposited with the preprint or will be made available upon request.
- The acknowledgements do not explicitly identify non-author contributions (e.g., Dr. Kate Reddington's role in Nanopore sequencing is thanked but not formally attributed as a contribution), which is a soft transparency issue under current standards.

## Questions
- Does the institution have a biosafety committee approval or statement confirming that this work meets the appropriate biosafety level for L. monocytogenes laboratory culture and genetic manipulation?
- Are the supplementary tables and figures (S1, S2) deposited with the preprint or available through a linked repository, or will they be provided upon request?
- Were any AI tools or language models used in manuscript preparation, analysis, or figure generation that should be disclosed per emerging authorship guidelines?
