# Editorial Desk Screen

**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This manuscript presents original empirical research on translational control mechanisms in Listeria monocytogenes, combining laboratory evolution experiments, whole-genome sequencing, and systematic comparative genomics analysis. The work is technically sound, clearly within scope for In Silico (original research with inspectable evidence and deposited materials), and makes a coherent contribution to understanding stress response regulation through start codon usage. While the presentation contains numerous typographical errors (likely OCR artifacts from the submission format), these do not obscure the scientific content or prevent evaluation of the claims. The experimental design is appropriate, the data are presented with proper controls, and the genome-wide analysis is well-motivated. The manuscript warrants full peer review.
