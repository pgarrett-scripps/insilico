# Journal Recommendations

## As-is (current quality)
- **bioRxiv**
  - Fit: This manuscript is a large-scale proteomics resource paper with extensive validation and open data. bioRxiv is the appropriate preprint home for work of this scope and rigor before or during peer review at a formal venue, and the paper's detailed methods and supplemental tables are well-suited to preprint dissemination.
  - Realism: bioRxiv accepts all research manuscripts without peer review; posting here is immediate and appropriate for a paper under major revision at a formal venue.

## After required revisions
- **Molecular & Cellular Proteomics**
  - Fit: This is a core proteomics methods and discovery paper with deep validation of peptide identification, quantification of protein proteoforms across tissues and cancers, and mechanistic insights into protein stability. MCP is the flagship venue for proteomics-focused work of this scale and rigor, and the paper's six-pronged validation strategy and resource contribution align directly with the journal's scope.
  - Realism: After addressing the multiple-comparison corrections, softening causal claims about stability, and providing effect sizes and confidence intervals, this paper has realistic prospects at MCP. The panel's major-revision verdict and the strength of the underlying validation support acceptance conditional on executing the required revisions.
- **Nature Methods**
  - Fit: The paper presents a systematic methodology for identifying and validating amino acid substitutions from alternate translation using mass spectrometry and transcriptomics. The six-pronged validation pipeline, cross-dataset reproducibility, and open code/data are methodological contributions that fit Nature Methods' scope for techniques enabling new biological discovery.
  - Realism: Nature Methods is highly selective and typically requires either a novel method or a landmark resource. This paper qualifies as the latter, but the major-revision verdict and the need to soften mechanistic claims mean acceptance is uncertain even after revision. Realistic only if the corrected statistics and effect sizes substantially strengthen the tissue/cancer-specificity findings.
- **Molecular Systems Biology**
  - Fit: This journal publishes systems-level proteomics and genomics work integrating multiple data types (MS, RNA-seq, degradation rates, structural features, population genetics). The paper's analysis of RAAS across tissues, cancers, protein domains, and species, combined with machine-learning prediction of substitution ratios, fits the integrative systems scope.
  - Realism: MSB accepts papers of this quality and scope regularly. After revision to correct multiple comparisons, add effect sizes, and soften causal framing, this paper has solid prospects. The major-revision verdict and the strength of the validation pipeline support realistic acceptance odds.

## Alternative outlets
- **Journal of Proteome Research**
  - Fit: JPR publishes proteomics discovery and methods papers with strong validation. This manuscript's detailed peptide-identification validation, quantification across large sample cohorts, and open data/code fit the journal's scope for rigorous proteomics work.
  - Realism: JPR is more specialized and less selective than the headline venues, making it a reasonable fallback if Molecular & Cellular Proteomics or Nature Methods decline. Acceptance odds are moderate-to-good after revision, given the panel's major-verdict and the soundness of the underlying validation.
- **eLife**
  - Fit: eLife publishes high-quality research across biology with emphasis on rigorous methods, reproducibility, and open science. This paper's extensive validation, open data, and cross-species conservation fit eLife's values, and the major-revision verdict is consistent with eLife's typical acceptance bar.
  - Realism: eLife has a broad scope and values methodological rigor and reproducibility, both strengths of this work. However, eLife is selective and the paper's mechanistic claims (stability driving abundance) require softening per the editor's decision. Realistic as a secondary target after the headline proteomics venues.
- **Genome Biology**
  - Fit: This journal publishes integrative genomics and proteomics work. The paper's use of RNA-seq, MS proteomics, gnomAD population genetics, and machine-learning prediction of RAAS across tissues and cancers fits the journal's scope for systems-level genomic discovery.
  - Realism: Genome Biology is selective but broader than specialized proteomics journals. After revision, the paper's resource contribution and cross-dataset validation support realistic prospects, though the major-revision verdict means acceptance is not assured.
