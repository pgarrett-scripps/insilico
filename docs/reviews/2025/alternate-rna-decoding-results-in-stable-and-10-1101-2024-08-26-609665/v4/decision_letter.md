# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel converges on a substantial, technically careful proteomics resource. The six-pronged peptide-identity validation (ionization efficiency, detectability, multi-digest reproducibility, shared-peptide triangulation, retention-time prediction, degradation rates) was praised independently by five reviewers and survived adversarial scrutiny in debate; the skeptical reviewer conceded it. Ethics is a clean pass. The confidence-weighted score (3.56/5, modal verdict 'major', no rejections) matches my own reading, and I do not depart from it.

Two issues carry the decision. First, the multiple-comparison problem in the Fig. 4b,c and Fig. 5c RAAS dotplots. The authors concede this and commit to BH correction within analysis families with disclosed family sizes. This is the load-bearing item: the Abstract's 'strong tissue-type and cancer specificity' claim depends on which comparisons survive correction, and the outcome cannot be anticipated in advance. I therefore require the corrected re-analysis and a claim strength that tracks its result. I separately credit the authors' disagreement on the tissue-enrichment analysis (Extended Data Fig. 5h,i): the quoted Methods passage does show BH correction was applied, so that reviewer point was a misreading, and only family-size disclosure is required there.

Second, the causal framing of 'stability contributes to substitution abundance.' Three reviewers independently flagged that detection/selection bias offers a competing explanation, and the authors' response is well judged: they concede the upstream detectability filter is not escaped by the shared-peptide design, while making the defensible argument that, conditional on detection, the shared-peptide test was a genuine prediction that could have failed. I accept that the analysis is informative but not causally decisive, and require the softened language and explicit alternatives paragraph they propose.

The authors' defence of non-genetic origin (patient-matched customProDB databases incorporating each sample's own SNVs/indels, not gnomAD alone) is credible and I do not require further work beyond documenting the LSCC genomic check. Their framing of the 55-site human-mouse overlap as an explicitly acknowledged lower bound is also fairly represented in the manuscript text quoted; I ask only that the Abstract-level phrasing match that caution.

The compliance audits raise one HARD methods gap (library-prep kit identity) and a set of soft/unverifiable items, chiefly missing PRIDE/MassIVE accessions for the MS data, absent XGBoost hyperparameters and seeds, and three references lacking DOIs/PMIDs. None of these threaten the central claims, but the repository accessions and ML hyperparameters are cheap to supply and materially affect whether the work can be rebuilt.

The manuscript's concessions are extensive, specific and, if executed as described, sufficient. The one item whose outcome is genuinely open is the corrected dotplot statistics, which is why this is a major rather than minor revision.

## Required Revisions
1. Re-run all RAAS dotplot comparisons (Fig. 4b,c, Fig. 5c, Extended Data Fig. 5) with Benjamini-Hochberg correction applied within explicitly defined analysis families. Report the family size (number of tests) for each family in the Methods, update all significance cutoffs and figure legends to corrected q-values, and state in the Results which previously significant comparisons no longer survive correction.
2. Scale the tissue-type and cancer-specificity claims to whatever survives step 1. If a substantial fraction of comparisons lose significance, revise the Abstract wording ('strong tissue-type and cancer specificity') and the corresponding Results/Discussion statements to descriptive or exploratory framing, and say so explicitly rather than leaving the original phrasing intact.
3. Remove causal language about protein stability driving high RAAS from the Abstract, Results heading and Discussion, replacing it with association language, and add a Discussion paragraph enumerating the detection/selection-bias and reverse-causality alternatives. State explicitly that the shared-peptide analysis (Fig. 3e,f) is conditional on SAAP detection and does not escape upstream detectability filtering, and name the perturbation experiments (e.g. synonymous codon engineering, targeted mutagenesis) that would be needed to establish causation.
4. Disclose the family size for the tissue-enrichment FDR correction (Extended Data Fig. 5h,i) in Methods — the correction itself is documented in the manuscript and is not in question, only its reported scope.
5. Add a supplemental table documenting the LSCC genomic comparison: variant-calling pipeline, sample size, matching procedure and result, cited at the point in Results where the claim that patient mutations did not correspond to identified AAS is made.
6. Add the table (or figure panel) reporting the number of proteins with median RAAS>1, >0.5 and >0.1, computed separately for the full 8,801-site set and the 2,000 high-confidence subset, and clarify in the text how the 360-protein figure is derived and how sensitive it is to the confidence threshold.
7. Report effect sizes alongside p-values as proposed: eta-squared with 95% CI for each ANOVA factor (Fig. 4f, Fig. 5m), Spearman rho with 95% CI and codon counts per RAAS quartile (Fig. 5k), and n (SAAP/proteins) for each degradation-rate correlation (Fig. 3d, Extended Data Fig. 3m,n).
8. Report the full distribution (median, IQR, 95% CI) of test-set Pearson correlations across the 1000 XGBoost retrainings, state explicitly that hyperparameter tuning used training-fold cross-validation only, and add a null baseline (global median RAAS) for comparison with r=0.44.
9. Specify the XGBoost hyperparameters actually used (learning rate, max depth, n_estimators, subsampling, or an explicit statement that R defaults were used) and state whether random seeds were set for the 1000 retrainings and 100 leave-one-factor-out runs; deposit seeds or the seed-setting code in the GitHub repository.
10. Provide a resolvable public repository accession for the MS data used, or state clearly for each dataset which existing accession it corresponds to (PXD010154, PXD030983 are given; CPTAC PDC study identifiers should be listed individually rather than only as a portal URL). Do the same for the CPTAC RNA-seq data.
11. State the RNA-seq library-preparation kit (vendor and catalogue number) for data generated for this study, or state explicitly that all RNA-seq data are reanalysed public datasets and cite the source publication where the library prep is described.
12. Add the Discussion paragraph comparing the stability-based mechanism to the stress/starvation-induced substitution mechanism of Pataskar et al. (2022) and Yang et al., stating specifically which of the two the degradation-rate data can and cannot distinguish.
13. Add DOIs or PMIDs for References 1 (Cantwell-Dorris et al. 2011), 3 (Wright & Vissel 2012), 17 (Karijolich & Yu 2011) and 38 (McCormick et al. 2024 preprint). For Reference 17, clarify in the text whether the cited work addresses sense-codon recoding or only stop-codon readthrough, since the Discussion argument depends on which.

## Minor Suggestions
- Add the Introduction paragraph stating the two central hypotheses, and the one-sentence formal definition of 'alternate translation', as proposed in the rebuttal.
- Consider matching the Abstract's cross-species wording to the appropriately hedged Results text: the 55-site overlap is framed in Results as a detectability-limited lower bound, and the Abstract should not read more strongly than that.
- Cite the Wisniewski et al. justification for the 30x10^6 histone copies/cell value and include the proposed +/-50% sensitivity analysis in Extended Data.
- State the replication structure for both the CPTAC TMT and label-free datasets (technical/biological replicates per sample, whether replicates were averaged), even if this is inherited from the source studies.
- Add an environment specification (renv.lock, requirements.txt, or Dockerfile) to the GitHub repository, and record library versions for the Python components where these are currently unstated.
- Specify the exact gnomAD query/filtering criteria used, or deposit the queried variant subset, so the constraint analysis can be reproduced against a fixed snapshot.
- Name the MS instrument models for the reanalysed datasets, or state that these vary by source study and point to the relevant table.
