# Methods Completeness & Reagent Traceability Auditor

## Summary
This manuscript reports cryo-EM structures of AL amyloid fibrils extracted from patient cardiac tissue, with mass spectrometry sequencing, histology, and computational analysis. Detected categories include human subjects/clinical (patient tissue with informed consent), antibodies/immunodetection (western blot), mass spectrometry (proteomics and de novo sequencing), microscopy/imaging (cryo-EM, TEM, histology), and computational/modeling (APR prediction, sequence alignment, solvation energy calculations). Most methods are described with adequate detail or resolvable citations. Key gaps include: MassIVE accession number withheld pending review, some cryo-EM processing parameters not fully specified, and several protocol-provenance items that are unverifiable from the manuscript alone (e.g., the exact contents of cited fibril extraction and histology protocols). The data-availability statement is present but incomplete (MassIVE ID pending). Software versions are generally provided for major tools (RELION 4.0, Casanovo v4.2.0, Phenix v1.20.1, ChimeraX v1.7.1) but some are missing (e.g., MSConvert version, CTFFIND version stated as 4.1 but full version string absent). Statistical testing is not applicable to this structural biology work, but sample sizes (n=1 patient, 1.4M particles, ~40k particles per final map) are stated. The manuscript is transparent about limitations of cryo-EM bias and polymorphism detection.

- **[Computational/modeling] Random seed specification or seed-averaging statement** — No random seeds reported for RELION 3D classification, particle picking (Topaz), or any stochastic computational steps.
