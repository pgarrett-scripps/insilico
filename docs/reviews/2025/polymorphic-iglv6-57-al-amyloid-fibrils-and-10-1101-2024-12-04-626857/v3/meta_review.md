# Meta-Review

**Draft recommendation:** major

## Synthesis
The panel agrees the cryo-EM structural core is sound and genuinely useful: a 3.0 Å ex-vivo IGLV6-57 fibril structure plus a rarer double-protofilament morphology, both properly deposited (PDB 9ELS/9ELT, EMDB EMD-48160/48161) with gold-standard FSC validation (clarity 4/5, data_analysis 4/4, novelty 4/4). Ethics and consent practices are adequate modulo minor transparency gaps (IRB number, de-identification statement). Where the panel splits is on everything built on top of that structural core. Methodology (3/4), reproducibility (3/4) and rigor (3/4) converge on two distinct but reinforcing problems. First, the paper's central mechanistic claim — a 'shared folding pathway' — is elevated to the title and given its own figure (Fig. 6b) despite the authors' own text conceding 'our data are not sufficient to support or eliminate these models'; the rigor and methodology reviewers independently show the underlying evidence (a shared C-shaped turn and disulfide reversal across three related sequences) is equally consistent with convergent sampling of a conformational landscape, and no quantitative structural comparison (RMSD/TM-score) is even provided to establish that the three structures are as similar as claimed. Second, the reproducibility reviewer identifies that the de novo MS sequence — the single input that enabled all atomic modeling — rests on raw spectra listed as 'available to reviewers upon request' rather than deposited, with the authors themselves flagging three ambiguous residues that 'may also be asparagines.' The debate sharpened rather than dissolved these points: the advocate's claim of 'triangulated' sequence validation (MS + proteinase K protection + intact mass) was directly and, in this synthesis's judgment, correctly rebutted by the skeptic — proteinase K protection and intact-mass sizing confirm boundaries and approximate mass, not residue-level identity, so they cannot substitute for accessible raw spectra on the load-bearing sequence claim. The advocate's defense of the folding-pathway hedging also does not fully survive: hedged language in the Discussion does not neutralize a headline-level claim structurally promoted via the title and a dedicated figure. Both critiques are explicitly revision-fixable (deposit the MS accession publicly; retitle/reframe Fig. 6 as exploratory; add quantitative structural alignment metrics; report solvation-energy sensitivity and APR thresholds per data_analysis and clarity reviewers) rather than fatal flaws in the underlying data.

## Decisive Factors
The confidence-weighted score (3.65/5) and the 5-minor/3-major split sit near the minor/major boundary; I resolve it toward major rather than minor because the debate specifically validated, rather than deflated, the two heaviest critiques (reproducibility's MS-availability gap on the load-bearing sequence, and rigor/methodology's overclaiming of the folding-pathway hypothesis at the title/figure level), while the advocate's counterarguments on these two points were shown to be incomplete rather than dispositive. This is not harsher than the panel's own range: three specialists (methodology, reproducibility, rigor), each at confidence 4, already recommended major revision, and their concerns are substantive analytical gaps (missing quantitative structural comparison, undeposited raw MS data, unresolved solvation-energy/stability contradiction) rather than stylistic quibbles. No reviewer recommended rejection, and nothing in the record suggests the structural core itself is unsound, so reject is not warranted. The path to acceptance is concrete and bounded: public MS deposition, quantitative structural metrics for the three-structure comparison, and reframing the folding-pathway claim (including the title) as an explicitly stated hypothesis rather than a headline finding.
