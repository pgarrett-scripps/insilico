# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel converged on a clear picture: the structural core of this work — a 3.0 Å ex-vivo IGLV6-57 cardiac AL fibril structure plus a rarer 3.78 Å double-protofilament morphology, both deposited (PDB 9ELS/9ELT, EMDB EMD-48160/48161) with gold-standard FSC validation — is sound, well documented and a genuine contribution. Ethics, literature coverage, clarity and novelty all scored 4/5. The disagreement concerned what is built on top of that core, and the debate sharpened rather than dissolved two critiques. First, the de novo MS sequence that anchors every atomic model rests on raw spectra listed as 'available to reviewers upon request'; the advocate's 'triangulation' defence (proteinase K protection plus intact mass) was correctly rebutted, since neither establishes residue-level identity. Second, the 'shared folding pathway' hypothesis is elevated to the title and given a dedicated figure while the Discussion concedes 'our data are not sufficient to support or eliminate these models'.

The rebuttal is unusually strong and materially shaped this decision. The authors conceded both load-bearing points outright and committed to specific, checkable fixes: public MassIVE/ProteomeXchange deposition with the accession replacing the placeholder, retitling to remove 'shared folding pathway', relabelling Fig. 6b as an untested hypothesis, and adding an explicit statement that convergent sampling of a common conformational landscape is an equally plausible alternative. They also conceded the missing quantitative structural comparison (RMSD/TM-score), the solvation-energy sign-convention tension, the absent APR threshold, the IRB/de-identification gaps and the missing docking cross-correlation. Three of their four disagreements are credible and supported by manuscript quotations: the particle-tracing rebuttal (real-space (x,y) coordinates mapped onto raw micrographs, not 2D-projection inference) is a fair correction of the novelty reviewer; the double-protofilament mechanism framing is already explicitly hedged in the quoted Discussion text; and the two symmetry claims are indeed textually sequential. I have not converted these into required revisions. Their partial defence of the MS sequence — that density fit and MolProbity statistics independently cross-validate ordered-core residues — is reasonable as far as it goes, but it does not cover the constant-domain ambiguities (D134/D175/D176) or unordered regions, so deposition remains required rather than optional.

Why major and not minor, given the 3.65/5 weighted score and 5-minor/3-major split: the two load-bearing items touch the paper's verifiability and its headline claim, and one of them (title and figure reframing) changes the manuscript's central mechanistic identity. That is more than a minor revision, even though the path is bounded and entirely within the authors' stated plans. Why not reject: no reviewer recommended it, nothing challenges the integrity of the structural data, and every concern has a concrete remedy the authors have already agreed to. The methods audit's two HARD gaps (IRB protocol number; unreported random seeds for stochastic processing steps) are additive requirements, not obstacles to evaluating the central claims. I expect this to be acceptable after one revision round.

## Required Revisions
1. Deposit the complete raw MS/MS dataset (.raw and converted .mgf files for the trypsin, chymotrypsin, elastase and proteinase-K digests) to MassIVE/ProteomeXchange with public release, and replace 'available to reviewers upon request' in the Data Availability statement with the live accession number. This is the single blocking item: the de novo sequence is the input to every atomic model and must be independently checkable. Also state in Methods the ambiguity at D134/D175/D176 and whether it was resolved or left open in the deposited data.
2. Reframe the folding-pathway claim as an explicit hypothesis, as committed in the rebuttal: (a) remove 'shared folding pathway' from the title; (b) relabel Fig. 6b and its legend as a proposed, untested folding hypothesis; (c) add a sentence in the Discussion stating that convergent sampling of a common conformational landscape is an equally plausible alternative not excluded by the data. The revised title and figure must not assert a mechanism the Results cannot support.
3. Add quantitative structural comparison metrics for the three-structure comparison: pairwise Cα RMSD after core-region superposition (with the superposed residue ranges stated) and TM-scores where feasible, between 9ELS and each of 6HUD and 9OKA. Report these in Figure 5 and the corresponding Results text, replacing the current visual-similarity argument.
4. Resolve the solvation-energy contradiction in the text accompanying Supplementary Fig. 6: define the sign convention explicitly, state that the metric captures only the burial contribution to stability, and state directly that the compensatory electrostatic/hydrophobic/interlocking interactions are proposed (not demonstrated) to offset the unfavourable burial term.
5. Pre-specify the APR threshold in Methods (consensus score ≥7/10 methods, per the rebuttal), apply it uniformly in the Results text and Figure 4, and label any sub-threshold regions discussed as exploratory.
6. Add the IRB protocol number and approving committee to Methods, together with a statement that patient data were de-identified under institutional HIPAA-compliant procedures (methods audit HARD gap). State inclusion criteria for the tissue donor, even if n=1.
7. Report a quantitative score for the rigid-body docking of the single-protofilament model into the double-protofilament map (cross-correlation coefficient and/or clash score) in place of 'visually reasonable', and add one sentence noting how the 3.78 Å resolution limits confidence in the Asn72/Ser73 interface assignment.
8. State whether random seeds were fixed or results checked for seed-independence for the stochastic processing steps (Topaz picking, RELION 3D classification), or explicitly note that seeds were not recorded and what this implies for exact reproduction (methods audit HARD gap). A brief statement suffices; re-running is not required.

## Minor Suggestions
- Add the spectral-count and fold-change supplementary table for the proteinase K enrichment argument (Fig. 1f), as proposed in the rebuttal.
- Cite Morgan et al. 2025 (Amyloid 32:129-138) alongside refs 11-13 in the Introduction discussion of germline gene-organ tropism, rather than only in the Acknowledgements.
- Add the clarifying sentence separating the 'first cardiac amyloid fibril with 2-fold rotational symmetry' claim from the Atlas-wide 9/615 rarity statistic, and include the Amyloid Atlas access date and query parameters so the count is reproducible. The panel's 'conflation' reading is not required by the current text, but the added sentence removes the ambiguity at no cost.
- Provide the MSConvert version and the full CTFFIND version string (e.g. 4.1.13) alongside the already-reported command lines.
- Give the size of the hand-picked Topaz training set and the Topaz model version, since the manuscript already candidly notes that picking bias may not capture the full morphology ensemble.
- Confirm the publication status of the Hicks et al. 2025 bioRxiv preprint (DOI 10.1101/2025.06.25.661559) at revision, and update if it has since appeared in a journal.
- Ensure Supplementary Table 1 (cryo-EM collection/refinement statistics) and Supplementary Fig. 3 (reconstruction scheme, FSC curves) are included in the deposited preprint package; several load-bearing parameters are delegated to them and reviewers could not inspect them.
