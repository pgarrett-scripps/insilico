# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
Overall, from a clarity and presentation standpoint, this manuscript is well-structured and written. The narrative flow from motivation through proteomics discovery to mechanistic characterization of Git1 is logical and easy to follow. The figures are generally well-designed and the captions are informative. The main weaknesses are a few instances of ambiguous quantitative phrasing in key comparative claims, and some figure-referencing gaps that would slow a careful reader. These are fixable with modest revision; the argument is not fundamentally obscure.

## Weaknesses
- Several comparative quantitative claims lack explicit comparators. In the Results section under 'Loss of Git1 diminishes Hh signaling,' the phrase 'substantial increase' is used for Gli1 transcription in WT cells and 'markedly lower' for Git1-null cells; the reader cannot determine the magnitude of the effect from the text alone (the figure reports it, but the text should too). Similarly, on the same page, 'significantly higher compared to WT cells' for Gli3R level states direction but not magnitude — '1.7-fold higher' (or whatever the value is) lets the reader assess it inline.
- Figure 2 panel C is referenced in the text as showing volcano plots for 'different time points after Shh activation,' which is correct, but panel B is described as showing proteins before Shh treatment. However, the legend for Figure 2 does not explicitly label which sub-panels in B and C correspond to which time points (the caption says 'Volcano plots showing protein fold change before Shh treatment biotin (B), and at different time points after Shh activation (C).' — the language 'at different time points' is vague; a reader cannot tell which of the four panels in C corresponds to which time point without guessing or cross-checking the axis labels in each panel.
- The abstract's claim that 'Loss of Git1 impairs Smo phosphorylation by Grk2, a critical step for Smo-PKA interaction, leading to attenuated Hh signaling and reduced cell proliferation in granule neuron precursors' is a HARD presentation issue: it combines the molecular mechanism (Smo phosphorylation) with the phenotypic consequence (reduced proliferation) without making clear which part is directly shown in GNPs and which is inferred from NIH3T3 data. A reader cannot determine from the abstract alone whether proliferation was measured in GNPs under Git1 knockdown (it was) or whether Smo phosphorylation was also verified in GNPs (it was not; only Gli1 transcript and EdU were reported there). The abstract should scope claims to match the experimental system in which each was tested.
- In Figure 4D and 4F, the text refers to 'phosphorylated Smo (pSmo)' staining. The antibody is described in the Methods as 'Rabbit anti-pSmo (7TM antibodies, 7TM0239A-IC)'. The text says it 'specifically recognizes the Grk2 phosphorylation sites on Smo.' A reader cannot determine from the text what specific residue(s) this antibody detects — is it S6 in the C-terminal tail? Multiple serines? The citation to Walker et al. 2024 is given, but not all readers will have immediate access to that paper's validation. A brief statement of the validated phosphosite(s) would resolve this.
- Several figure panels have abbreviations in legends that are not defined in the caption or the text at first use. In Figure 2D, the heatmap labels include abbreviations such as 'PKA-Ca' and 'Gnai2'; these are standard but are not expanded in the caption. In Figure S4, 'Indel' is used without definition. While most of these are standard in the field, the presentation guideline is to define all non-standard abbreviations at first use.
- In the Methods section under 'Streptavidin purification,' the washing protocol lists 'RIPA twice, 1 M KCl once, 0.1 M Na2CO3 once, 2 M Urea (in 10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0) once, RIPA twice and DPBS once.' The order of operations is recoverable but a reader cannot determine how long each wash was incubated or whether each was a simple resuspension-and-pellet step versus a longer incubation with rotation. This matters because different washing stringencies can affect which biotinylated proteins are retained.
- The phrase 'Smo-TurboID' is sometimes written as 'Smo-V5-TurboID,' sometimes as 'Smo-TurboID,' and once as 'Smo-V5-TuboID' (typo in Figure 1 legend). While these are clearly the same construct, the inconsistency is a SOFT clarity issue — a careful reader may pause to wonder whether the V5 tag makes a difference. Pick one name and use it consistently.
- Figure 7F, the schematic model, uses arrows and T-bars that are described in the caption but the directionality of 'promotes' versus 'inhibits' is not color-coded or labeled on the diagram itself. For example, the arrow from Git1 to Grk2 says 'facilitates Grk2 translocation' in the text but the arrow is unlabeled on the figure. The legend says 'Git1 at the ciliary base facilitates Grk2's interaction with Smo; it also promotes Grk2 translocation into the cilium' but does not specify which arrow represents which step.
