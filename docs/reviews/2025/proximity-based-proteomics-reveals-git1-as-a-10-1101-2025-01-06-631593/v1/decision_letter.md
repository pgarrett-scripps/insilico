# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
This is a genuinely novel and well-executed study: a time-resolved Smo-TurboID proteomic resource plus the identification of Git1 as a regulator of Grk2-dependent Smo phosphorylation in the primary cilium. The novelty reviewer found no prior Git1–Hedgehog work, and the literature reviewer confirmed fair attribution of the Smo–PKA–Grk2 lineage. The core causal chain is tested at multiple independent points (loss of ciliary pSmo with normal Smo accumulation, reduced ciliary PKA-C, Gli1/Gli3/Gli2 readouts, and a Grk2-ciliary rescue), which is a genuine strength and anchors the decision.

However, the panel's numerical signal (2.85/5; 5 major, 2 reject, 1 minor) reflects three load-bearing claims that, as worded, outrun the evidence, plus substantial reproducibility and compliance gaps. (1) The central Grk2-translocation claim rests on a ~1.2-fold increase in lentivirally overexpressed Grk2-V5 at the limit of detection, with no stated threshold and no positive control demonstrating the imaging system can resolve such a signal from noise — an orthogonal readout is required, not optional. (2) The "primary site" conclusion is not established by the rescue, which compares Grk2-V5-DArl13b against a V5-DArl13b backbone rather than against non-ciliary Grk2-V5; as designed it shows Grk2 expression rescues Gli1, not that ciliary localization is the operative variable. (3) The GNP proliferation claim is not shown to be Hh-mediated, given Git1's known adhesion/migration roles and the absence of an Hh-specific readout or rescue in GNPs. These are fixable with named controls and scaled-back language rather than fatal, which is why I depart from the two reject votes: the converging multi-assay evidence supports a provisional causal model, and every substantive gap has a concrete, feasible remedy.

The reproducibility and compliance audits add mandatory items: no mass-spec repository accession, no analysis code, no shRNA/gRNA/primer sequences, no Data Availability statement, no IACUC protocol number for the P7-mouse GNP work, and no competing-interests declaration. The statistics reviewer correctly flags the absence of FDR for the proteomic screen, unreported ANOVA interaction terms, pseudoreplication in cell-level quantifications, and an unpowered "no difference" claim for cilium length. These do not invalidate the conclusions but must be addressed before the work can be independently verified and built upon.

The abstract and several Discussion statements also overreach across experimental systems (e.g., "leading to reduced cell proliferation in granule neuron precursors" implies a causal chain not tested in GNPs; "essential for Hh signaling" overstates a reduction to 15–30% of control; "Git1 interacts with both Smo and Grk2 at the cilium base" implies an endogenous ternary interaction not demonstrated). These are wording and scoping fixes. Overall this is sound, useful, incremental science that belongs in the record after a substantive major revision.

## Required Revisions
1. Provide an orthogonal readout for the Grk2-ciliary-translocation claim (Fig. 5C–E). The current evidence is a ~1.2-fold increase in lentivirally overexpressed Grk2-V5 at the limit of detection, with no stated threshold and no positive control showing the imaging system can resolve such a signal from noise. Add either (a) biochemical enrichment of Grk2 from cilia-enriched fractions, (b) a Grk2-V5 titration demonstrating the assay's dynamic range, or (c) an endogenous Grk2 ciliary measurement, and state the formal detection threshold (e.g., >2 SD above a no-primary/untransduced control). Without this, the claim is indistinguishable from 'ciliary Grk2 could not be measured under these conditions.'
2. Add the missing rescue control that isolates ciliary localization as the operative variable. The current rescue (Fig. 6G–H) compares Grk2-V5-DArl13b against a V5-DArl13b backbone. Add an arm expressing Grk2-V5 without the DArl13b ciliary tag (and ideally a non-ciliary membrane-anchored Grk2) at matched expression, and report whether it also restores Gli1 in Git1-null cells. This control is required before the 'primary site of Smo phosphorylation is the cilium' conclusion can stand as worded.
3. Resolve the GNP proliferation claim. Either add an Hh-specific readout or a rescue linking the reduced EdU incorporation to the Smo/Grk2 pathway in GNPs, or explicitly scope the claim to what the GNP data establish (Git1 knockdown reduces Gli1 and EdU incorporation) without asserting that the proliferation defect is Hh-mediated. Note that Smo phosphorylation was not measured in GNPs.
4. Scale the abstract and Discussion claims to the systems in which each was tested. (a) Fix the abstract sentence 'Loss of Git1 impairs Smo phosphorylation by Grk2... leading to attenuated Hh signaling and reduced cell proliferation in granule neuron precursors' — the Smo-phosphorylation and Hh-signaling data are in NIH3T3 cells, while GNPs were tested only for Gli1 and EdU. (b) Replace 'Git1 is essential for Hh signaling' with 'important for / required for full activation' (Gli1 is reduced to 15–30% of control, not abolished). (c) Temper 'Git1 interacts with both Smo and Grk2 at the cilium base' — the Smo–Git1 proximity is shown only by TurboID biotinylation of overexpressed YFP-Git1 and the Git1–Grk2 interaction only by Co-IP of co-overexpressed proteins in 293T; neither demonstrates an endogenous ternary interaction at the cilium base. (d) Soften the claim that basal-body Grk2 'is less likely contributing' to Smo phosphorylation, which is asserted from absence-of-effect rather than a direct test.
5. Strengthen the statistical reporting. (a) Report an FDR/q-value or permutation-based threshold for the proteomic screen (the >1.5 TMT ratio and p<0.05 eBayes cutoff is arbitrary without a false-discovery assessment), and annotate the volcano plots with the multiple-testing correction used. (b) Report the ANOVA interaction terms for the two-way ANOVAs in Figs 4E/G, 5D–E, 6A/F, and state the number of pairwise comparisons and correction applied. (c) Address pseudoreplication: cell-level quantifications pooled across three experiments have an effective n of 3 experiments, not the cell count — analyze at the experiment level or use a mixed model. (d) For the cilium-length 'no difference' claim (Fig. S4E), add a power analysis or equivalence test (e.g., TOST), since a non-significant result in an unpowered sample does not support a claim of no effect. (e) State the qPCR reference gene(s), their stability validation, and the quantification method (delta-Ct vs standard curve); report the interaction term for the Gli1 comparisons.
6. Deposit the data and reagents required for independent verification. (a) Deposit the raw mass-spectrometry data (PRIDE/MassIVE or equivalent) with an accession number, and upload the custom R code for TMM/scaling normalization and eBayes analysis to a version-controlled repository. (b) Provide the full gRNA sequence used for Git1 knockout, the shRNA target sequences (Git1 shRNA #1/#2 and scrambled control), and the qPCR primer sequences for Gli1 and Git1. (c) Provide plasmid maps or accession numbers for Smo-V5-TurboID, YFP-Git1, and Grk2-V5-DArl13b. (d) Add a Data Availability statement covering all of the above.
7. Add the required compliance statements. (a) State the IACUC (or equivalent) approval and protocol number for the use of P7 C57BL/6J mice to harvest GNPs, and confirm that the PKA-null MEF work is covered by the same or a named approval. (b) Add a competing-interests statement declaring the presence or absence of competing interests for all authors.
8. Complete the methods and reagent traceability. (a) Name the mass-spectrometer model and acquisition mode, and give versions for R, FIJI/ImageJ, ProLuCID, DTASelect2, Census2, and RawConverter. (b) Provide RRIDs (and clone identifiers where applicable) for all antibodies, and state application (WB/IF/IP) and dilution for each. (c) Provide vendor/catalog or CAS identifiers for SAG, recombinant ShhN, and biotin. (d) State the validated phosphosite(s) recognized by the pSmo antibody (7TM0239A-IC), or cite the specific validation. (e) Report animal sex, number of animals per condition, and any randomization/blinding for the GNP work. (f) State whether cell lines were STR-authenticated and mycoplasma-tested.
9. Address the 'data not shown' claim that the Smo–Git1 interaction is not Co-IP-detectable, which is the stated rationale for relying on TurboID proximity labeling. Either show the Co-IP data or rephrase the rationale so it does not rest on an unverifiable negative result.
10. Clarify the proteomic resource and its relationship to prior work. (a) State explicitly which known Smo interactors were recovered and which were missed, to substantiate the 'majority of known Smo-associated proteins' claim. (b) Compare the dataset explicitly to the closest prior time-resolved ciliary Hedgehog proteomics (May et al. 2021, JCB) and Mick et al. 2015, clarifying that the novelty is the Smo-tagged TurboID and time-resolved design rather than proximity labeling per se. (c) Report the overlap of candidates across the three Shh time points (15 min, 1 h, 4 h) rather than pooling them into a single post-Shh set.
11. Minor presentation fixes: (a) correct the 'Smo-V5-TuboID' typo in the Fig. 1 legend and use one consistent construct name; (b) label the Fig. 2 volcano sub-panels by time point and define abbreviations in the Fig. 2D heatmap; (c) label the arrows in the Fig. 7F schematic; (d) state the wash incubation times in the streptavidin-purification protocol; (e) fix the embedded-space/OCR artifacts in the DOI list; (f) note the preprint status of Steiner et al. 2024 (ref 18) or update the citation if it has since been published.

## Minor Suggestions
- Add a positive control to the cyclopamine time course (Fig. 3C–D) confirming that Smo remains at the cilium across the 24 h interval, so the flat PKA-C signal cannot be attributed to loss of drug activity or toxicity.
- State the number of independent Git1-null clones used in each key experiment (Figs 4D–E, 5C–E, 6A–F) — the text says two clones were selected but does not specify whether both were tested in each figure.
- For the Git1 biotinylation experiment (Fig. 4B), quantify the western blot with loading-control normalization across replicates and a statistical test before claiming a Shh-dependent increase.
- Report individual data points or a consistent n for all quantified western-blot conditions (Figs 6B–D, S3B, S4C).
- Specify how cilia were identified for intensity quantification (automated threshold vs manual selection) and the pixel size/binning used, to reduce friction for replicators.
- Clarify the selection pressure and clone-isolation protocol for the Smo-TurboID Flp-In screen so a replicator can repeat it.
