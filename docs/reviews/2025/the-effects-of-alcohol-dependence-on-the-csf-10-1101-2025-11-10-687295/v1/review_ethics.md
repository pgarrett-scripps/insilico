# Ethics & Compliance Reviewer

## Summary
The manuscript is an animal study (mice) with no human subjects, no clinical trial, no dual-use or biosafety concerns, and no restricted-consent data. The animal research approval statement is present and adequate (IACUC #09-0006, ARRIVE compliance, NIH Guide). Funding is disclosed. The only gap is the absence of an explicit competing-interests statement, which is a soft issue. The paper is otherwise compliant and well-reported on the ethics dimensions I can assess.

## Strengths
- The animal research approval is explicitly stated with the IACUC protocol number (#09-0006), ARRIVE compliance, and NIH Guide reference.
- Funding is fully disclosed with specific grant numbers.
- The manuscript is candid about its limitations, including the small sample size and the IL-6R antibody administration, which supports honest reporting.

## Weaknesses
- The manuscript does not include an explicit competing-interests statement; the authors should declare whether any exist or state that none exist.
- The manuscript does not state whether the anti-IL-6R antibody administration or the CSF collection procedure caused any animal distress beyond what is described, though the IACUC approval covers this.

## Questions
- Do the authors have any competing interests to declare, financial or otherwise?
- Was any additional animal welfare monitoring performed during the CSF collection procedure beyond standard IACUC-approved protocols?
