# Decision Letter

**Decision:** major

## Summary of Evaluation
The panel's numerical signal (3.03/5, verdict distribution 1 accept / 2 minor / 2 major / 3 reject) and the debate converge on a clear picture: this is an honest, genuinely novel hypothesis-generating pilot whose descriptive core (different CSF protein detection patterns between dependent and non-dependent mice in the CIE-2BC model) survives scrutiny, but whose interpretive scaffolding — 'dichotomous/qualitative transition,' 'BBB breakdown,' 'loss of immune surveillance' — outruns a detection-based, n=4–5 design. I am not persuaded by the skeptic's strongest claim that the BBB interpretation is fatally confounded beyond repair: the collection-artifact alternative is real and must be addressed with a measured blood-marker ratio, but that is an achievable, specific revision rather than a redesign. However, I do side with the skeptic and the methodology/rigor reviewers on two points that cannot be resolved by wording alone: (1) the dose/route/dependence confound means the central 'transition to dependence' question is not testable with this design and the causal language must be scaled to 'dependence-associated'; and (2) the immunoglobulin 'immune surveillance' interpretation is entangled with the injected anti-IL-6R antibody detected in every sample and must be either removed or explicitly requalified. The reproducibility breaks (private GitHub, non-standard MassIVE accession) are HARD and must be fixed at submission. The citation audit raised a load-bearing potential misattribution (ref 32, Gaither, for the BIP/consumption-grade claim) that the authors must resolve. The panel's blind spot — no threshold-sensitivity analysis and no quantitative comparison of the 404 shared proteins — is a genuine omission that the authors should close. Given the venue's bar (claims must be supported by the evidence presented) and the number of load-bearing claims that currently outrun the evidence, major revision is appropriate: the descriptive resource is worth publishing, but it needs new data (contamination metric, threshold sensitivity, quantitative shared-protein comparison) and substantial requalification before it can be accepted.

## Required Revisions
1. Resolve the BBB-disruption vs. collection-artifact question with a measured metric. Report per-sample hemoglobin and/or albumin concentration (CSF vs. serum ratio) and show whether it correlates with the Dep-specific protein detection pattern. If a blood-sparing collection method (e.g., Lourbopoulos 2025) is used, state this explicitly. Without this, the 'BBB breakdown' claim cannot stand as written.
2. Scale the causal and interpretive language to the evidence. Replace 'dichotomous/qualitative transition,' 'transition to alcohol dependence,' and 'BBB breakdown/dysfunction' with 'dependence-associated' and 'preliminary evidence consistent with' throughout the abstract, title, and discussion. State explicitly that the design cannot separate dependence from cumulative ethanol dose, route (vapor + i.p. injection vs. oral), and withdrawal stress, and that the dichotomy extends (rather than establishes) the authors' own companion transcriptomic work (Hashimoto 2025).
3. Resolve the immunoglobulin/immune-surveillance interpretation. Because the injected rat anti-IL-6R antibody (IGG2B, DILLISQNAK) was detected in every CSF sample, the KV5AB/LV1C/HVM02/03 detection patterns cannot be read as a clean measure of endogenous adaptive immunity. Either remove the 'loss of immune surveillance' claim or explicitly requalify it as entangled with the administered antibody and BBB permeability state.
4. Make all load-bearing artifacts accessible at submission. Make the GitHub repository public with a commit hash/tag (or deposit an archived copy, e.g., Zenodo DOI), and confirm the MassIVE accession is live, in the correct format (MSV0000xxxxx), and released at submission rather than 'upon publication.'
5. Add a sensitivity analysis for the detection threshold. Report the 140-vs-67 protein counts at ≥1 and ≥3 replicates and show that the biological interpretation survives threshold variation. Define the 'top 10' selection criterion for Figure 2.
6. Add a quantitative comparison of the 404 shared proteins (DIA provides quantitative peak areas). This is the analysis that could most strengthen or weaken every claim and serves as a partial control for the presence/absence framework.
7. Resolve the load-bearing citation questions. Confirm that ref 32 (Gaither 2025) actually reports BIP/GRP78 correlating with alcohol consumption grade in human liver biopsies (the cited title concerns drug-metabolizing enzymes/transporters) or correct the citation; and confirm that ProteomeXchange dataset PXD053568 (Lourbopoulos 2025) contains the Hb and keratin identifications claimed.
8. Add the missing methods details: animal age (weeks/months), a randomization/blinding statement, and a statement on MS precursor/fragment tolerances (or confirmation that DIA-NN handles these internally).
9. Report the sex composition of each group explicitly (the current sentence is ambiguous) and state whether any of the 140 Dep-specific proteins are driven predominantly by one sex; note the n=2-per-sex limitation in the Dep group.
10. Report total protein per CSF sample (or otherwise normalize detection for the 4-fold CSF volume range, 5–20 µL) to distinguish biological signal from sample-load technical variation.

## Minor Suggestions
- State explicitly that the study extends a dichotomy already established in the same CIE-2BC model at the astrocyte transcriptome level (Hashimoto 2025), rather than presenting the dichotomy as this paper's own discovery.
- Name a nearest-neighbor CSF proteomics study and state the delta over it, given that the individual markers (GFAP, MMP2, CHI3L1, UCHL1) are established biomarkers in other contexts.
- Flag the single-peptide identification of the IL-6R antibody (DILLISQNAK) as a limitation in the main text, not merely in the supplement.
- Add an RRID for the mouse strain and consider reporting confidence intervals for detection rates rather than (or in addition to) the post-hoc power analysis, which is circular.
- Consider whether the anti-IL-6R antibody (150 kDa, present in CSF) itself alters BBB permeability, and integrate this into the BBB interpretation rather than treating it only as a methodological validation.
