# Citation Integrity Auditor

## Summary
This is a preliminary discovery proteomics study comparing CSF from alcohol-dependent (Dep) vs. alcohol-exposed non-dependent (Non-dep) mice in the CIE-2BC model, using DIA-MS. The audit covered reference resolvability, claim-citation support, quotation/number fidelity, self-citation/citation inflation, and retracted/predatory sources. The reference list is heavily truncated by PDF conversion: refs 10-15 are entirely absent and refs 16-31 appear as bare numerals, so several in-text citation ranges (notably "9-14" for the CIE-2BC procedure) cannot be resolved from the provided list. Most printed entries lack DOIs/PMIDs, but the entries as printed also lack them (a parse gap, not a manuscript defect). Verified existing: ref 31 (Prystupa 2015, PMID 26094532), ref 58 (Lourbopoulos, DOI 10.1101/2024.06.18.599559), and ref 68 (Hashimoto, DOI 10.1101/2025.09.12.675849). No retracted or predatory sources were identified. The most consequential open item is F5: the claim attributed to ref 32 (Gaither) that increased BIP levels correlate with alcohol consumption grade in human liver biopsy samples could not be reconciled with the cited paper's apparent scope (drug-metabolizing enzymes and transporters in human liver) and is load-bearing for the BIP/BBB-disruption narrative; this is raised as a question/potential misattribution. Other unverifiable items (F1, F2, F4, F6, F8, F9, F10) are raised as questions to authors rather than asserted to be wrong, per the conservative standard. Self-citations (refs 68-74) are germane methodological/model citations rather than padding. No score or accept/reject judgment is rendered.

## Unverifiable (raise as questions)
- **[Reference resolvability] In-text citation range '9-14' (CIE-2BC procedure)** — Only ref 9 (Lopez & Becker 2014) is present in the typed list; refs 10-14 are absent from the parse. Per the conversion caveat, absence from the typed list is not evidence of non-existence. Question to authors: confirm refs 10-14 exist and are listed in the full bibliography.
- **[Reference resolvability] Full entries for refs 16-31** — Refs 16-31 appear as bare numerals with no content in the typed list; a parse gap, so these entries' contents are unavailable for checking. Question to authors: confirm the full entries for refs 16-31.
- **[Claim-citation support] Ref 31 (Prystupa 2015): MMP2 elevated in serum of alcoholic liver disease patients, correlates with cirrhosis development** — Reference verified to exist (PMID 26094532; 'Activity of MMP-2, MMP-8 and MMP-9 in serum as a marker of progression of alcoholic liver disease'). The title supports the 'marker of progression' component; the specific cirrhosis-correlation detail could not be confirmed from the abstract.
- **[Claim-citation support] Ref 32 (Gaither 2025): increased BIP levels positively correlated with alcohol consumption grade and reflected degree of alcohol-induced damage in human liver biopsy samples** — The cited paper's title concerns 'the Composition of the Ensemble of Drug-Metabolizing Enzymes and Transporters in the Human Liver' — BIP/GRP78 correlation with alcohol consumption grade in liver biopsy is not obviously within its scope. Could not confirm this claim is contained in ref 32. Potential misattribution; raised as a question. If load-bearing for the BIP/BBB-disruption argument, authors should verify the correct source.
- **[Claim-citation support] Ref 58 (Lourbopoulos 2025): undetectable Hb by ELISA yet Hb and keratins identified by proteomics via earlier DIA-NN pipeline, data at PXD053568** — Reference verified to exist as a preprint (DOI 10.1101/2024.06.18.599559). The specific claims (ELISA-undetectable Hb, keratin classes, PXD053568 dataset identifier) could not be confirmed from the abstract.
- **[Claim-citation support] Ref 68 (Hashimoto 2025): same CIE-2BC model shows strong astrocyte neuroimmune response in nucleus accumbens** — Preprint verified to exist (DOI 10.1101/2025.09.12.675849). The claim is consistent with the preprint's title/scope and is germane. Note: this is a self-citation of the authors' own group's work (see self-citation finding).
- **[Claim-citation support] Refs 1, 2: chronic alcohol disrupts tight junctions via MMP activation, oxidative stress, and purinergic signaling** — Ref 1 (Muneer 2012) is titled around MMP-mediated VEGFR-2 degradation in alcohol ingestion, plausibly supporting the MMP/vascular component; the purinergic-signaling component is more plausibly carried by ref 28 (Togre). Full text of ref 1 could not be retrieved to confirm. Question to authors on which reference supports the purinergic component.
- **[Claim-citation support] Refs 6, 7: neuroimaging/CSF studies 'confirmed' AUD is characterized by activated microglia, elevated cytokines, and complement dysregulation** — Ref 6 (Adams 2023 meta-analysis) and ref 7 (Azuar 2021) are plausibly on-topic, but contents could not be retrieved.
- **[Quotation/number fidelity] PXD053568 dataset identifier attributed to ref 58** — The dataset identifier could not be verified against the source. Question to authors to confirm the dataset identifier.
