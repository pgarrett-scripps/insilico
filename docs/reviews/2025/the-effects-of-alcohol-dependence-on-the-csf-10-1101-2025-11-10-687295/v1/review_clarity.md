# Clarity & Presentation Reviewer

## Summary
The central claim — that alcohol dependence produces a qualitatively distinct CSF proteome rather than a quantitative extension of exposure — is stated explicitly and the limitations are handled candidly, which is to the authors' credit. However, several presentation defects block a reader from actually verifying that claim: the group-specific protein counts are labeled three different ways that cannot be reconciled, the sex composition of the experimental groups is unrecoverable, and the anti-IL-6R treatment that is part of every animal's history is never motivated. The tables and figure legends contain garbled or ambiguous elements. All are fixable, but they currently force the reader to guess at the evidence structure.

## Weaknesses
- The central quantitative claim is labeled inconsistently, so the reader cannot determine what the 140/67 numbers mean. The Results say "140 proteins preferentially detected in alcohol-dependent mice and 67 proteins preferentially detected in Non-dep mice (Figure 1b)," but the Figure 1b legend calls these "Dep unique = 140; Non-dep unique = 67," and the Classification section defines "Dep-preferred" with different thresholds (≥3/4 vs ≤1/5 for strong; ≥2/4 for moderate). "Unique" (0 in the opposite group) is a stricter set than "preferred" (≤1/5 in the opposite group), yet the same numbers are attached to both labels. Table 1 then lists proteins such as BIP (4/4 vs 1/5) that are "preferred" but not "unique," so the relationship between the 140, the Venn diagram, and the tables is never stated. A reader cannot tell whether the 140 includes proteins detected in one Non-dep animal or not. One sentence stating the exact threshold that produced the 140/67 counts would settle this.
- The sex composition of the experimental groups is unrecoverable. The Animals section reads "The male and female C57BL/6J mice (n = 4 and 5; 29.2±1.63 g and 22.4±0.55 g, respectively)" — presumably 4 males and 5 females, but the reader cannot determine how many of each sex are in Non-dep (n=5) versus Dep (n=4). This matters directly for the PCA claim that "Dep mice show clustering regardless of sex, whereas control mice... segregate by sex along the PC2 axis," and for the stated need for future sex-stratified work. State the sex counts per group.
- The anti-IL-6R antibody treatment is never motivated. The abstract and Methods state that all mice received the InVivoMAb anti-mouse IL-6R antibody (200 μg/day × 7 days), and the Limitations section concedes it "might have attenuated some general IL-6 related mechanisms," but no sentence anywhere states why the antibody was given. A reader cannot evaluate whether the design is appropriate or what the treatment's presence means for interpreting the CSF proteome. One sentence in the Introduction or Methods stating the hypothesis the antibody was testing would resolve this.
- Table 1 and Table 2 headers are garbled. The final column header reads "Evidence Detection Detection Strength" — the column structure is unparseable, and the reader must infer from the data rows that the values are "Strong"/"Moderate."
