**Outcome:** Passed Desk Screen

## Rationale
This manuscript presents an original, empirical proteomics study (DIA-MS comparison of CSF in alcohol-dependent vs non-dependent mice) with claims that are scoped to the evidence (the authors explicitly flag the small n, power limits, and preliminary nature throughout). The data, code and raw files are deposited or promised in accessible repositories, so the central claims are checkable. It is in scope for a general original-research venue, does not involve patient-safety decisions, and the acknowledged limitations do not amount to a fundamental flaw evident on the face of the submission. It should proceed to full review rather than be desk-rejected.
